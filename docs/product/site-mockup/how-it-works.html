How it works

From an empty account to an agent that pays for what it needs.

Five steps. None of them hands anyone your keys.

1 · Your account

An account only you control.

One Face ID prompt creates a smart account you own outright. No seed phrase, no credit card. Add USDC when your agents need it, and a backup passkey when you like.

2 · Budgets

A budget per agent, not a card for all of them.

An amount and a period per agent, optionally pinned to one recipient. You sign it once, it refills itself, and you can pause or revoke it at any time.

3 · Connecting an agent

One command wires any agent in.

Paste the setup prompt to Claude, ChatGPT or your own harness. The agent's key is made on its own machine, Haven never sees it, and you approve the budget with your passkey.

4 · When it pays

Seconds from paywall to result. No human in the loop.

Haven checks the price against the budget, the agent signs locally, Haven relays. Over budget does not get flagged. It reverts, and nothing moves.

Security

Built so that Haven cannot be the weak point.

Haven is non-custodial software, not a custodian or a payment processor. Even a fully compromised Haven cannot move your money.

Funds stay in your account

Haven never holds funds, or a key that could move them.

Limits are enforced on-chain

An over-budget payment reverts at execution. It is not a rule in our database.

Nothing for an agent to leak

An agent's credential cannot spend past its budget. Rotate it in one step if exposed.

An exit that needs no Haven

Inspect and revoke every budget with only your wallet and a public RPC.

Recovery you control

A backup passkey replaces a lost one. Every signer change is signed by you.
