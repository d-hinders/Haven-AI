- **#1453** — the SDK's x402 option selection becomes settlement-scheme aware, and in doing so CLOSES a stranded-funds class rather than opening anything. Before this, `selectStandardPaymentOption` returned the first `accepts[]` entry matching scheme/network/asset/amount and never read `extra.assetTransferMethod` — so a merchant that listed its erc7710-tagged entry first made a Haven client **echo the erc7710 option while signing a standard EIP-3009 authorization**. The merchant rejects that mismatch cleanly, but on the legacy two-leg the Safe→delegate funding transfer has ALREADY executed by then, so the user-visible result is a stranded delegate balance for the sweep to reclaim. Nothing but our own demo merchant's `accepts[]` ordering was holding that shut, and that pin binds our merchant, not the merchants we do not control. The standard selector now skips erc7710-tagged entries; the class is gone by construction. Authority: selection is a CLIENT-side choice of what to ask for, and it grants nothing — the backend still validates the requested scheme against the account's rail (`validateGenericSchemeRail` refuses erc7710 from a legacy AllowanceModule account) and the on-chain caveats remain the only spend control. The #1450 preference rule (prefer erc7710 when the account is on the delegation rail AND the merchant advertises it) lives in ONE function, `selectX402SettlementScheme`, so callers cannot each re-derive it differently; its rail half is supplied by the caller, and a caller that lied about its rail selects a scheme the backend then refuses — a rail mismatch fails where it should, adjacent to the chain, not in a client that could be wrong about itself. When neither scheme has a payable entry the selector returns null rather than degrading to the other scheme: "nothing payable" is the safe answer, "pay it the wrong way" is the failure this issue exists to remove. An advertised EMPTY `facilitatorAddresses` array is treated as absent rather than as a pin to nobody, matching the backend's 400 on an empty redeemer list — an empty pin is not a narrower delegation, it is an unbuildable one. Non-address entries are dropped rather than passed into a caveat. EIP-3009-only merchants (~all real x402 traffic today) select byte-identically, pinned by characterization tests written against the UNCHANGED selector before the fix and still green after. Mutation-proven three ways: removing the erc7710 skip fails 3 tests, returning `[]` for an empty facilitator list fails 1, and ignoring the rail condition fails 2. No new route, no new credential scope, no signing change, no key material moved. Perimeter unchanged; one mis-selection path removed.
