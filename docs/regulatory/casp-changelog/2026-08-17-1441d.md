- **#1441 (follow-up)** — A product's own `x402.settlementMethods` now bind the **402 challenge it serves**, not just its catalogue metadata. The two disagreed: `settlementMethodsForProduct` shaped the directory/`list_products` view, while `handlePaymentGate` built the challenge without passing the product's list at all — and the processor's wrapper wrote `{ ...params, settlementMethods }`, placing the merchant-wide set AFTER the spread so any caller value was silently discarded. A product restricting itself to eip3009 therefore advertised erc7710 in the 402 anyway, a delegation-rail client preferred it per #1450, and the funding-leg topology stayed unreachable even after `vpn_legacy` existed specifically to make it reachable. **No authority, custody, or execution boundary moves.** This is a testnet fixture's advertisement shaping: nothing changes about what may be spent, by whom, or under which caveats, and Haven's scheme selection is untouched and was correct throughout — `selectX402SettlementScheme` only ever picks erc7710 when the merchant's `accepts` contains it, so the merchant was mis-advertising and the client believed it. The composition is an **intersection, not a precedence**, and that distinction is the substance: the merchant-wide list is a HARD GATE (erc7710 absent means unconfigured, and must never be advertised however a product describes itself), while the product's list may narrow that gate but never widen it. An initial "caller wins" formulation was caught by a pre-existing test — `does not advertise erc7710 when the flag is off` began advertising it — which is precisely the security-relevant direction and the reason the gate is asserted separately from the narrowing. Recorded because an over-advertised settlement method is a misstatement to the payer about how their money will move: a client that believes erc7710 is available prepares a delegation-redemption path the merchant cannot honour, and on a real merchant the equivalent mistake would route a payer to a scheme with different custody properties than the one actually supported. No funds were at risk here — the mismatch surfaced as an unpayable quote, fail-closed, on testnet. Found by a live `qa-dev` run rather than by any test: the leg failed with `hosted quote selected "erc7710" for plan 'legacy', which advertises eip3009 ONLY`, which is the failure message #1441 added for exactly this ambiguity and which correctly named merchant advertisement as one of its two candidate causes. Mutation-proven: restoring the override makes the new 3009-only-challenge assertion fail, and a companion assertion pins that a both-methods product still receives both, so the fix cannot be read as "always narrow". Perimeter unchanged.
