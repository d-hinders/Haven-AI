- **#1508 (second and actual fix)** — `completeX402MerchantCall` now has a no-funding-leg path, so a hosted erc7710 settlement reaches the merchant instead of being refused after the fact. The helper encoded the EIP-3009 lifecycle in two gates — a readiness test wanting `confirmed`, and a mandatory Haven funding-tx hash — and #1456 reused it for erc7710 unadapted. Neither gate is satisfiable on a scheme where the MERCHANT redeems the [child, budget] chain: the intent sits at `submitted` by design (`modules/x402/settle.ts` — "final settlement is observed via the merchant/receipt path") and Haven submits no transaction at all, so every hosted erc7710 call threw `HavenPaymentStateError(status.message)` — surfacing as "The payment was submitted and is waiting for confirmation" — **after the settlement had already succeeded**, with the merchant never contacted. **No authority, custody, or execution boundary moves.** The relaxation is strictly opt-in and reaches only what funding meant: the `submitted`-is-ready branch and the tx-hash requirement fire only when the caller passes `noFundingLeg`, which exactly one call site does (the erc7710 branch of `haven_settle_mcp_tool`, where `funding_leg: false` is already asserted). The rail check and the approved-resource-URL match are **kept on both paths** — they are real safety properties about WHERE the payment may be spent, unrelated to funding, and a test pins that a `submitted` intent WITHOUT the flag is still refused, so the gate is not quietly widened for every scheme. The settlement child's bounds remain backend-computed and on-chain enforced, and the delegate key still never leaves the local signer. Evidence reporting is deliberately SKIPPED on this path (owner decision 2026-08-17): `POST /machine-payments/evidence` and the reconciliation-event route both hard-require `txHash` (`400 txHash is required`), and both SDK callers swallow failures, so an erc7710 row would vanish silently rather than fail loudly — writing a row that cannot be written is worse than not writing one. The consumer of those rows is funding-leg reconciliation (#713 — stranded delegate balances), a class this rail structurally cannot produce, and the merchant-receipt path (`/machine-payments/:id/merchant-receipt`, which requires no txHash) still runs, so the paid call remains recorded. Recording the merchant's settlement hash instead is the tracked follow-up. Correcting the record on the FIRST attempt (`2026-08-17-1508.md`): it named `ensureFundingConfirmed`'s read as the thrower. That was wrong — `getPaymentStatus` returns the body rather than throwing, provable from the error text being `status.message`, a parsed field of that body; and `GET /payments/:id` answers 200 regardless of status, since `agentPaymentStatusHttpCode`'s 409 belongs to the idempotent-replay path on POST. That change (skipping a funding wait on a scheme with no funding transaction) remains correct on its own terms and is retained.
