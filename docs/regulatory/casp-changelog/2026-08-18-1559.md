- **#1559** — Final slice of epic #1554: nonce allocation for the relayer's queue-lane submitters (sweep, hybrid deploy, passport attest/revoke, the bump worker) moves into the durable record's **stamp**, and the stamp moves **before** the broadcast. `submitRecorded` signs the transaction locally (the hash is known pre-send), stamps the `outbound_txs` row under the partial UNIQUE (chain_id, nonce) live-broadcast index, and only then broadcasts. The stamp is simultaneously the record and the **fencing token**: a slow claimant whose row was re-adopted gets a refused stamp (`OutboundFencedError`) and never broadcasts — whoever stamps, sends; a cross-replica double allocation is arbitrated by Postgres (the stale replica's stamp hits the index, re-reads the nonce, re-signs) — both proven against real Postgres and mutation-tested (removing the retry or the fence each fails its test). The crash-between-broadcast-and-stamp state recorded as residual risk on #1558 **stops existing**: the worst crash now leaves a stamped row whose tx never reached the mempool, which the bump worker's receipt-check→re-broadcast path already self-heals from the stored calldata. `withRelayerSendLock` is reduced to the in-process belt inside the pipeline and the only serialisation for the Safe-bound legacy sites (which retire with #1440 — multi-replica remains gated on THEM, and the relayer header now says so instead of calling itself "interim"). The #1546 scan is re-pointed: queue-lane files must reference `submitRecorded`; the legacy lock-only set is PINNED and can only shrink; the scan's ability to see a violation is itself tested. **No authority, custody, or execution boundary moves.** The relayer stays gas-only; what may be spent, by whom, and under which caveats is untouched; this changes how the relayer's transactions are sequenced and recorded, not what they do. Perimeter unchanged.
