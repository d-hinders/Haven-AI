- **#1673** — the counterfactual account deploy is serialised per (chain, address) with a keyed Postgres advisory lock, so two concurrent first payments from a brand-new agent cannot both pass `ensureHybridDeployed`'s bytecode check and both broadcast a factory deploy to the same CREATE2 address. **No authority, custody or execution boundary moves and nothing new is grantable** — a 4337 factory deploy is PERMISSIONLESS and grants the deployer nothing, which is why this was never a fund-safety defect: the loser of the race merely burned a second relayer gas spend and could record a spurious `record.failed`. What changes is relayer spend and the honesty of the outbound-queue record, not what may be spent or by whom. The exposure arrived with #1667, which put the check on every erc7710 authorize — before that it was reachable only from grant activation, effectively once per account and UI-serialised — and two merchants mean two idempotency keys, so the #961 replay dedupe does not apply. The lock BLOCKS rather than skipping, unlike `runIfLeader`: a caller that loses still needs an answer, so it waits and then re-reads the bytecode, returning `alreadyDeployed` instead of broadcasting a duplicate. It is bounded by `lock_timeout` and **fail-open past that bound** — the lock stops duplicated work, it does not make the work safe, so failing to acquire degrades to exactly the pre-#1673 behaviour rather than failing a payment. Proven against real Postgres (two sessions genuinely exclude each other; different subjects never wait) and mutation-proven end to end: remove the lock and two concurrent calls broadcast twice. Perimeter unchanged.
