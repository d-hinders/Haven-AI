- **#1726** — `Button size="sm"` painted a 36px-tall control and `md` 40px, both under the ~44px commonly cited as a comfortable touch target; `sm` and `md` now carry a transparent `::after` overlay that extends the **hit area** to 44px while the button still renders at its declared height. **No authority, custody or execution boundary moves, and nothing new is grantable** — this is presentation only: no route, no signing path, no policy evaluation, no balance or amount arithmetic, and no wire shape is touched. Rendered pixels are unchanged by construction, which is the point of the mechanism over raising `h-9`/`h-10`: the compact sizes are compact because 93 call sites across 25 files chose them for density, and moving them would re-rhythm every table and row list in the product to recover 8px on one card. The change reaches this doc only through `components/ApprovalQueue.tsx`, and only for spacing. That file's one edit is the Reject/Approve pair's mobile stack going `gap-2` → `gap-3` (`sm:gap-2` preserved, so desktop is pixel-identical). It is deliberate and confined to that pair: with both buttons now carrying a 44px target over a 36px paint, at 8px their overlays meet at exactly the midpoint — no overlap, but zero slack — and this is the one control pair in the product where a mis-tap rejects or approves a **real payment**, so the 4px buys margin against subpixel rounding rather than relying on two targets tessellating perfectly. Nothing about what either button *does* changes: the same handlers, the same `OnchainActionGate` on Approve, the same `disabled` predicates, the same confirmation branch for Reject. The mis-tap risk this reduces is a user-side slip, not an authority question — an approval still requires the account's own signature and is still enforced on-chain. Verified in real Chromium rather than inferred: probes 3px above and below the painted edge now hit the button and 6px misses, 3px to the left misses (the overlay grows vertically only, so a button cannot swallow taps meant for its neighbour), and the old button misses the same probes — so this is a genuine hit-area extension, not an inert overlay. Mutation-proven in both directions: shrink the overlay, raise the paint, inflate it horizontally, or drop its centring, and the guard fails; the painted heights are asserted at 36/40/44 so the change fails loudly if it ever stops being layout-neutral. Residual: the 4px/2px overhang could collide with an interactive element placed closer than ~8px in future code, which is why the ~8px minimum for stacked controls is now written into `docs/product/design-system.md` rather than left tribal. Perimeter unchanged.
