- **#1743** — the stuck-`passport_attest` lane wedge gains its **operator-triggered
  recovery** (Option A, owner decision on the issue): a same-nonce 0-value relayer
  **self-send** that burns the stuck nonce, encoded through the outbound pipeline
  (`infra/outbound-lane-cancel.ts`, `ops:cancel-stuck-attest`) instead of the
  hand-run transaction runbook §3 used to prescribe. **No custody or authority
  boundary moves.** The cancel is signed by the relayer key the perimeter already
  holds, targets the relayer's own address, carries `value: 0` and empty calldata —
  it can move relayer gas and nothing else; no user key, delegation, allowance or
  route is involved, and nothing new is grantable. The trigger surface is a CLI in
  the operator's shell (same env access the manual procedure already assumed), not
  an API: no new authenticated surface exists for an agent or merchant to reach.
- **What actually changes on the money path.** (1) `outbound-bump-worker.ts`'s
  `REBROADCAST_SAFE_SUBMITTERS` gains `lane_cancel` — the canonical idempotent
  payload (a duplicate self-send moves nothing), so a stuck cancel self-heals and a
  cancel that lost its race is bounded by the existing `MAX_BUMPS_PER_NONCE`
  incident alert. (2) `modules/passport/attestation.ts`'s `classifyAnchorTxLiveness`
  now walks a `replaced` row's link and treats only a **payload-carrying**
  replacement as `live`; a cancel-style replacement falls through to #1745's
  finalized-depth nonce evidence. This *tightens* nothing grantable and *loosens*
  nothing fail-closed: an unwalkable link still reads `live` (no evidence = no
  re-mint), and the duplicate-credential guard's arbitration is consensus itself —
  cancel and attest share nonce N, at most one mines; attest wins → unchanged hash,
  #1043 receipt recovery, no re-mint; cancel wins → burned nonce is the one
  positive death fact, exactly one re-mint via `claimForAnchoring`. Both outcomes
  and every refusal (young/slow attest, mined attest, second trigger, worker-owned
  submitter) are proven on real Postgres and mutation-proven able to fail
  (`stuck-attest-lane-wedge.test.ts`, `anchor-tx-liveness.test.ts`, tables in the
  PR).
- **Risk delta:** negative. The manual procedure's hazards (typo'd nonce, wrong
  chain, wrong wallet, no durable record) disappear; the blocked-lane outage shape
  keeps its human trigger (no timer decides an attest is dead — owner decision);
  worst case of a mistaken trigger is relayer gas plus re-mint latency, never a
  duplicate credential and never user funds.
