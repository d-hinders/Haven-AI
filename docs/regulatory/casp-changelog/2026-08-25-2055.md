# 2026-08-25 — #2055: drop approval_requests, delete the residual approvals surface

Migration 070 drops `approval_requests` and #2055's code half removes its last
surface (epic #1440; owner decision recorded on #2021: queue-history
readability for legacy accounts is waived). `routes/approvals.ts` is deleted
and deregistered — the five queue operations (list/approve/proposed/reject/
executed) answer 404 now, where approve/proposed had been unconditional 410s
since #1986 and nothing programmatic ever called the rest (#1989 removed the
only UI). Every remaining reader is re-anchored on `payment_intents` or
removed: transaction history and x402 history lose their approval-sourced
rows, the dashboard's actionable/pending counters and the agent-activity
pending counter are structurally zero (wire fields kept for compatibility),
monthly spend aggregates over intents alone, the agent payment-status and
machine-payment evidence/reconciliation lookups drop their approval-id
fallbacks, and the send-idempotency replay no longer consults the queue. The
FK hazard #2021 documented is the control this change was designed around:
`machine_payment_evidence.approval_request_id` was `ON DELETE CASCADE`, so
the migration is `DROP TABLE ... CASCADE` with an explicit no-`DELETE`-first
warning in its header — the drop severs the CONSTRAINTS while every evidence
and reconciliation ROW survives with its historical `approval_request_id`
value intact, proven by a real-Postgres migration test that recreates the
CASCADE FK and shows the evidence row outliving the drop. Authority and
custody narrow, never widen: the queue could not make anything spendable
since #1986 (approve/proposed refused fail-closed), no signing scheme, key
custody, funding, sweep, or settlement surface changes, and proof-of-payment
evidence — the CASP-relevant record — is preserved byte-for-byte. Perimeter
unchanged in kind, reduced in surface.
