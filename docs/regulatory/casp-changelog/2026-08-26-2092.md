- **#2092** — erc7710 direct settlement gained a completion seam so it produces
  the same settlement evidence every other scheme does. On this scheme the
  MERCHANT redeems the `[child, budget]` delegation chain and Haven submits
  nothing, so the intent stopped at `submitted` forever, no
  `machine_payment_evidence` row was ever written, and the payments were
  invisible to the Fortnox reporting feed, `GET /receipts`, merchant-receipt
  capture and transaction history. `POST /machine-payments/evidence` now
  completes such an intent from the settlement transaction hash the merchant
  returned in `PAYMENT-RESPONSE`, after verifying that hash **on-chain**
  (`infra/chain/settlement-transfer-verifier.ts`): mined on the intent's own
  chain, receipt status success, and an ERC-20 `Transfer` emitted by the
  intent's token contract, `from` the payer smart account, `to` the merchant
  `payTo`, for exactly the authorized atomic amount. **No authority moved and
  no fund movement was added or changed** — this path observes and records a
  settlement the caveat enforcers already authorized and the chain already
  executed; Haven signs nothing, holds no key, and cannot cause a transfer
  here. The reported hash is treated as an untrusted claim, never as
  permission: an agent that fabricates or borrows a hash cannot inject a row
  into the user's bookkeeping, because the `from`/`to`/token/amount binding
  ties the transfer to that user's own account and that merchant, and the
  guarded `UPDATE` (serialized by a per-hash `pg_advisory_xact_lock`) refuses a
  hash that already confirms another intent, so one real settlement can never
  be multiplied into several book entries. Fail-closed in both directions: an
  unreachable RPC is reported `503` (retryable) and never becomes either a
  confirmation or a permanent rejection; a revert or mismatch is `409` and
  leaves the intent `submitted`. The transition is scoped by SQL to x402 +
  `execution_rail = 'delegation'` + `settlement_scheme = 'erc7710'` +
  `status = 'submitted'` + `tx_hash IS NULL`, so eip3009 and the legacy
  AllowanceModule rail keep exactly the transitions they already had. Every one
  of those guards is mutation-proven (20 mutations, 20 kills, one equivalent
  mutant, plus a positive control that catches a seam which confirms nothing).
  A merchant returning no settlement hash still leaves the intent `submitted` —
  documented as the accepted residual gap rather than closed by inventing an
  anchor client-side. Perimeter unchanged.
