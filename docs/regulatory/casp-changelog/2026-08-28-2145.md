- **#2145** — a confirmed x402 EIP-3009 payment whose merchant leg was never
  reported no longer answers `next_action: none` — *"The payment is
  confirmed."* — and the x402 resume call gains its first reachable trigger.
  **No custody boundary, signing surface, credential, route, schema or
  migration moves.** The status projection (`GET /machine-payments/:id/status`)
  is a READ: no new spend authority is created, no payment executes without
  the same delegate-signed, on-chain-enforced flow as before.

  **The correctness half (protects money today).** The old answer was wrong on
  the money-relevant fact: value had left the treasury (funding leg confirmed,
  sitting on the delegate EOA), the merchant was never paid, and the
  agent-facing contract's first field said *done, nothing to do*. The new
  answer, `phase: funded_but_unsettled` / `next_action:
  retry_original_x402_request`, is derived **entirely from evidence Haven
  holds server-side** — a `machine_payment_evidence` row upgraded past the
  server-written `payment_confirmed` base is the "merchant responded" signal —
  deliberately not from the client-written
  `merchant_retry_rejected_after_payment` reconciliation event, whose only
  producer is an agent that survived the exact failure being recovered from.
  A 15-minute grace window (`MERCHANT_REPORT_GRACE_MIN`) after the funding
  confirmation prevents instructing a concurrent retry while a live agent is
  mid-flow. Client-reported merchant rejection still answers
  `sweep_stranded_funds` and takes precedence.

  **The capability half.** `retry_original_x402_request` now has exactly one
  producer, and it is the backend. The retained fail-closed gates
  (`assertCanResumeX402`, the hosted-MCP gate) are unchanged — they were
  waiting for a producer, and now consume it. Resume does not mint authority:
  it re-signs a fresh EIP-3009 header **locally with the delegate key** over
  funds the budget delegation already released; Haven still never signs on the
  agent's behalf. The SDK's dead `executed → retry_original_x402_request`
  fallback is resolved fail-closed (`stop_and_tell_user`), matching #2101's
  treatment of its sibling branches, so the trigger cannot be minted
  client-side from a retired status.

  **Residual risk, stated rather than glossed.** A delivered payment whose
  best-effort evidence upgrade never reached Haven reads as undelivered and is
  told to retry a merchant that was already paid. That is the safe direction —
  x402 merchants answer a re-request of a settled purchase idempotently
  (#1519), and the alternative (treating missing evidence as delivered) is the
  defect this issue removes. Scoped to `settlement_scheme: 'eip3009'`; erc7710
  (`confirmed` *is* settlement) and scheme-less intents fail closed to the
  prior answer.

  **Evidence.** Real-DB characterization tests
  (`packages/backend/src/modules/payments/__tests__/x402-funded-unsettled-status.test.ts`)
  pin the crash shape, the precedence of rejection over non-report, the
  delivered/erc7710/scheme-less/grace non-firing cases; the pre-fix wrong
  answer was pinned first in the same file's history. The agent-facing
  guidance #2131 had rewritten to "nothing emits this" is re-pointed at the
  now-true gate in the same change, so no shipped state advertises a trigger
  that does not exist.
