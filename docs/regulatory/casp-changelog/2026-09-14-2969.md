- **#2969** — the demo merchant's receipt-honesty half of the #2970 companion
  finding: the two zero-hash paths (the `MERCHANT_SKIP_SETTLE_PRODUCT` QA hook
  and `AuthorizationAlreadyUsedError` recovery) were rendered identically as
  `delivered_unsettled` even though they are different facts — one never
  attempted a settlement, the other's authorization already moved the money in
  an EARLIER transaction this process never observed — and the invoice's
  `blockkedje_referens`/`settlement_tx_hash` still carried the zero hash.
  `SettledPayment.settled?: boolean` (derived from `txHash !== ZERO_TX_HASH`)
  is replaced by an explicit `settlement: 'settled_onchain' |
  'already_settled_earlier' | 'settlement_unknown'`, threaded from each of the
  three call sites in `x402.ts` (no more boolean inference); the boolean had
  no other reader, so it is removed rather than kept as a derived alias.
  `already_settled_earlier` now gets its own heading ("Paid in an earlier
  transaction — the settlement reference is unavailable" / Swedish
  equivalent), its own `PurchaseSummary.status` and invoice `status` ("Betald
  i tidigare transaktion — referens saknas"), distinct from
  `settlement_unknown`'s unchanged #2970 "Delivered — not confirmed on-chain".
  `PurchaseSummary.settlement_tx_hash` and `InvoiceJson.blockkedje_referens`
  are now `string | null` — `null` on both non-settled states, never the zero
  hash — and the rendered invoice text omits the BLOCKCHAIN REFERENCE /
  BLOCKKEDJEREFERENS block entirely on those two states instead of printing
  it. The x402 WIRE to the buyer (`paymentResponse`, `paymentResponseHeader`,
  `transaction: ZERO_TX_HASH` on both non-settled paths) is byte-identical —
  that is the x402 protocol surface and stays out of scope, exactly as #2970
  left it. Mutation-tested: collapsing `already_settled_earlier` back into
  `settlement_unknown` at its `x402.ts` call site re-fails the http.test.ts
  and erc7710.test.ts already-used/restart-recovery assertions; reverting
  `blockkedje_referens` to always print `Tx: ${txHash}` unconditionally
  re-fails four assertions across invoice.test.ts, http.test.ts and
  erc7710.test.ts (the zero hash reappears); collapsing
  `PurchaseSummary`'s `already_settled_earlier` status back into
  `delivered_unsettled` re-fails purchase-summary.test.ts, http.test.ts and
  erc7710.test.ts. Because the state is now caller-supplied rather than
  derived, `buildSettledPayment` pins the one fact the hash can still assert
  (`assertSettlementConsistent`: a real hash is `settled_onchain`, the zero
  hash never is) and throws on a contradiction before anything is rendered;
  disabling that check re-fails settlement-state-invariant.test.ts.
  Perimeter unchanged.
