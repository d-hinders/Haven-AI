- **#2988** — demo merchant invoice numbering: the counter behind
  `fakturanummer` / `ocr_nummer` (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/invoice.ts`)
  was seeded from the clock in SECONDS at startup, so a restart re-issued
  numbers — and the OCRs derived from them — whenever more invoices had been
  issued than seconds had elapsed since the previous start. The seed is now
  milliseconds (`createInvoiceNumberer`); the `FAK-<year>-<digits>` shape is
  unchanged and the accounting feed keys on the payment id, not on this
  number. No payment, settlement, custody or authority change; the demo
  merchant is not on the money-path runtime globs. Mutation-tested: reverting
  to a seconds seed fails both new tests. Perimeter unchanged.
