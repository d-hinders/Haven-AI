- **#2991** — `haven_quote_mcp_tool` and `haven_quote_catalog_purchase` add
  two additive fields, `expected_settlement_scheme: 'erc7710' | 'eip3009' |
  null` and `expected_funding_leg: boolean | null` — plus
  `expected_settleable: boolean` when the rail is known, `false` where
  prepare/pay will refuse with `ERC7710_RAIL_REQUIRED` (erc7710-only merchant,
  non-delegation account) so a predicted scheme never reads as "Haven will
  settle it" — computed by running the
  IDENTICAL `selectX402SettlementScheme` selection call
  `haven_prepare_catalog_purchase` / `haven_pay_mcp_tool` run at prepare/pay
  time, so a read-only quote can never predict a different settlement shape
  than the one those tools go on to select. Before this, `accepted_scheme`
  described only the MERCHANT's offer: at a merchant advertising both a
  standard and an erc7710-tagged entry (the demo merchant's shape), a
  delegation-rail agent was quoted `accepted_scheme: 'standard'` and then
  asked, at prepare/pay, to sign an erc7710 settlement child instead — a
  different signature, no funding leg, and a different party triple than the
  quote implied, discovered only after the cap decision. The quote is now a
  truthful prediction of that shape, computed BEFORE the cap decision rather
  than after it. When the agent's execution rail cannot be read (the same
  non-throwing `getAgent` prefetch failure the pay/prepare tools already
  tolerate), `expected_settlement_scheme` is `null` with a new
  `X402_SCHEME_UNKNOWN` warning rather than a guess (prepare re-reads the
  rail and refuses hard on failure; `requireSettleableSelection` refuses an
  unknown rail only at an erc7710-only merchant — pre-existing).
  `expected_settleable: false` names the two cases where prepare/pay will
  refuse: an erc7710-only merchant for a non-delegation account
  (`ERC7710_RAIL_REQUIRED`) and ANY merchant for a non-delegation account
  (Haven's x402 entry points 410 every retired rail).
  `accepted_scheme` and `erc7710_only` are unchanged. No new network call: the
  agent read this reuses is the SAME one `quoteMcpX402` already makes
  internally for the x402-wallet address, deduped by the SDK's existing
  in-flight `getAgent` cache. No authority change — the quote still reserves
  no price, creates no intent, and moves no funds; the fields are read-only
  narration of a decision prepare/pay make independently and re-derive fresh
  regardless of what the quote predicted. Mutation-proven: hard-coding
  `expected_settlement_scheme` from `accepted_scheme` re-fails the
  both-entries/delegation-rail and the failed-agent-read tests; flipping
  `expected_funding_leg`'s scheme comparison (`=== 'erc7710'` instead of
  `=== 'eip3009'`) re-fails the delegation-rail and legacy-rail tests. A
  prepare run immediately after the quote in the same test asserts the two
  agree. Perimeter unchanged.
