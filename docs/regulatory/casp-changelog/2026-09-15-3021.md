- **#3021** — `X402_EMIT_PAYER_CONTEXT`, the operator flip that makes the
  backend emit the payer identity inside the Haven-signed x402 expected
  context (#1690, version 3), was read as `!== '1'` at every emit site: any
  other value — `true`, `on`, `0` — read as OFF with nothing logged, and on
  this flag a silent off is an invisible wire-format decision (the payer
  context simply not emitted). It is now parsed ONCE at boot through the
  #3015 `parseBooleanFlag` (`readEmitPayerContext` in
  `infra/chain/x402-binding-signer.ts`): the documented literal `1` and
  `true` mean on, `false`/unset/blank mean off, anything else refuses the boot
  naming the variable. The signed-context CONTENTS, the version derivation and
  the signer-side refusal are untouched — only how the operator's intent is
  read. Operator step before deploy: confirm the Railway dev/prod value of
  `X402_EMIT_PAYER_CONTEXT` is unset, `1` or `true`/`false`. Mutation-tested:
  reverting to `raw === '1'` re-fails three cases. Perimeter unchanged.
