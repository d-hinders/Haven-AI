- **#3116** — unsupported x402 transfer methods and payment flows are
  refused before funding or signing. `selectStandardPaymentOption` treated
  every non-erc7710 `accepts[]` entry as EIP-3009-compatible, so a first
  Permit2 entry (`extra.assetTransferMethod: 'permit2'`) or an entry naming
  an unrecognized `extra.paymentFlow` was selected ahead of a compatible
  alternative and signed by `packages/signer/src/core.ts` into an EIP-3009
  `authorization` payload (`signature`/`authorization`, no
  `permit2Authorization`) — a payload the official exact-EVM specification
  does not define for those entries, and §6.1 of the v2 specification
  forbids for an unrecognized flow. The fix reads the two protocol-reserved
  keys as claims: an entry is EIP-3009-constructible only when
  `extra.assetTransferMethod` is absent or exactly `'eip3009'` AND
  `extra.paymentFlow` is absent or exactly `'authorization'`; a present but
  unsupported value — including a malformed non-string one — makes the
  entry unsupported. Unsupported entries are SKIPPED when a supported
  alternative exists and refused (null → the no-compatible-option refusal,
  which now names the capability as the reason, #2054's honesty pattern)
  when unsupported-only — always before intent creation, funding, or
  signing. `toStandardPaymentRequirements` carries the same test as a
  last-stop guard so a future caller that bypasses the selectors cannot
  encode Permit2 as EIP-3009; implementing Permit2 remains out of scope by
  design. No authority surface moves: the refusal narrows what Haven
  constructs (Red Line 4's narrowing pattern) and changes nothing about who
  authorizes a payment — the owner-signed delegation and the agent-signed
  EIP-3009 transfer are unchanged, and the supported EIP-3009/erc7710 paths
  are pinned byte-identical by the pre-existing #1453/#1450
  characterization suites, which still pass. Characterization-first per the
  money-path playbook: the defect itself was pinned green on unmodified
  source before the selection change (issue reproduction re-run in this
  checkout at origin/dev @ ce79bf0c), then inverted by the fix commit.
  Boundary tests cover the SDK selectors, the client intent path
  (`createX402Intent`/`authorizeX402`), the edge-signer boundary
  (`buildX402PaymentHeader`), and the hosted prepare/pay/quote tools.
