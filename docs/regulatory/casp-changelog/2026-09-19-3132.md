- **#3132** — **Each list row states its scope, and the two silent fallbacks
  on the wallet feed's x402 rows are marked rather than hidden (epic #3130
  slice 2/4; owner decision 3). Reads only — no funds move, no signing path,
  no authority changes; `money-path` by file (`routes/machine-payments.ts` by
  exact path, `modules/mpp/**` by glob) and by the issue's label.**
  Before this change a settled x402 payment was reported by two surfaces
  that declared neither their population nor their narrowing: `GET /receipts`
  is agent-scoped by the authenticated principal (evidence rows only), while
  `GET /transactions` (the CLI's `activity list`) is wallet-scoped (every
  account's explorer window plus synthesized confirmed intents; sweeps and
  funding legs included) with `agentId` applied as a post-hoc filter — and
  nothing on either response said so. Now every row of both carries
  `scope: { source, filter }` as two separate values (`{ source: 'agent',
  filter: null }` on receipts; `{ source: 'wallet', filter: 'agent' |
  'account' | 'account+agent' | null }` on the feed), stated per row because
  the SDK's `mapPaymentReceipt` discards the envelope; the SDK carries it as
  `HavenPaymentReceipt.scope` (absent on an older backend, never invented).
  The synthesized x402 row's `paymentProofStatus` no longer substitutes
  `'payment_confirmed'` over the LEFT JOIN — a confirmed payment with no
  evidence row reports `null`, as `enrichment.ts` always did on the same
  endpoint (the lifecycle lands on the same `confirming_merchant`, so no flow
  status moves; the placeholder was outside the `'paid'` set, which is why
  this was a reconciliation defect and never a money-safety one). The row's
  `timestamp` keeps its `confirmed_at ?? created_at` sort-key fallback but
  `timestampSource` names the column that produced it (`'block'` on explorer
  rows) and `confirmedAt` carries the recorded, nullable confirmation time —
  the same value the receipts view reports, so the two views can no longer
  disagree on whether a payment has one. `haven_list_receipts`'s selection
  guidance stops inviting the substitution ("This agent's payment evidence,
  not the wallet's transaction history"); the hosted description mean stays
  under the #1591 budget (re-measured by `description-size.test.ts`).
  Additive only: no field renamed, removed or retyped; tests pin that the
  pre-change row shapes of both endpoints still validate. The vocabulary map
  (#3131) records the outcome: `proof state` and `payment rail / source`
  move from `blocked-on-fallback` to `converge-pending` (the third fallback
  the map assigned here is measured, not fixed: the synthesized row's
  `'x402'` restates the query's own `pi.source = 'x402'` predicate, and the
  SQL's `'direct'` classifies an intent that names no protocol at all),
  `settled at` converges on `confirmedAt`, and the baseline's open count is
  ratcheted 5 → 4. Perimeter argument: two read endpoints gain fields; no
  write, no new authority, Haven still holds no key and no funds. Mutation-
  tested (each restored byte-identically): the proof-status placeholder
  restored (red); `timestampSource` forced to `confirmed_at` (red);
  `confirmedAt` dropped (red); explorer rows' `'block'` mark dropped (red);
  the feed's `filter` forced to `null` under `agentId` (red); the receipts
  route's per-row `scope` dropped (red); the SDK mapper dropping `scope`
  (red). Custody unchanged. Perimeter unchanged.
