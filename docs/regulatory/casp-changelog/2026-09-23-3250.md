- **#3250** — catalog discovery now splits a normalized search into at most eight words and requires every word to match the product name, description, or category; the same rule applies to operator-curated and verified ingestion entries. This changes only which read-only discovery rows are returned. It does not join or search merchant names, and it does not create a quote, signature, approval, payment intent, or transfer. Existing status, rail, category, and network visibility constraints remain in place, while the live merchant 402 remains authoritative for recipient, asset, network, amount, and settlement options. API credentials remain identity-only and still cannot spend without the existing local signature and on-chain account policy. Perimeter unchanged.
