- **#3271** — a direct payment (`haven_send` / `haven_pay`, plain `POST /payments`) is now signed by `payment_id` instead of by an agent hand-relaying a base64 typed-data blob between tools, the relay that corrupted payment `291c33a8…` and failed on-chain with AA24. **No custody or authority boundary moves.** Three changes:
  - **Backend: `GET /payments/:id/sign-context` (`modules/payments/direct-sign-context.ts`).** A read-only, agent-authenticated route that serves the stored `prepared_user_op` of the caller's OWN `pending_signature` delegation-rail intent as the account's exact EIP-712 typed data, with its hash and `direct_sign_context_version`. Not-found and not-yours are one 404; retired-rail rows answer the same 410 tombstones `POST /payments/:id/sign` answers, checked before any status branch; confirmed, non-pending and expired rows are refused (409 / 410). It writes nothing beyond the lazy expiry the x402 sibling already performs. It returns bytes Haven already handed the agent in the `POST /payments` result, so it adds no disclosure.
  - **SDK + signer: the UserOp binding check (`packages/sdk/src/userop-binding.ts`).** Before signing any UserOp typed data (direct payments, whether fetched or relayed, and the x402 EIP-3009 funding leg through `signForData`, whose `sign_data.hash` is `fundingAuth.prepared.userOpHash`), the signer recomputes the ERC-4337 v0.7 UserOp hash from the typed data's own message and domain, and pins the domain and EntryPoint. A mismatch with `payload_hash` is refused with `USEROP_BINDING_MISMATCH` and nothing is signed. This narrows what the signer will sign; it widens nothing.
  - **Hosted MCP.** The direct-payment result names `haven_sign({ payment_id })` as its next step, keeps the `payload_hash` + `typed_data_b64` relay fields for older signers, and carries a `signer_compatibility` notice. Which route the agent takes is agent-mediated, the same pattern as #1155.

  Enforcement is unchanged: budget, recipient pin and expiry are still enforced by the caveat enforcers during gas estimation and redemption, and the agent's delegate key stays local. Haven serves only what it already prepared, and a served payload that does not hash to its own `payload_hash` is refused by the signer, so a compromised or man-in-the-middled sign-context cannot obtain a signature over a different UserOp. Tested: the route's refusal matrix on the real-DB harness (`payments-direct-sign-context.test.ts`), the binding check's refusal on each corrupted field (`userop-binding.test.ts`), and the signer's fetch, fallback and mismatch refusal (`direct-sign-fallback.test.ts`, `hosted-signer-integration.test.ts`). Perimeter unchanged; the signer's refusal surface narrows.
