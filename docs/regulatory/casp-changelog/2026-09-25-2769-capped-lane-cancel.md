- **#2769 (operator cancel for a relayer nonce lane the bump worker has given up on)**: an operator can now clear one more stuck-lane shape with the existing encoded cancel. No authority, custody, route or signing change, and nothing is triggered automatically. `cancelStuckOutboundLane` (`packages/backend/src/infra/outbound-lane-cancel.ts`, run as `ops:cancel-stuck-attest` or its new alias `ops:cancel-stuck-lane`) burns a stuck relayer nonce with a 0-value relayer self-send at that nonce, through the outbound pipeline. Until now it refused every rebroadcast-safe submitter (sweep, hybrid deploy, passport revoke, lane cancel) unconditionally, because the bump worker fee-replaces those itself. But the worker stops for good once a lane has three counted attempts, and hands the lane to an operator ("nonce lane stuck after 3 replacements — INCIDENT, not retrying"), so a capped rebroadcast-safe lane had no encoded recovery, only the hand-run self-send the runbooks forbid. That shape occurred on dev on 2026-09-25: rows stamped `broadcast` during the dRPC `flashblocks` refusal window never reached a node, their bump attempts failed and reached the cap, and later sends queued behind the hole at nonce 3170. The trigger now accepts a rebroadcast-safe row only when its nonce is AT the worker's cap (`countLaneAttemptsAtNonce` ≥ `MAX_BUMPS_PER_NONCE`); below the cap it still refuses (`automated_recovery_owns_it`) and sends nothing. Every other gate is unchanged: the row must be `broadcast`, stamped, older than the stale threshold and unmined by receipt, the claim is still the `broadcast` → `replaced` flip that lets exactly one trigger proceed, and the cancel is stamped before broadcast. Burning such a payload is safe because each owner retries on a fresh record: a sweep is re-run (its EIP-3009 authorization is single-use, so a late duplicate reverts and moves nothing), a hybrid deploy is re-attempted at the next activation (CREATE2, idempotent), and `reconcileRevocation` submits a fresh revoke. The cancel is a self-send of zero value, so it moves no funds; budget, recipient pin and expiry are still enforced by the caveat enforcers on-chain. Proven on real Postgres in both directions: a sweep one attempt below the cap is refused with nothing sent, and a capped sweep, which the bump tick demonstrably alerts on and walks away from, is cancelled at its nonce. Raising the gate by one fails the first test, and removing it fails the second. Residual, unchanged: a cancel releases every later nonce at once, so the operator must read what sits above N first, as `stuck-revoke-alarm.md` §4 precondition 5 requires. Perimeter unchanged.
