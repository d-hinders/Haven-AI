- **#2769 (relayer nonce when the RPC refuses `pending`)**: the relayer's nonce source for its own transactions changes on one provider refusal. No authority, custody, route or signing change. Every relayer send without an explicit nonce takes its nonce from `eth_getTransactionCount(relayer, "pending")` in `submitRecorded` (`packages/backend/src/infra/outbound-queue.ts`). That covers account deploys at first budget activation and at a fresh agent's first erc7710 authorize (`modules/x402/delegation-authorize.ts`, #1667), sweeps, passport attestations and revokes, and the bump worker's orphan re-sends. Lane cancels and the bump worker's same-nonce replacements pass an explicit nonce, never read `pending`, and are untouched by this change. dRPC's Base plans, the dev primary since 2026-09-24, answer that `pending` read with "no available upstreams … No label `flashblocks`" while serving `latest` normally. So every relayer send on dev failed from 2026-09-25. A new user's first budget activation answered 502 "Could not deploy the account", a fresh agent's first erc7710 authorize answered 502 for its undeployed delegate account, and sweeps could not return stranded delegate funds. UserOp payments, the EIP-3009 funding legs, merchant redemption of erc7710 settlements, and reads were unaffected: none of them uses the relayer's nonce. On that refusal only (`isPendingTagRefusal`, matching dRPC's refusal text), the nonce is the first one at or above the same provider's `latest` count that no live `outbound_txs` row holds. It is a walk over CONTIGUOUS live broadcasts (`listLiveBroadcastNoncesFrom`, backed by the existing partial UNIQUE `idx_outbound_txs_live_nonce`), never a jump to `MAX(nonce)+1`, so a stale row far above the count cannot push every later send into a gap. Every send with a record id is stamped there before it is broadcast, so the walk steps over our own in-flight transactions that the node will not report. The ledger read fails closed: a database error propagates before anything is signed. Any other error from the `pending` read (timeout, rate limit, dead endpoint, a differently worded refusal) propagates unchanged. Residuals: the ledger cannot see an unstamped send (a failed-open record, the bump worker's orphan re-send) or the two transient handoffs (a lane cancel stamping after the attest it cancels left `broadcast`; the bump worker between marking a row replaced and stamping its successor). A fresh send can then take that nonce and be rejected, or, with fees at least 10% higher on both fields, replace our own unrecorded transaction. A live row whose transaction was dropped at `latest` is stepped over, so later sends stall behind that hole until the bump worker re-sends it (a rebroadcast-safe submitter: sweep, deploy, revoke, lane cancel) or an operator clears it (a `passport_attest`, or a lane past its bump cap), as the passport runbook describes. The send lock, the stamp-before-broadcast fence and the UNIQUE live-nonce index are unchanged. A nonce decides only the ORDER of the relayer's own transactions, never what they do: the relayer still signs a plain factory deploy or a sweep whose destination the account's own delegation fixes, and budget, recipient pin and expiry are still enforced by the caveat enforcers on-chain. No residual can misdirect funds. Perimeter unchanged.
