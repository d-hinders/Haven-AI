- **#3307:** receipt and payment-status addresses are EIP-55 checksummed at their read boundaries. **No custody or authority boundary moves. This is a value-casing change on two read surfaces.**
  - **What changed.**
    - `mapEvidence` (`modules/mpp/evidence.ts`) checksums the receipt's `merchant_address`, `payer_address`, `settlement_address`, `token_address` and every `parties` entry.
    - `getAgentPaymentStatus` (`modules/payments/agent-payment-status.ts`) checksums its top-level `merchant_address` / `payer_address` and its `parties`. The payment-status part is an owner decision on #3307.
    - It uses the same `toCanonicalAddress` the transactions feed has used since #3129, so a payment's receipt, transaction row and status agree byte for byte.
  - **Untouched.**
    - Storage stays `LOWER(...)`, and `tx_hash` is unchanged.
    - The relayed merchant objects stay byte-for-byte (#3125).
    - The status rail context stays as stored. `resume_state` rebuilds the merchant-facing payment requirement (`accepted.payTo`, `asset`) from it, and that payload is pinned byte-identical by a test.
    - The signed receipt bundle is unchanged: its content is signed and its verifier compares lowercased.
    - The x402 authorize payloads to merchants and facilitators are unchanged.
  - **No behavioural change.** No execution path, signing input, spend check, allowance, fee or accounting entry changes. The OpenAPI `address` pattern is case-agnostic, so no wire schema moves.

  No route, credential scope, key-material handling, signing path or spend authority is added or widened. Perimeter unchanged.
