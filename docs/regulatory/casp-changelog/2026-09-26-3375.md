- **#3375** — **The SDK's own EIP-3009 funding leg is pinned to where it pays (epic #3284, slice 3). This narrows the delegate key's signing surface. No funds move differently, no route, credential scope or custody changes.** `HavenClient`'s in-process x402 funding leg (`X402FundingLeg` → `signForData`) now runs `assertFundingLegPaysDelegate`, the recipient pin #3281 added to the signer's x402 arm. The leg's single execution must transfer the 402 option's amount of the 402 option's token into the signing key's own delegate EOA. The delegate address comes from the local key, and the token and amount from the option being paid, never from Haven's `/x402` response.

  Authority argument: under the epic's owner-confirmed threat model, Haven's API is untrusted for the signing decision. Before this change, a compromised API could serve a funding-leg UserOp that passed every earlier check (own account, pinned chain, one redemption of the budget grant) while paying any recipient up to an open budget. After it, such a leg can only move the quoted amount into the agent's own EOA. That is the hot-delegate residual the sweep already covers. Accepted residual 1 narrows on the SDK path. Direct payments (`pay()`) are deliberately untouched, since their recipient is the caller's intent and the budget caveats bound it. The SDK's `sweepDelegate()` destination (served by Haven's API) is now named in the security model's residual 2 and is not closed here.

  Live paths: the backend builds exactly this shape (`delegation-authorize.ts` passes the requested amount and the delegate `payTo` to `prepareRedemption`), so the pin refuses no live leg. The SDK and `@haven_ai/mcp` test fixtures were rebuilt to that shape, not exempted. The funding-leg fixtures pay the delegate. `haven_send`'s direct-payment fixture keeps a third-party recipient, and `signing-guard.test.ts`'s `pay()` control asserts its recipient is not the delegate.

  Tests: `packages/sdk/src/funding-leg-pin.test.ts` serves `authorizeX402` guard-shaped legs paying a different recipient, amount or token, plus a two-link `[task, budget]` leg paying someone else. Each is refused by the pin, with nothing posted to `/sign`. The controls (the backend's shape, single- and two-link) sign. Disabling the pin, or dropping the expectation from the funding leg's wiring, turns four of them red. Perimeter narrowed.
