- **#3392 — idempotent replay now refuses a `task_budget` mismatch with a 409, on every replay path (a #3328 follow-up).** An idempotency key already pinned a payment's token, recipient and amount; the task budget the payment was charged to was not part of that pin, so a retry reusing the key with a different `task_budget_id` — a different budget, none where the first payment had one, or one where it had none — was answered with the FIRST payment, charged to the other budget, with nothing saying so. Nothing was ever charged twice; what was wrong was attribution and the caller's view of which budget paid.
  - **Where it was fixed.** `/payments`: the replay `mismatch()` compares `task_budget_id` alongside token/recipient/amount (lower-cased, "absent" is a value), and the replay lookup now selects the column. `/x402` (delegation rail): the mismatch comparison — budget included — now runs on `pending_signature` (unexpired) AND `confirmed` rows BEFORE the confirmed-200 replay branch, at all three `delegationReplay` call sites via the shared replay context; expired rows are still never compared and a lazily expired row still frees the key. SDK: the x402 receipt cache and in-flight map record the `taskBudgetId` each entry was created under; `authorizeX402`, `fetch`, `payX402Quote` and `resumeAuthorizedX402` throw the new typed `X402TaskBudgetMismatchError` before any network call instead of returning or joining a receipt paid under another budget. The erc7710 path still has no receipt cache.
  - **Owner decision (2026-09-26):** refuse with a 409, exactly like a token, recipient or amount mismatch. A caller who wants to pay again uses a new key. Fail-closed; never a silent replay onto another budget.
  - **No authority change.** The caller, Haven and the enforcers keep exactly their previous powers; on-chain enforcement is unchanged. No payment can be made that could not be made before, and none is refused that the rail would have honoured under the named budget — the replay contract now reports the budget mismatch it used to hide. Not authority-narrowing, not authority-widening.
  - **Wire compatibility.** No wire field is added, removed or renamed and there is no migration (`payment_intents.task_budget_id` exists since migration 095). Published SDKs before 0.6.0-alpha.0 never send `taskBudgetId`, so their retries are none-vs-none and replay as today; the only mixed case is a `0.0.0-dev.*` snapshot from between #3329 and #3378, which now gets a 409 where it silently replayed — intended.
  - **Perimeter unchanged.**
