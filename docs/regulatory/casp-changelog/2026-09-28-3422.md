- **#3422** — demo merchant invoice OCR check digit: `generateOcr`
  (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/invoice.ts`) appended a Luhn digit computed
  with the VALIDATING form of the algorithm, which doubles from the
  second-to-last digit. When a check digit is being generated, the base's
  rightmost digit is the first one doubled. So every `ocr_nummer` the demo
  merchant issued failed the modulus-10 validation Bankgirot OCR references
  use. The four OCRs observed on prod purchases on 2026-09-28 reproduce
  exactly. The helper is now `luhnCheckDigit` and starts doubling at the
  rightmost digit. Only the last digit of `ocr_nummer` changes; its length
  (base + 1), the `fakturanummer` it derives from, and every other invoice
  field are unchanged, and the accounting feed keys on the payment id, not on
  this number. No payment, settlement, custody or authority change; the demo
  merchant is not on the money-path runtime globs. Mutation-tested: restoring
  the old doubling start fails all three new tests (textbook vector, the four
  live invoice numbers, 200 consecutive numbers through an independent
  validator). Perimeter unchanged.
