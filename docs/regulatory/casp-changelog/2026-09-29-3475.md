- **#3475** — an agent can report an eip3009 payment's merchant settlement.
  On the plain-HTTP x402 path with the EIP-3009 funding leg, Haven recorded
  only its own funding transaction (treasury → the agent's delegate EOA). The
  merchant's settlement (delegate → merchant) reached no receipt, because the
  agent retries that merchant itself (#2292) and no tool accepted the hash.
  Per the owner decision on #3475, `haven_report_settlement_evidence` now
  takes it too. `haven_report_x402_outcome` keeps its no-hash contract. The
  backend seam behind `POST /machine-payments/evidence`
  (`packages/backend/src/modules/mpp/evidence.ts`) sends a funded eip3009
  intent reported with a hash other than its funding hash to
  `observeEip3009MerchantSettlement`
  (`packages/backend/src/modules/x402/eip3009-settlement-evidence.ts`). That
  verifies the hash with the existing `verifySettlementTransferTx`: a
  successful transaction carrying a Transfer of exactly the intent's amount,
  in its token, from its delegate to its merchant, mined between funding
  confirmation (minus 120 s skew) and `expires_at` plus the SDK's authorization
  lifetime (`X402_MAX_AUTHORIZATION_WINDOW_SECONDS` +
  `X402_SETTLEMENT_FORWARD_MARGIN_SECONDS`) plus skew. It then records the hash
  as `machine_metadata.merchant_settlement_tx_hash`
  (`recordEip3009MerchantSettlement`,
  `packages/backend/src/infra/repositories/x402-authorizations.ts`).
  Receipts prefer that verified hash over the merchant's unverified
  `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` echo.
  The write refuses:
  - a hash another payment already holds, as its funding or its recorded
    settlement;
  - a second, different hash for the same payment;
  - a block that an earlier-funded, same-shaped payment with no recorded
    settlement could equally own. Haven never sees the EIP-3009 nonce on
    this path, so overlapping look-alikes are attributed oldest first;
    blocking both ways would lock such a pair out for good.
  The write runs under the erc7710 confirm's per-hash advisory lock.
  **No custody or authority change:** nothing is signed, submitted or moved.
  The intent's status, its funding `tx_hash` and its evidence row's proof
  status stay as they were; a verified settlement proves the merchant pulled
  the funds, not that it delivered. Haven still never contacts the merchant.
  No key, signature, delegation, caveat, budget or recipient pin changes.
  Mutation-tested on the real-DB suite
  (`modules/mpp/__tests__/eip3009-settlement-evidence.test.ts`, 23 tests):
  - bypassing the seam fails 18 tests;
  - blocking look-alikes in both directions fails the oldest-first test;
  - a twin guard that never matches fails it too;
  - a hash-taken guard that never matches fails 2 tests;
  - accepting a second different hash fails 1;
  - dropping the lower window bound fails 1;
  - widening the upper bound tenfold fails 1;
  - receipts that ignore the verified hash fail 2;
  - rewriting the proof status fails 1;
  - moving the rail check, or the resource check, after the chain read fails 1
    each.
  Perimeter unchanged.
