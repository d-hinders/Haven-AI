- **#3475** — an agent can report an eip3009 payment's merchant settlement.
  On the plain-HTTP x402 path with the EIP-3009 funding leg, Haven recorded
  only its own funding transaction (treasury → the agent's delegate EOA). The
  merchant's settlement (delegate → merchant) reached no receipt, because the
  agent retries that merchant itself (#2292) and no tool accepted the hash.
  Per the owner decision on #3475, `haven_report_settlement_evidence` now
  takes it too. `haven_report_x402_outcome` keeps its no-hash contract.
  The backend seam behind `POST /machine-payments/evidence`
  (`packages/backend/src/modules/mpp/evidence.ts`) handles a funded eip3009
  intent reported with a hash other than its funding hash as follows:
  - It checks the rail and resource, and writes the base evidence row.
  - `observeEip3009MerchantSettlement`
    (`packages/backend/src/modules/x402/eip3009-settlement-evidence.ts`)
    verifies the hash with the existing `verifySettlementTransferTx`. The
    transaction must succeed and carry a Transfer of exactly the intent's
    amount, in its token, from its delegate to its merchant. It must be mined
    after funding confirmed (minus 120 s skew) and before the report (plus
    skew). The far edge is the report rather than `expires_at`, because a
    funded payment can be re-signed long after its deadline (#2290).
  - It records the hash as `machine_metadata.merchant_settlement_tx_hash`
    (`recordEip3009MerchantSettlement`,
    `packages/backend/src/infra/repositories/x402-authorizations.ts`), under
    the erc7710 confirm's per-hash advisory lock.

  The write refuses a hash another payment already holds, as its funding or
  its recorded settlement. That check runs from the eip3009 side only. It also
  refuses a second, different hash for the same payment (a compare-and-set on
  `IS NULL`).
  Which of two same-shaped payments a transfer settled is not decided: Haven
  never sees the EIP-3009 nonce on this path. A look-alike guard was built and
  dropped in review, because every payment it refused stayed unrecorded and
  refused the next. The bound instead: a hash can be recorded on any
  earlier-funded payment of the same agent, amount, token, delegate and
  merchant, including one whose merchant leg failed; its receipt and the
  funded-retry remedy then follow that attribution. That takes an agent
  misreporting a payment id, and no money moves because of it.

  Receipts prefer the verified hash over the merchant's unverified
  `PAYMENT-RESPONSE` echo. `isFundedX402AwaitingMerchantLeg`
  (`packages/backend/src/modules/payments/agent-payment-status.ts`) excludes a
  payment with a recorded settlement. The status therefore stops offering
  `retry_original_x402_request`, and sign-context stops re-opening signing, for
  a payment whose merchant settlement is recorded. A refusal's 409 now carries its reason and no
  longer says the payment is unconfirmed.

  **No custody or authority change:** nothing is signed, submitted or moved.
  The intent's status, its funding `tx_hash` and its evidence row's proof
  status stay as they were; a verified settlement proves the merchant pulled
  the funds, not that it delivered. The one authority-adjacent change narrows
  signing: the delegate key is no longer offered a second EIP-3009
  authorization for a payment whose merchant provably took the first. Haven
  still never contacts the merchant. No key, signature, delegation, caveat,
  budget or recipient pin changes.

  **Mutation-tested** on the real-DB suites
  (`modules/mpp/__tests__/eip3009-settlement-evidence.test.ts`, 27 tests;
  `modules/payments/__tests__/x402-funded-unsettled-status.test.ts`, 20).
  Each mutation below fails the listed number of tests:
  - bypassing the seam: 20;
  - a hash-taken check that never matches: 6;
  - removing the UPDATE's own `NOT EXISTS`: 1;
  - accepting a second, different hash: 1;
  - dropping the lower window bound: 1;
  - an unbounded upper edge: 1;
  - receipts that ignore the verified hash: 2;
  - removing the rail check: 1;
  - removing the resource check: 1;
  - ignoring a base-row failure: 1;
  - rewriting the proof status: 1;
  - a 409 without its reason: 1;
  - a funded-retry predicate that ignores a recorded settlement: 1 in the
    status suite.

  Perimeter unchanged.
