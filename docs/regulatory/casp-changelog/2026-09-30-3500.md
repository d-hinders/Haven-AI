- **#3500** — a payment past an open task budget's cap is a typed refusal,
  decided before anything is built. A task budget's cap is an
  `ERC20TransferAmountEnforcer` caveat on its child delegation, enforced
  on-chain; a payment past it reverted during simulation and reached the agent
  as an untyped 502 whose hosted next step said "transient, retry once". On
  the erc7710 leg, where Haven builds no UserOp, it would only have failed at
  the merchant's redemption. The refusal ledger booked that revert as the
  generic `onchain_revert`.

  Now every path that takes a task budget pre-checks it:
  - `POST /payments` (`packages/backend/src/routes/payments.ts`);
  - the x402 EIP-3009 funding leg and the erc7710 authorize
    (`packages/backend/src/modules/x402/delegation-authorize.ts`).

  The pre-check (`checkTaskBudgetCap`,
  `packages/backend/src/modules/task-budgets/task-budget-cap.ts`) reads the
  enforcer's own `spentMap(delegationManager, childHash)`
  (`packages/backend/src/infra/chain/task-budget-spent-reader.ts`), the figure
  the chain applies. It refuses with a typed 403 `task_budget_exceeded`
  (task budget id, remaining, max and amount in atomic units) before any
  UserOp or settlement child exists. An unreadable chain skips the check, and
  the enforcer remains the gate.

  If a transfer-cap revert reaches the chain anyway, it gets the same 403, but
  only once the task budget's own spent figure confirms it. The same enforcer
  carries a budget delegation's optional lifetime cap, and that case keeps its
  502. Both are booked as `delegation_budget_exceeded`, the task-budget one
  with `detail.error_code: task_budget_exceeded`; no migration.

  The hosted MCP maps the 403 to `TASK_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` /
  `stop_and_tell_user` (`packages/mcp-server/src/tools/support/errors.ts`),
  never the retry step.

  **No custody or authority change:** a refusal decided earlier than the
  chain would decide it, from the chain's own figure. Nothing is signed, built
  or moved on a refused payment; no key, signature, delegation, caveat, budget
  or recipient pin changes, and the on-chain enforcer is still the control.
