- **#3503** — a direct payment past the agent's remaining PERIOD budget is a
  typed refusal, decided before anything is built. The x402 legs have pre-checked
  that budget since #2082/#2706; `POST /payments`
  (`packages/backend/src/routes/payments.ts`) had not. There, an over-budget
  payment was answered only by the `ERC20PeriodTransferEnforcer` revert during
  simulation: an untyped 502 whose hosted next step said "transient, retry
  once".

  The route now reads the live remaining period budget
  (`readRemainingBudget`, `packages/backend/src/infra/chain/delegation-budget-reader.ts`)
  of the delegation the payment redeems: a sub-budget's or task budget's parent
  by hash, else the (token, recipient) grant. That grant is selected once and
  handed to `prepareDelegationPayment`
  (`packages/backend/src/rails/delegation-authorization.ts`), so the row read is
  the row redeemed. A payment it cannot cover gets the 403
  `delegation_budget_exceeded` body the x402 legs answer (remaining, shortfall
  and amount in human and atomic units, `next_action:
  fund_account_or_raise_allowance`) before any UserOp exists. An unreadable
  read, or a delegation without a readable period caveat, skips the check.

  If a period revert reaches the simulation anyway (a concurrent payment landed
  between read and prepare), it gets the same 403, but only once a fresh read
  confirms it (`isPeriodBudgetRevert`,
  `packages/backend/src/modules/payments/refusal-ledger.ts`); otherwise it keeps
  its 502. Both refusals are booked as `delegation_budget_exceeded`, source
  `payment`; no migration.

  **No custody or authority change:** a refusal decided earlier than the chain
  would decide it, from the chain's own figure. Nothing is signed, built or
  moved on a refused payment; no key, signature, delegation, caveat, budget or
  recipient pin changes, and the on-chain enforcer is still the control.

  **Mutation-tested** in `payments-period-budget.test.ts`, each mutation
  failing the listed number of tests:
  - the pre-check removed: 2;
  - `<=` for `<` (the exact remainder refused): 1;
  - fail closed on `fromChain: false`: 1;
  - fail closed on an unreadable read: 1;
  - the revert fallback removed: 1;
  - the revert predicate never matching: 1;
  - a task budget reading the (token, recipient) grant instead of its parent: 2;
  - `prepareDelegationPayment` re-selecting instead of using the passed row: 2;
  - the shortfall miscomputed: 2;
  - the pre-check booking no ledger row: 1.

  Perimeter unchanged.
