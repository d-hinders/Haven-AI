- **#3503** — a direct payment past the agent's remaining PERIOD budget is a
  typed refusal, decided before anything is built. The x402 legs have pre-checked
  that budget since #2082/#2706; `POST /payments`
  (`packages/backend/src/routes/payments.ts`) had not. There, an over-budget
  payment was answered only by the `ERC20PeriodTransferEnforcer` revert during
  simulation: an untyped 502 whose hosted next step said "transient, retry
  once".

  The route now reads the live remaining period budget
  (`readRemainingBudget`, `packages/backend/src/infra/chain/delegation-budget-reader.ts`)
  of the delegations the payment redeems:
  - a task budget's parent, by hash;
  - for a sub-budget, all three links (B's grant, A's parent-child link, A's
    budget), each of which carries its own period caveat — the smallest
    remaining decides;
  - otherwise the (token, recipient) grant, selected once and handed to
    `prepareDelegationPayment`
    (`packages/backend/src/rails/delegation-authorization.ts`), so the row read
    is the row redeemed.

  A payment they cannot cover gets a 403 `delegation_budget_exceeded` with the
  error code and budget fields the x402 legs answer (remaining, shortfall and
  amount in human and atomic units, `next_action:
  fund_account_or_raise_allowance`) before any UserOp exists. An unreadable
  link, or one without a readable period caveat, is skipped (fail open).

  If a period revert reaches the simulation anyway (a pre-check that failed
  open, or a concurrent payment landing between read and prepare), it gets the
  same 403, but only once a fresh read confirms it (`isPeriodBudgetRevert`,
  `packages/backend/src/modules/payments/refusal-ledger.ts`); otherwise it keeps
  its 502. Both refusals are booked as `delegation_budget_exceeded`, source
  `payment`; no migration.

  **Ledger classification correction.** A bundler relays an enforcer's reason
  as an ABI-encoded `Error(string)`, hex, so the ledger's revert patterns
  (period budget, #3500's transfer cap, expiry) only ever matched decoded test
  fixtures, and a real caveat revert was booked as `onchain_revert`.
  `flattenErrorText` now decodes those payloads, so such reverts are booked
  under their named reason. Reporting only: no refusal decision changes except
  through the revert fallbacks above (and #3500's task-budget one, which
  depended on the same match).

  **No custody or authority change:** a refusal decided earlier than the chain
  would decide it, from the chain's own figures. Nothing is signed, built or
  moved on a refused payment; no key, signature, delegation, caveat, budget or
  recipient pin changes, and the on-chain enforcer is still the control.

  **Live QA:** the `over-budget-refused` leg (`packages/qa-agent`) now asserts
  the typed 403 with a `remaining_atomic` equal to the live read, and fails on
  a 502. It used to be the one live leg that watched the enforcer revert; no
  live leg does now. The deployed period enforcer's own refusal stays proven
  in CI by `non-custody-onchain-enforcer.contract.test.ts` (an `eth_call` of
  its `beforeHook` over budget on Base Sepolia), and a budget compiled without
  its period caveat still turns the leg red (the pre-check fails open and the
  request comes back signable).
