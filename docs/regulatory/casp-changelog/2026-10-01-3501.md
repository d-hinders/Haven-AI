- **#3501** — task budgets now REPORT what the chain still allows through
  them. `GET /task-budgets` and `GET /task-budgets/:id` attach
  `spent_atomic` / `remaining_atomic` (plus display forms and a
  `remaining_is_from_chain` honesty flag, #1319-style) to open rows, read
  live from the same authority the cap is enforced with — the
  `ERC20TransferAmountEnforcer`'s `spentMap(delegationManager, childHash)`
  (`packages/backend/src/infra/chain/task-budget-spent-reader.ts`, now behind
  a bounded advisory transport; the enforcing pre-check keeps its unbounded
  read). The SDK's `getAgentSummary()` maps the figures into
  `haven_get_agent`'s `taskBudgets[]`, and `haven_get_allowances` names
  `haven_get_agent` as where task budgets live.

  **No custody or authority change:** the surface is read-only. Nothing is
  signed, built, moved or refused differently — the enforcer's own storage
  stays the only authority on what a child may still transfer, and the
  cap's on-chain verdict at redemption is untouched. The one behaviour
  decision is honesty about unreadable state: a failed on-chain read
  reports `spent_atomic: null, remaining_atomic: null` with
  `remaining_is_from_chain: false` and a server-side warning — it never
  falls back to the full cap as "remaining", because that optimistic lie is
  exactly what made the #3501 agent attempt a payment the chain had to
  revert. No new route, no key, signature, delegation, caveat, budget or
  recipient pin changes; owners and agents revoke and read permissions
  outside Haven exactly as before.

  **Tested:** unit coverage pins the degraded read (null figures, false
  flag, warning — never the cap), the clamp at zero, the parallel
  enrichment, key presence/absence by row status on both routes, the SDK
  presence-preserving mapping and summary derivation, and the #1591
  description-size pins re-derived for the added tool-description bytes.
  Perimeter unchanged.
