- **#3518** — the budget precheck now selects the payment's OWN budget
  (`selectBudgetForPaymentReport`, `packages/backend/src/infra/repositories/
  delegation-budgets.ts`, mirroring the payment rule
  `SELECT_DELEGATION_FOR_PAYMENT_SQL`) instead of the FIRST per-token row,
  in `mpp/budget-precheck.ts` and the same first match in
  `mpp/balance-coverage.ts`. This is a REFUSAL GATE change, stated in both
  directions: an agent holding an open 0.001 budget plus a recipient-pinned
  0.005 budget for the same token was refused buying from the pinned
  merchant (the compare ran against the open row) and now passes; an agent
  holding a large open budget beside a small pin for that payee passed the
  compare against the open row and is now refused on the pin's remaining.
  The pinless-quote fallback is stated: no `merchantTo` in the call leaves
  only the open budget eligible — a quote that names no payee cannot claim
  a recipient-scoped grant. When the agent then has NO open budget for the
  token but holds live merchant-locked ones, the precheck answers 409
  `budget_requires_recipient` naming the pins and records nothing: an
  incomplete question is not an exhausted budget, and is never ledgered as
  a `delegation_budget_exceeded` refusal (`balance-coverage` reports the
  same pins beside its "0"). The reported figures (success and refusal)
  describe the budget that pays, and the bodies carry its identity
  (`budget_id`, `budget_delegation_hash`, `budget_recipient_address`,
  `budget_merchant_id`).

  **No custody or authority change:** the precheck never moved money and
  never admits what the chain would refuse — it READS which delegation the
  payment path already selects and refuses earlier on that same row. The
  `ERC20PeriodTransferEnforcer` in the delegation's caveat stack remains
  the actual spend gate at redemption; the selection change makes the
  off-chain check AGREE with the on-chain gate instead of diverging from
  it (both directions above are the check aligning with the enforcer: it
  no longer refuses a payment the enforcer would redeem, and no longer
  passes one the enforcer would revert). Nothing signed, built, relayed or
  retried changes; no delegation, caveat, key, recipient pin or ledger
  semantics move — the refusal ledger row's merchant column keeps its
  #3492 role and the typed refusal contract (`error_code`, `phase`,
  `next_action`, `remaining_atomic`, `shortfall_atomic`) is unchanged,
  only citing a different (now correct) budget's remaining. Reporting
  changes are visibility only: settle-summary selection by the recorded
  `budget_delegation_hash` (never re-derived), allowance scope fields
  (`recipient_address`, `merchant_id`), `reserved_haven_atomic` summed
  beside the on-chain remaining (never folded into it), the agent list's
  new `status=live` filter, and `haven_get_task_budget` read-by-id — reads
  over rows and routes that already existed. A settle summary whose
  recorded paying budget is not one of the agent's own (a sub-budget
  payment, or a re-key between pay and settle) now reports the figure
  unavailable instead of another budget's remaining.
