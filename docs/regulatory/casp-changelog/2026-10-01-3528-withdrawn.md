- **#3531 (owner decision) — the #3528 own-account prepare hint is
  withdrawn.** PR #3558 (#3528) added a `SELF_TRANSFER` warning to the
  payment prepare response whenever the recipient was one of the owner's own
  Haven accounts (`listOwnerAddressesForUser` over `smart_accounts`). That
  answer is a membership oracle: an agent — or whoever controls its API key —
  could prepare payments to guessed addresses and learn which belong to the
  owner, which is exactly why the owner restricted #3531's recipient
  classification to the agent's OWN payment history (`previously_paid` /
  `new_address`) and ruled `own_account` out. The owner decided (2026-10-01)
  that this account information must not be exposed, so #3558 is reverted in
  full: the owner-address query, the hint module and its call sites in
  `routes/payments.ts` and `modules/x402/delegation-authorize.ts`, the
  `warnings` field on `SignablePaymentIntent` (spec and `packages/core`
  types), the SDK relay (`client.ts`, `x402.ts`, `x402-erc7710.ts`) and the
  hosted MCP relay (`plain-http-x402.ts`, `state-direct-recovery.ts`). The
  #3528 shard stays as the record of what shipped to `dev`.

  The change never reached `main`, so no production prepare ever carried the
  hint. It moved no money and changed no authority, refusal or `sign_data`
  in either direction: adding it was warning-grade and removing it returns
  every prepare to its pre-#3558 bytes, which the reverted tests had pinned
  as the stranger case. Nothing in the delegation, its caveats, the signer
  or the refusal ledger changes. Perimeter unchanged.
