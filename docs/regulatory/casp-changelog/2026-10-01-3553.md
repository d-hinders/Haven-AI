- **#3553** — sub-budget issuance and its owner signature relay refuse a retired agent. The files in scope:
  - **Issuer gate:** `POST /agents/:id/sub-budgets` and `POST /agents/:id/sub-budgets/:sub/sign` (`routes/agent-sub-budgets.ts`) answer 409 `issuer_retired` when the issuing agent is revoked or archived, before body validation and before anything is built or written. That includes a half-revoked agent whose budget delegation is still active on-chain (the #3542 state).
  - **Sub-agent gate:** issuance answers 409 `sub_agent_retired` when the receiving agent is revoked, archived or still `pending_approval`; only an `active` or `paused` agent may receive a sub-budget (owner decision, 2026-10-01). The dashboard's sub-agent picker applies the same rule.
  - `DelegationAgentRow` carries `archived_at`; the gate lives in the two routes, not in `loadOwnedDelegationAgent`, so the authority-reducing routes (sub-budget `DELETE`, the list and tree reads, `revoke-all`) keep serving retired agents.

  Authority and custody argument: authority only narrows; nothing is granted.
  - **Invariant (owner-confirmed threat model, 2026-10-01):** no new spend authority is created through or for a revoked or archived agent, matching `REVOKED_AGENT_REFUSAL` on budget delegations. Before this, a half-revoked issuer could have new sub-budget rows carved from its live budget, and the owner relay would open a `pending` row with any signature that recovers the delegate key, including one made before revocation.
  - **Unchanged:** the narrowing refusal against the parent delegation, the signer-recovery check on the relay, and the agent-side submit (`agentAuth` already refuses revoked and archived agents). `paused` issuers and sub-agents pass, as on the delegation routes. Haven still signs nothing.
  - **Residuals, accepted by the owner:** an existing active budget of a half-revoked agent, and any open sub-budget under it, stay redeemable until the owner ends the issuer's budget (Finish revoking, #3542); `pending` rows created before revocation are not closed, and the relay gate stops them opening; a check-then-insert race with a concurrent revoke can leave at most `pending` rows, which the relay refuses; the in-flight re-key check is absent from these routes and is a separate follow-up.

Frontend tests pin the refusal copy for both codes and the picker rule.
