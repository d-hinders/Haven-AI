- **#3564** — the receipt-unconfirmed submit (`POST /payments/:id/sign`) is
  re-booked: the intent row is NO longer marked `failed` before its on-chain
  outcome is known. `SubmittedUserOpFailedError` now carries a typed
  `outcome` (`rails/delegation-rail.ts`) — `receipt_unconfirmed` (the
  `waitForUserOperationReceipt` wait threw or timed out; the op MAY have
  landed) vs `included_reverted` (the op landed and reverted; nothing
  moved). The #3494 classification below still runs ONLY on the known
  half: the reverted variant and every pre-send bundler rejection are
  booked `failed` by `failSubmittedIntent` exactly as before. The unknown
  half instead books the row outcome-PENDING — `status` stays `submitted`,
  `machine_metadata` gains the recorded `user_op_hash` and
  `submission_outcome: 'unknown'` (`infra/repositories/payment-intents.ts`
  `bookSubmittedOutcomePending`, a CAS on the open submit) — and answers
  the `submission_outcome_unknown` 502 with `status: "submitted"` and the
  remedy "poll `haven_get_payment_status`". Status IS the source of truth
  now: while unresolved, `/machine-payments/:id/status` answers
  `next_action: check_status_later` with a do-not-recreate reason
  (`agent-payment-status.ts`); once resolved, it answers the real terminal
  state. Non-custody unchanged: the row's terminal state is derived only
  from the bundler's receipt for the recorded hash, read back through the
  SAME credential the submit used (`readUserOperationReceipt`), by the
  leader-gated submission reconciler tick (`submission-reconciler.ts`);
  a read that proves nothing (RPC error, unknown hash, missing
  credential) is "not known yet", never a verdict, and an op still
  unseen after a bounded window (24 h) resolves `failed` with that
  cause. Every terminal write is a CAS, so a concurrent settle wins and
  the tick is idempotent. Users keep full access outside Haven: the
  reconciliation only records what the chain already proves, and the
  userOpHash is on the row and in the 502 body for independent
  verification on an explorer. No new spender, no new key custody, no
  Haven-signed authority, no widening of any delegation: the reconciler
  writes only what the chain's own receipt already proves. Perimeter
  unchanged.
