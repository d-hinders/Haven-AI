- **#3609** — a delegation-rail prepare that fails is a typed, bounded answer,
  and a deterministic revert is no longer told to retry. After the typed budget
  fallbacks (#3500's task-budget re-read on both routes, #3503's period re-read
  on `POST /payments` only), the prepare catch on `POST /payments`
  (`packages/backend/src/routes/payments.ts`) and on the x402 EIP-3009 funding
  leg (`packages/backend/src/modules/x402/delegation-authorize.ts`) answered one
  untyped 502 carrying the whole viem error, redacted but unbounded, as
  `details`. Measured live on prod
  2026-10-02 it was about 6 KB: the whole `callData` (delegation chain,
  signatures, WebAuthn client data), the paymaster data and the hex revert. The
  hosted MCP turned every such 5xx into "transient, retry once", including a
  caveat revert that reverts again on every retry.

  Now both catches answer through `prepareFailureBody`
  (`packages/backend/src/modules/payments/prepare-failure.ts`), split by the
  refusal ledger's own classifier (`classifyRevertForLedger`) and by whether
  the revert happened in execution (`isExecutionRevert`, `refusal-ledger.ts`):
  - `prepare_reverted` when the redemption reverted in EXECUTION (a decoded
    `Error(string)`, a named caveat-enforcer error, the timestamp caveat's
    text or viem's `EstimateGasExecutionError`): `refusal_reason` (the
    classifier's value), `revert_reason` (the first decoded `Error(string)`,
    else a plain enforcer error — never an AA code, since one makes the
    answer `prepare_failed` — printable ASCII cut at 120 characters plus an
    ellipsis; `revertReasonOf`) and a remedy
    `message`. Booked in the ledger exactly as before.
  - `prepare_failed` otherwise: a failure that is not a revert (bundler, RPC,
    transport; unbooked, as before); an ERC-4337 validation failure the
    bundler words as a revert — an `AA` code such as AA25 (a nonce race) or
    AA31/AA33 (the sponsoring paymaster) — which can clear on its own; or a
    classified revert with no nameable execution cause (a custom-error
    selector, a Panic, a bare "execution reverted"). The hosted step keeps
    "retry once"; a classified one is still booked as the classifier says
    (usually `onchain_revert`), as before. Telling an agent to stop there would be false: review finding,
    probed against viem's real error wrapping.

  Both keep HTTP 502 and carry `details` bounded at 300 characters after
  redaction, built from a viem error's short message and cause rather than its
  `.message` — viem puts the cause AFTER kilobytes of request arguments, so a
  plain cut would have kept the callData and lost the cause. The whole
  redacted error is logged server-side on both prepare paths. It is the bound
  #3494 gave the sign route (`boundedErrorDetails`). Every other response
  `details` built from a caught error now goes through it too:
  - redacted but unbounded before: the erc7710 settlement delegation build and
    delegate deploy (`delegation-authorize.ts`), the settle payload assembly
    (`modules/x402/settle.ts`), the budget account deploy
    (`routes/agent-delegations.ts`), and six local `safeDetails` helpers
    (`routes/agent-rekey.ts`, `task-budgets.ts`, `sub-budgets.ts`,
    `agent-delegations.ts`, `rails/hybrid-transfers.ts`,
    `rails/hybrid-signer-actions.ts`);
  - **not redacted at all** before — an RPC or bundler URL carrying its API key
    could ride the response: the delegate sweep (`modules/mpp/sweep.ts`, four
    answers), the sub-budget routes (`routes/agent-sub-budgets.ts`, three
    answers plus its `safeDetails` helper) and the account address derivation
    (`routes/hybrid-accounts.ts`).

  A source-scan guard (`packages/backend/src/__tests__/no-raw-error-details.test.ts`)
  refuses a `details` built from a raw error, a `…Details` helper that returns
  one unbounded, and a `details` fed from a variable holding one.

  The hosted MCP (`packages/mcp-server/src/tools/support/errors.ts`) maps
  `prepare_reverted` to `PREPARE_REVERTED` with a `stop_and_tell_user` step and
  the two reason fields; `prepare_failed` keeps the generic retry-once step.

  **No custody or authority change:** the same failures, answered with less of
  the error and a correct next step. Nothing is signed, built or moved on any
  of these paths; no key, signature, delegation, caveat, budget or recipient pin
  changes, the ledger books the same rows, and the on-chain enforcer is still
  the gate. Responses now carry strictly less chain and request data than
  before, and the eight previously unredacted answers (plus the sub-budget
  helper's callers) can no longer carry a vendor key.
