# 2026-10-04 — #3618: POST /payments adopts the budget-scope resolver (epic #3615 S-C)

What changed: `packages/backend/src/routes/payments.ts` stopped hand-rolling the
authorize-time budget decision and now asks the #3616 budget-scope module
(`resolveBudgetScope`, `evaluatePeriodPrecheck`, the refusal-body/ledger-detail
builders, `refuseBothScopeIds`): the inline task-budget resolution, the inline
sub-budget resolution, the duplicated refusal status/message tables, the
period pre-check's per-link min-of-reads, the direct 403 body and both
payment_refusals ledger `detail` objects moved into the module, and the route
hands the resolved scope (including the none-scope delegation row) to
`prepareDelegationPayment`, so the row read is still the row redeemed. The
revert classification in the sign route is deliberately untouched and reads
nothing from the resolver — its task-cap confirmation still re-reads the task
budget row and its period confirmation still reads
`selectActiveDelegationByHash(agent.id, intent.budget_delegation_hash)`
(byte-identical region, proven against the pre-adoption file); its 502 body
keeps its own literal, the one remaining `delegation_budget_exceeded` literal
in the file. The refusal census was re-pinned to the moved `refuse(` lines
(same 15 sites, same statuses, same ledger/no-writer split; TARGET_FILES
unchanged because no `refuse(` call left the route).

Authority/custody argument: nothing about who decides or who can spend moved.
The pre-check remains exactly what the CASP pattern requires — an optional
fail-open mirror that may only NARROW what Haven constructs (per-link fail
open, `<` not `<=`, no measurement → no refusal), while the caveat enforcers
stay the only gate on any redemption; an over-budget payment still reverts
on-chain, never queues. Haven still holds no key, signs nothing, and the
refusal ledger remains a fire-and-forget record written strictly after the
decision, with the same bounded `detail` subset (now produced by the module's
builders, table-tested field-for-field against the pre-adoption bodies — the
hosted MCP's #3504 parsing surface is unchanged byte for byte, proven by a
9-case pre/post harness over the verbatim closures plus the route suites'
existing byte pins). The resolver consolidates reads and classification; it
introduces no new authority, no credential path, no custody, and no
discretionary decision (Red Lines 2/3/4 and the mirror-only pattern
unaffected). Mutation-proven after adoption: the scan's M3
(min-of-links → last link) reddens `payments-sub-budget.test.ts` 3/16 through
the resolver path, with a sha-verified byte-identical restore.

Perimeter unchanged.
