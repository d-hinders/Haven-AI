# 2026-10-04 — #3619: one settled-replay rule for the budget decision; pre-check 403 through the #3616 builder (epic #3615 S-D)

What changed: `packages/backend/src/modules/mpp/budget-precheck.ts` no longer
hand-builds the `delegation_budget_exceeded` 403 body or its
`payment_refusals` ledger `detail` — both come from the #3616 budget-scope
module (`buildPeriodExceededBody` flavor `mpp`, `periodExceededLedgerDetail`),
equal field for field to the hand-built body for the same inputs (the hosted
MCP parses this body, #3504, so the equality is wire; the pre-#3616 body is
preserved in the module's characterization table). The settled-replay
predicate `isSettledX402Replay` keeps every guard it had: `confirmed` +
`tx_hash`, the `erc7710`/`eip3009` scheme set, no task-/sub-budget pin on the
row, same payee/resource/token/amount. The documented differences between the
three replay rules (`delegationReplay`, `isSettledX402Replay`,
`findPaymentReplay`) are now pinned cell by cell by
`routes/__tests__/replay-rules-parity.test.ts` — the same seeded rows through
all three (rule 3 via the `POST /payments` route, un-edited) — 12 cells;
mutation-proven: dropping the pin guard reddens CELLs 4/5/12, dropping the
scheme guard reddens CELL 2, sha-verified byte-identical restore.

Authority/custody argument: nothing about who decides or who can spend moved.
The pre-check remains exactly what the CASP pattern requires — an optional
fail-open mirror that may only NARROW what Haven constructs (per-link fail
open, `<` not `<=`, no measurement → no refusal), while the caveat enforcers
stay the only gate on any redemption; an over-budget payment still reverts
on-chain, never queues. The settled-replay short-circuit still only ever
turns a WOULD-BE refusal into a success (a replay of an already-settled
payment is never reported as a refusal — #3492/#3527), so it cannot widen
what Haven spends. Haven still holds no key, signs nothing, and the refusal
ledger remains a fire-and-forget record written strictly after the decision,
with the same bounded `detail` subset (now produced by the module's builder).
The consolidation introduces no new authority, no credential path, no
custody, and no discretionary decision (Red Lines 2/3/4 and the mirror-only
pattern unaffected). `BudgetPrecheckBody` gains no scope field — epic #3615
Note 4 is unsettled, so the criterion is recorded not-in-scope on the issue.

Perimeter unchanged.
