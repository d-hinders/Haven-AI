- **#3671 (epic #3634)**: the Gnosis-only `RPC_URL` variable and `config.rpcUrl` are removed,
  and the known chain shape (`ChainConfig`) no longer carries an RPC URL. Under the owner's
  decision (c) on #3635, Gnosis Chain (100) stays a known, history-only chain with no RPC.

  RPC is now resolved through `rpcUrlForChain(chainId)` in `domain/chains.ts`, which answers
  only for a supported chain (8453, 84532) and throws `Unsupported chain for RPC` for chain 100
  or any unknown id. The ethers relayer provider (`getProvider`) and the viem failover endpoint
  list (`rpcEndpoints`) both go through it, so neither can open a connection to chain 100.
  `GNOSISSCAN_API_KEY` stays, for the chain-100 explorer history read only.

  Perimeter NARROWED: one fewer network on which any RPC read or relayer path can run. No new
  authority, signer or settlement path. Custody unchanged.
