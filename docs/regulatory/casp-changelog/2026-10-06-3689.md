## What changed

`AGENT_COMMAND_MODIFICATION_SENTENCE` (`packages/sdk/src/agent-guidance.ts`)
named two permitted changes to the connector command — appending `--json`, and
one `--runtime` retry after a `runtime_undetermined` refusal — and forbade
everything else. The connector's `wiring_collision` refusal tells an agent to
relay the choice to its user and then "re-run only with the flag your user
chooses" (`--name` to add alongside, `--replace` to replace). The prompt
therefore forbade the very step its own relay sentence set up, and on
2026-10-06 an agent harness blocked the user-chosen `--name` re-run as an
unauthorised change. The sentence now names a third change: **only after the
user has answered a `wiring_collision` relay**, one re-run with the one flag the
user chose, keeping any earlier change; if that re-run is refused, the agent
relays it and stops. `AGENT_NETWORK_ACCESS_SENTENCE` drops "third" from "not a
command modification", and the runbook's "two-changes rule" becomes "the
command-modification rule".

## Why the perimeter is unaffected

- **The decision stays the user's.** `AGENT_WIRING_COLLISION_RELAY_SENTENCE`
  is unchanged and still ends "Never pick for me by adding --replace or --name
  yourself"; the new clause is conditioned on the user's answer to that relay.
  The prompt widens what an agent may *type*, not who decides.
- **No new authority is reachable.** Both flags already existed on the
  connector and were already named in its refusal. The setup token is unused
  after a `wiring_collision`, so the re-run is the same one-time setup, not a
  second agent's authority; budget approval is still human plus WebAuthn, and
  nothing in the re-run signs or approves. `--replace` retires the previous
  agent's local wiring (its on-chain budget is untouched until the user revokes
  it), which is exactly why the flag stays the user's choice.
- **Mutual exclusion is the connector's, not the prompt's.** `--name` and
  `--replace` together are refused by the connector's argument parser
  (`packages/connect/src/args.ts`), so the prompt's "the one flag I chose" is
  backed by a hard refusal.

## Enforcement

`agent-connection-setups.test.ts` pins the new clause literally, on a phrase
only the modification sentence carries ("only after I have answered a
wiring_collision relay — --name …"), so deleting the clause fails even though
the relay sentence still names `wiring_collision` and `--name`; it also pins
"Never pick for me by adding --replace or --name yourself." The generated
copies (`haven guide`'s CLI copy, `/for-agents.md`, the step files) are held by
`npm run lint:runbook-parity`, and the skill bundle by its byte-parity test.

Perimeter unchanged.
