- **#3728 (signer SIWX phase 1)**: `@haven_ai/signer` gains `haven_sign_siwx` —
  a new LOCAL-ONLY signing primitive on the delegate key. The tool answers a
  merchant's x402 Sign-In-With-X challenge (CAIP-122 / EIP-4361) with an
  EIP-191 signature and returns the finished `SIGN-IN-WITH-X` header.

  What it is NOT: no new spend authority, no money movement, no Haven
  involvement. The signer composes the EIP-4361 message itself from
  grammar-validated challenge fields with the delegate address read from the
  key — it never signs a caller-supplied message, hash or byte string, so a
  SIWX signature cannot double as a payment authorization, a UserOp signature
  or any other Haven signature. Refusals (domain ≠ url host, resource-uri
  origin mismatch, non-https url, malformed/missing nonce, expiry beyond the
  5-minute max age plus a pinned 30-second skew tolerance, `version ≠ "1"`,
  credential chain absent from `supportedChains` as `eip191`) are structured,
  produce no signature and write no audit entry. Every signature is audited
  locally with the sha256 of the composed message.

  Accepted residual (owner-confirmed on #3728, 2026-10-07): the signature
  lives ≤ 5 minutes but the session a merchant mints from it lives longer
  (Bitrefill ~2 h) and reaches every past purchase by that EOA at that
  merchant until a rekey; what a session can do is merchant-defined; the
  signer cannot distinguish a server-issued nonce from an invented one; and
  sign-ins appear in the local signer audit only, linking the delegate EOA to
  any https site the agent chooses. The sign-in identity changes on
  credential rotation.

  Perimeter UNCHANGED in substance: the delegate key already signs one EIP-191
  message elsewhere (the connect setup proof), and the signer's tool surface —
  not the key's power — is what widened. Phase 2 (EIP-1271/6492 for the
  delegate account) and treasury-account sign-ins remain out of scope.
