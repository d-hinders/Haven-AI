- **#3815 — per-agent refusal count counts rows, not reasons** — a
  reporting fix; it moves no funds and touches no signing, authority, custody
  or key-management path. Money-path by file
  (`packages/backend/src/infra/repositories/payment-refusals.ts`).
  - **Change:** `aggregateRefusalsForUserByAgent` reported `refusals` as
    `COUNT(*)` over an inner `GROUP BY agent_id, reason`, i.e. the number of
    distinct refusal reasons per agent, while its contract (and the analytics
    "refused" total, the per-agent `refusals` column and the revoked-agent
    visibility filter that read it) means distinct refusal rows. It now sums
    the per-reason row counts. `attempts` and `by_reason` were already right
    and are unchanged. The result order now sorts on the numeric attempt
    sum (it bound to the `::text` output column, so `'9'` sorted above
    `'10'`); no consumer depends on the order.
  - **Perimeter:** unchanged. The aggregate is read only by the analytics
    overview and no payment, pre-check or budget decision reads it.
  - **Verification:** the real-DB test gives one agent two
    `delegation_budget_exceeded` rows on different resources plus one
    `onchain_revert` row and expects `refusals: 3`; it read `2` on the
    previous SQL and passes after the fix (12/12 in the file).
  - **Verdict:** Perimeter unchanged.
