- **#3917** — the accounting feed's receipt underlag (and the Fortnox
  connector's `feedDescription`) rendered `amountAtomic` — 1500 — as if it
  were whole USDC, so Accounted's archive and AI extraction read
  1 500,00 US$ off a 0.0015 USDC payment. The fix is display/evidence only:
  the Amount figure is now the human-decimal token amount
  (`machine_payment_evidence.amount_human`, already the figure the ledger
  and book-value lines are computed from) with the atomic value kept as a
  labelled annotation (`1500 atomic units`), and rows that never captured a
  human amount label the atomic figure explicitly instead of implying a
  scale. No money moves differently: `amountAtomic` itself, the payment
  intents, allowances, x402/MPP authorization plumbing and every signed
  surface are untouched — nothing that reaches a chain, a signer, a Safe,
  or an agent's spending authority changes, and no API contract changes
  (the underlag PDF text and the Fortnox description are the only rendered
  artifacts). Perimeter unchanged.
