# 2026-10-14 — #3925: EIP-3009 merchant-settlement records are nonce-bound, not shape-attributed

**Perimeter: unchanged.** Nothing in this change moves funds, signs anything
on Haven's behalf, or widens any authority. It NARROWS what evidence the
backend accepts before writing a merchant's settlement hash onto a payment
row (`payment_intents.machine_metadata.merchant_settlement_tx_hash`) — a
record that feeds receipts, the Fortnox bookkeeping feed and transaction
history, and that moves no money by itself.

## What changed

`verifySettlementTransferTx` gains check 9: an optional
`expectedAuthorizationUsed: { authorizer, nonce }` on the expected shape. When
present, the receipt must carry the token's own
`AuthorizationUsed(authorizer, nonce)` log — the same attribution proof the
passive #3888 sweep has always been held to — checked BEFORE the transfer
scan, on the chain's own attribution key. The three refusals:

- the token burned a different nonce for this delegate → refused ("the nonce
  does not match the reported authorization"); if the burned nonce equals
  another payment's derived nonce, the refusal names it ("settles another
  payment");
- no `AuthorizationUsed` from the payment's token for this delegate at all →
  refused as not an EIP-3009 settlement (USDC's `transferWithAuthorization` /
  `receiveWithAuthorization` always emit it);
- a report that carries no X-PAYMENT header, or a header whose authorization
  is not signed by the payment's delegate, is refused before the chain is
  consulted.

The two attribution sources, both derived — never agent-chosen: the
agent-reported path reads the nonce from the X-PAYMENT header the SDK
already posts with the evidence (its `authorization` is signer-checked
against `intent.to_address` over the pinned token EIP-712 domain first), and
the passive sweep passes the nonce it derived from the payment id (the very
key `findAuthorizationUsedTx` found the transaction by).

## Why

Before this change the agent-reported path attributed a settlement by
TRANSFER SHA alone (exact delegate → merchant transfer of the payment's
amount inside the funding window). Two same-shaped payments — same agent,
token, delegate, merchant and amount, overlapping in time — could not be told
apart, so a report naming one payment could have its hash recorded on the
other (a hypothesis, quality-scan 2026-10-13 C1; no misattribution on
record). The record itself moves no money, but it decides what receipts and
the accounting feed book. The sweep was already held to the nonce standard;
this holds the reported path to the same standard.

## What does NOT change

- No signature, key role, enforcer, custody or budget path moves. The
  settlement record decides what is BOOKED, not what can be SPENT.
- The trusted set is unchanged: the chain (the receipt's logs) and Haven's
  own payment row. The agent's `payment_id`, `txHash` and X-PAYMENT header
  remain untrusted inputs that only NAME what to verify — the header's
  authorization is checked against the delegate's signature before its nonce
  is used.
- A merchant that never settles still records nothing; the
  `notBefore`/`notAfter` funding window stays the bound on which funding a
  transfer can be matched to; a pre-#3888 random-nonce payment is
  unattributable on both paths (the accepted residual).
- The wire contract is unchanged: refusals keep the 409
  `settlement_unverified` code; only the `reason` sentence is new, and
  clients key on the code, not the text.
- `machine_metadata` gains two audit-only flags beside the hash when the
  proof is recorded (`merchant_settlement_nonce_bound`,
  `merchant_settlement_nonce_derived`); no migration, JSONB.

## Verification

Real-DB suites (`packages/backend`, dedicated test Postgres): the evidence
suite `modules/mpp/__tests__/eip3009-settlement-evidence.test.ts` — 39/39,
including the new nonce-attribution block (misattribution refused against a
same-shaped sibling with the recorded-hash red check run on `641f527d`,
where the same report answers 202; recorded with `nonceBound`; derived-nonce
consistency both ways; wrong-signer, missing-header and no-`AuthorizationUsed`
refusals); the sweep, `x402-authorizations`, `agent-payment-status-grace` and
`accounting/entry` suites green; `tsc --noEmit` green.

**Verdict:** Perimeter unchanged — what narrows is the evidence a settlement
record may be written on, in the direction of strictly more proof.
