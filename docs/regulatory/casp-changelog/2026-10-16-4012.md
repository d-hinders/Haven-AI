# 2026-10-16 — #4012: the x402 settlement sweepers stop re-sending a refused `eth_getLogs`

**Perimeter: unchanged.** Nothing in this change moves funds, signs anything
on Haven's behalf, or widens any authority. It changes only how often and how
widely the two passive settlement sweeps (erc7710, #2117, and eip3009, #3888)
READ the chain. Those sweeps exist to attach accounting evidence (a merchant
settlement hash, a `machine_payment_evidence` row) to payments that already
settled. Neither sweep can move money, and this change adds no write.

## What changed

- **A failed read backs off.** Before, a candidate whose own chain read failed
  (log scan; on eip3009 also the funding receipt or `authorizationState`) was
  retried every two minutes until its 24-hour horizon. On Alchemy's free plan
  every 500-block `eth_getLogs` is refused, so that came to ~245k compute units
  an hour (the Alchemy dashboard's figure, 2026-10-16) against a monthly pool
  prod shares. Now it backs off on a growing
  schedule capped at an hour, in a map kept apart from the fruitless-scan
  backoff. The rule that an outage can neither confirm nor reject anything is
  unchanged. A failed read still writes nothing, judges nothing, does not
  advance the eip3009 cursor and never reaches the `unresolved` alert.
- **The batch span is the provider's.** `infra/chain/log-range-limit.ts`
  narrows the 500-block default by `ETH_GETLOGS_MAX_BLOCK_RANGE` and by the
  limit a range refusal names (learned per chain, expiring after an hour). The
  number of calls stays bounded: 20 per chain per tick on both sweeps. A window
  that cannot fit that budget is backed off rather than walked.
- **A truncated scan is "could not ask".** Both scanners now return `null`
  when their batch budget ends before the requested range does, instead of a
  partial index read as complete.

## Why the perimeter is unaffected

The attribution rules, the verifier (`verifySettlementTransferTx`), the
guarded compare-and-set writers and the precedence rules are untouched. A
backoff can only delay a record, never create or change one. The accepted
residual is narrower recovery, not wrong evidence. On a 10-block provider cap
the erc7710 sweep scans nothing, because no window fits 20 × 10 blocks. Those
payments stay exactly where #2096 leaves an unreported payment, recoverable
through the agent-reported path. Tested by
`modules/x402/__tests__/sweeper-getlogs-backoff.test.ts` and
`infra/chain/__tests__/log-range-limit.test.ts`, plus the rewritten AC 5 case
in the real-DB eip3009 suite. Perimeter unchanged.
