# 2026-10-19 — #4021: the account card's "Last payment" field

**Perimeter: unchanged.** This is presentation of balances and navigation on
`/accounts` (epic #4022's slice S5), plus one additive, read-only wire field.
It adds no route, no signer path, no agent-authority surface and no money
movement: nothing is grantable that was not grantable before.

## What changed

- **`infra/repositories/dashboard.ts` gained one READ beside its reads**
  (`LAST_OUTGOING_INTENT_SQL` / `findLastOutgoingIntentAt`): `MAX(pi.confirmed_at)`
  over confirmed payment intents joined to ONE account via
  `agents.account_id = $1`, `pi.status = 'confirmed'`. Same row set the
  `AGENT_LAST_PAYMENT_SQL` family already reads, aggregated to a single
  timestamp; no new column, no write, no scope widening — the account id comes
  from the route's own ownership check, never from the wire.
- **`modules/transactions/last-outgoing.ts` (new)** combines that read with
  the EXISTING chain-history pipeline (`fetchAccountTransactions`, the same
  cached read `GET /transactions` serves) to answer "when did this account
  last send anything" — owner sends included, per the owner ruling recorded
  on the issue. It filters the pipeline's own rows (`direction: 'out'`,
  not reverted, value-bearing), takes the max against the intent leg, caches
  the answer for five minutes per (chain, address), and refuses to cache a
  result computed from a failed chain read. It spends nothing, signs nothing
  and exposes nothing but an ISO timestamp.
- **`routes/portfolio.ts`** attaches the timestamp to the portfolio response
  it ALREADY served (`lastOutgoingAt`, additive; `PortfolioResponse` in the
  OpenAPI spec updated to match). The dashboard aggregate is untouched.
- **The frontend** renders it as the account card footer's "Last payment
  {relative}" / "No payments yet", and slims the card (zero-raw-balance rows
  hidden, single-holding cards show no rows, "Test network" chip on testnets
  only). Presentation only; no balance, budget or authority figure moves.

## Why the perimeter is unaffected

No key material is created, read, moved or verified differently; the JWT
session auth on the portfolio route is unchanged, and the new read is scoped
to the caller's own account by the ownership check that already gated the
route. A session holder learns the same class of fact the transactions page
already shows them about their own accounts — when their own account last
sent — with no counterparty detail, no recipient address and no amount on
the overview card. The intent leg reads only rows the agent-payment queries
already served; the chain leg only re-shapes rows the transactions feed
already served. No agent's authority, budget or delegation surface changes.

Tested by `modules/transactions/__tests__/last-outgoing.test.ts` (incoming
never counts, reverted and zero-value rows skipped, max of the two legs,
uncertain results not cached), `routes/__tests__/portfolio.test.ts` (the
additive field on every 200, ownership-scoped probe call), the real-DB
repository suites, and the frontend card tests
(`AccountsOverviewClient*.test.tsx`, `AccountDetailClient*.test.tsx`).
Perimeter unchanged.
