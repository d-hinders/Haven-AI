- **#4244**: SDK merchant transport only. Under a merchant-egress policy
  (the hosted path), `McpMerchantTransport.fetchUnderPolicy` follows GET
  redirects manually and sent the original request init on every hop. A
  header supplied for one origin, such as `Authorization`, `Cookie`, an API
  key or a payment header, therefore reached whatever origin the redirect
  named. A hop that changes origin (scheme, host or port, compared hop to
  hop) now carries only `accept`, `accept-language`, `user-agent` and
  `x402-wallet`, a public address the SDK adds itself. Dropped headers are
  not restored on a later hop. Five cases in
  `packages/sdk/src/merchant-egress.test.ts` pin it, one of them a
  same-origin characterization. Five mutations (inverted origin compare,
  no reduction, keep-all, hostname instead of origin, original init on the
  next hop) each redden at least one case.

  Where a merchant request may connect is untouched: `assertUrl` on every
  hop, the 3-hop budget, the non-GET refusal and the byte and time budgets.
  The no-policy SDK path is unchanged. No custody, signing, settlement,
  funding, budget or refusal decision changes, and `globs` and
  `controlGlobs` are not touched. Perimeter unchanged.
