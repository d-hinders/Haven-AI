- **#4257**: SDK merchant transport only. Without a merchant-egress policy
  (the SDK default, the local MCP, connect and embedders),
  `McpMerchantTransport.fetch` left redirects to the platform `fetch`, which
  drops only `Authorization` and `Cookie` on a cross-origin hop. An API key,
  an x402 payment header (`PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` / `X-PAYMENT`) and any other
  caller header therefore reached whatever origin a merchant redirected to,
  and a 307/308 also re-sent a POST body there, including an MCP
  `tools/call` body that carries the payment in `_meta["x402/payment"]`.

  Both paths now share one follower. A hop that changes origin (scheme, host
  or port, compared hop to hop) carries only the #4244 allow-list (`accept`,
  `accept-language`, `user-agent`, `x402-wallet`), and a hop that would
  re-send a body to another origin is refused mid-flight
  (`redirect_cross_origin_body`). Otherwise the no-policy path keeps the
  platform's rules: 20 hops, the Fetch spec's 301/302/303 method and body
  rewrite, a 3xx without `Location` returned as the answer, and a caller's
  `redirect: 'manual'` / `'error'` passed straight through.

  Tests: twelve cases in `mcp-merchant-transport.redirect.test.ts` run
  against two real loopback servers, seven of them characterizing the
  platform's behaviour before the change. Six no-policy cases sit in
  `merchant-egress.test.ts`. Twelve mutations are each caught: following
  delegated back to the platform, the body refusal inverted or removed, the
  method/body rewrite skipped, the 303 branch, the body headers kept, the
  hop budget off by one, the no-Location answer, the `'manual'`
  pass-through, the header reduction removed, host instead of origin, and
  the policy path's non-GET refusal on the shared loop.

  The policy path is unchanged in behaviour (`assertUrl` on every hop, the
  3-hop budget, the non-GET refusal, byte and time budgets). The change only
  narrows which headers and bodies leave for a redirect target. No custody,
  signing, settlement, funding, budget or refusal decision about a payment
  changes, and `globs` and `controlGlobs` are not touched. Perimeter
  unchanged.
