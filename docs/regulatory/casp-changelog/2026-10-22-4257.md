- **#4257**: SDK merchant transport, the local paid completion, and the local
  MCP's error envelope. **Money-path.** `money-path-classify.mjs` at head
  puts 1 of 14 files on the perimeter (runtime glob
  `packages/sdk/src/merchant-completion.ts`).

  Without a merchant-egress policy (the SDK default, the local MCP, connect and
  embedders), `McpMerchantTransport.fetch` left redirects to the platform
  `fetch`. The platform drops only `Authorization` and `Cookie` on a
  cross-origin hop, so the following reached whatever origin a merchant
  redirected to:
  - an API key;
  - an x402 payment header (`PAYMENT-SIGNATURE` / `X-PAYMENT`);
  - any other caller header;
  - and, on a redirect that keeps the body (a 307/308, or a 301/302 on a
    method other than POST), the re-sent body.

  Both paths now share one follower:
  - **Cross-origin headers.** A hop that changes origin carries only the #4244
    header allow-list.
  - **Paid requests are not re-sent.** Without a policy, a redirect that keeps
    the body (a 307/308, or a 301/302 on a method other than POST) and would
    re-send a paid request to another origin is refused mid-flight
    (`redirect_cross_origin_body`). A paid request is the paid delivery, or a
    body with an x402 payment in `_meta` (a string body, a UTF-8 byte body,
    or a JSON-RPC batch with any element carrying it; an unreadable body is
    not treated as paid). Other requests follow as the
    platform did, with the headers reduced.
  - **Everything else is the platform's rules**: 20 hops; the Fetch spec's
    rewrite (301/302 rewrite only POST, 303 everything but GET/HEAD); a 3xx
    without `Location` returned as the answer; a non-HTTP(S) `Location`
    failing; `redirect: 'manual'` / `'error'` passed through.
  - The policy path is unchanged in behaviour: `assertUrl` on every hop, the
    3-hop budget, the non-GET refusal, byte and time budgets.

  **A new refusal can fire during paid delivery.** It happens after the paid
  request (payment header and body) reached the first URL. It changes no
  custody, signing, funding or budget decision: nothing is signed or moved
  that would not have been. The one money-path decision it adds is how that
  outcome is recorded and reported:
  - `MerchantCompletion.retryRequest` writes the same
    `merchant_retry_rejected_after_payment` reconciliation event a non-2xx
    paid answer writes. It does so for any merchant-egress refusal raised
    after the paid request was sent:
    - `redirect_cross_origin_body`, without a policy;
    - `redirect_on_non_get`, `redirect_missing_location`,
      `redirect_invalid_location` and `redirect_budget_exceeded`, or a hop that
      the policy's `assertUrl` refused, for an embedder that sets a policy on
      the local completion.

    The hosted completion is a separate path and is unchanged.

    `retry_status` is the redirect status and `retry_body` is `null`.
    `details` add `refusal` and exactly one of:
    - `redirect_location` (the redirect target), for
      `redirect_cross_origin_body`, `redirect_budget_exceeded` and a hop
      refused by `assertUrl`;
    - `refused_url` (the URL that answered with the redirect), for
      `redirect_on_non_get`, `redirect_missing_location` and
      `redirect_invalid_location`, where no target was followed or named.
  - It re-throws the refusal with the `payment_id` and settlement scheme.
  - The local MCP answers with the hosted #1300 fields:
    `merchant_unresponsive_after_funding`; `funded_but_unsettled` and
    verify-then-sweep on EIP-3009, or `not_delivered` and check-status-later
    on erc7710; `haven_get_payment_status` as the next tool; and "do not pay
    again".
  - Before this change, the platform re-sent that request, payment header
    included, to the other origin.

  `globs` and `controlGlobs` are not touched. Perimeter unchanged.

  Tests:
  - `mcp-merchant-transport.redirect.test.ts`, against two real loopback
    servers: characterization of the platform's behaviour, the defect
    reproduced, and the method-rewrite cases.
  - `merchant-egress.test.ts`: mock no-policy cases and the policy branches
    of the shared loop.
  - `merchant-completion.redirect-refused.test.ts`: the record and the
    re-throw, for both schemes.
  - `packages/mcp/src/paid-redirect-refused.test.ts`: the local envelope.

  Every guard is mutation-proven; the cells are listed in the PR.
