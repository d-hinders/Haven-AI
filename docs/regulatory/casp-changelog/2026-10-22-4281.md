- **#4281** — the catalogue's outbound probes go through the SSRF guard.
  Since #4245, `merchant_catalog` is not operator-only. Where
  `CATALOG_DISCOVERY_ENABLED=true` (default false), the discovery cron inserts
  URLs that sellers listed in the external x402 Bazaar index. Two paths still
  probed those URLs with the process-global `fetch`, outside the
  resolved-address check that the ingestion probe (#1713) has always required:
  the discovery probe of each candidate, and the hourly refresh of every
  non-delisted row.
  What changed:
  - `packages/backend/src/infra/http/ssrf-guard.ts` gains `safeRequest`. It
    sends GET, HEAD, POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE through the existing guarded
    loop: https on the default port; no IP literals, embedded credentials or
    locally-bound names; resolution-time address classification; a socket
    pinned to the checked address; a total deadline; a byte cap; and at most
    3 re-validated redirect hops. It returns any status, because a 402 is
    the answer a probe wants. It builds the request headers itself, and the
    caller chooses only `accept`. A GET or HEAD carries no body. A redirect
    on POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE is refused. Any method other than those six
    is refused before a host is resolved or contacted.
  - `packages/backend/src/modules/catalog/merchant-catalog.ts` adds
    `createGuardedCatalogFetch`, a fetch-shaped adapter over `safeRequest`. It
    refuses a `Request` input and a non-string body. `guardedCatalogFetch`
    (15 s total deadline, 256 KiB) is now the default transport of
    `probeCatalogEntry` and `refreshCatalog` for every row, whatever its
    `source`. The 15 s covers the cold start of a sleeping merchant: the dev
    Demo-merchant measured 3.8–5.0 s on 2026-10-22, and the hourly refresh is
    what wakes it. A refusal is a failed probe. It writes no `pay_to` and
    counts a miss, as a probe that got no 402 already did.
  - `packages/backend/src/modules/catalog/catalog-discovery.ts` probes
    candidates through a separate `probeFetch`. Its default is
    `guardedDiscoveryProbeFetch`: 5 s total, the ingestion probe's budget,
    and 256 KiB. The caller's `fetchImpl` now reads only the
    operator-configured Bazaar index.
  - Comments in `modules/catalog/probe.ts`, `platform/leader-lock.ts` and the
    guard's header state the rule that now holds.
  - Docs: `catalog-ingestion.md` gains the rule. `railway-services.md` keeps
    its claim that a cold start alone does not fail the hourly probe.
    `dev-environment.md` and `delegation-rail-security-model.md` gain scoped
    re-verification notes.
  Unchanged:
  - The request shape: the MCP `tools/call` POST, and each row's declared
    method and example body.
  - What a successful probe records: `pay_to`, `asset_transfer_methods`,
    `http_method` and prices.
  - Every catalogue row seeded by migrations is an https URL on a public host.
  A probe that the bare `fetch` sent now fails whenever the guard refuses it.
  Some causes already failed it before: no 402, DNS and transport errors,
  and a redirect without a `Location`. This is the complete set of new
  causes:
  - a scheme other than https, or a port other than the default;
  - an IP-literal host, a locally-bound name, or embedded credentials;
  - a host that resolves to no public unicast address;
  - a declared method other than GET, HEAD, POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE;
  - any redirect on POST, PUT, PATCH or DELETE;
  - more than 3 redirect hops on a GET or HEAD;
  - a hop that breaks any rule above;
  - a response over 256 KiB;
  - no complete response within 15 s for a refresh, or within 5 s for a
    discovery candidate.
  A miss on a refreshed row adds to its failure streak. A discovery
  candidate is skipped.
  Tests: two new files, `modules/catalog/__tests__/catalog-probe-ssrf.test.ts`
  and `catalog-probe-budgets.test.ts`, plus new `safeRequest` cases in
  `infra/http/__tests__/ssrf-guard.test.ts`. The discovery and migration-117
  tests pass their fetch mock as `probeFetch`, because they test parsing and
  inserts, not transport. On `origin/dev` at `a7a1f96da`, with the global
  `fetch` stubbed to answer a valid 402, the three default-transport cases
  were red.
  Mutation-tested with `scripts/ci/prove-red.mjs`, author and reviewer
  cells. Each of these reddens a test:
  - reverting any default transport to the global `fetch`;
  - sending only operator rows to the global `fetch`;
  - probing a discovery candidate with the index fetch;
  - lowering the refresh budget to 5 s, or giving discovery the refresh
    transport;
  - refusing a 402;
  - following a redirect on PUT, PATCH or DELETE;
  - dropping the method allowlist;
  - letting a GET or a HEAD send a body;
  - dropping the CR/LF `accept` check;
  - keeping link-local addresses;
  - dropping the adapter's guard options;
  - turning a refusal into a response;
  - letting a `Request` or a non-string body through.
  The money-path classifier matches `packages/backend/src/db/migrations/**`
  on `__tests__/117_merchant_catalog_source.test.ts` only, which is a
  test-file edit. Every other changed file is off the perimeter. No custody,
  signing, settlement, funding or budget decision changes, and `globs` and
  `controlGlobs` are not touched. Perimeter unchanged.
