- **0.11.0-alpha.0 release: `0.10.0-alpha.0` → `0.11.0-alpha.0`, a MINOR bump.**
  - The published packages gain features and fixes: redirects that keep credentials and paid requests at their origin, settlement reports that carry the payment header, a quote-time budget and price stop, a narrower signer that builds an EIP-3009 header only for a funding leg, and per-chain RPC reads in the local MCP.
  - Nothing in the range removes a published surface: the `.d.ts` comparison against `0.10.0-alpha.0` (`sdk`, `signer`, `mcp`) removes and adds no declaration name. No CHANGELOG entry is marked **Update required**.
  - Three type-level widenings: `HavenCatalogEntry.source` gains `'bazaar'` (#4245), `MerchantEgressRefusalReason` gains `'redirect_cross_origin_body'` (#4257), and `AgentPaymentWarningCode` gains two codes (#3967). Only an exhaustive `switch` over one of them needs the new case.
  - Each merged change carries its own shard; this entry records what the release bundles.

  **What the release carries into published packages, by effect on authority:**
  - **#4242 (#4247), #4248 (#4256): the signer builds an EIP-3009 header only from a funding-leg binding.** A settlement-child binding is refused (`NO_FUNDING_LEG`) and retired at mint. Narrows what the delegate key signs; recorded in `delegation-rail-security-model.md` where it merged and re-read at this release.
  - **#4244 (#4255), #4257 (#4269): redirects keep credentials and payment headers at their origin.** A cross-origin hop carries only an allow-list of headers, and a paid request is never re-sent to another origin; a refused paid delivery reports the funded, undelivered state. Narrows egress; no authority change.
  - **#4061 (#4075), #4215 (#4222): settlement reports carry the payment header**, and the local report is retried while Haven answers `retryable`. Evidence only: it moves no funds.
  - **#4120 (#4153), #4062 (#4074): one post-funding merchant-answer decision**, and a plain tool error after funding is a rejection, never `settled: true`. Classification of an outcome; no authority change.
  - **#3967 (#3972): quote-time budget and price stops.** The hosted quote warns when the amount exceeds the remaining budget or differs from `expected_amount`; the local `haven_quote_x402` checks the price only. Both say stop. Advisory; the on-chain policy stays the gate.
  - **#3933 (#3970): CLI `budget grant` refuses an occupied slot** instead of silently replacing a live budget, which would have left the old delegation enabled. Narrows.
  - **#3996 (#4000): `HAVEN_RPC_URL_<chainId>`** lets the local MCP read a delegate balance on an idempotency collision. Read-only; opt-in.
  - **#3922, #3923, #4245, #4079, #4168, #4067:** `is_live` on budget reads, catalog `source: 'bazaar'`, and wording. Read paths and text.
