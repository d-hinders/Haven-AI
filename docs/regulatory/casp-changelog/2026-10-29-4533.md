- **#4533** — the accounting feed gains a second source so a settled **direct payment** (`haven_send`, the delegation-redemption rail behind `POST /payments`) produces an underlag. Before this, the feed enumerated only `machine_payment_evidence`, and a direct payment never gets an evidence row — `recordMachinePaymentEvidenceBase` refuses it on the rail gate (`PROTOCOL_RAILS` excludes `direct`) and, past that, on `resource_url NOT NULL` — so the documented fallback for a broken x402 merchant left no accounting record at all. The change is additive and read-side: a second branch in `ENTRY_SOURCE_SQL` (`modules/accounting/entry.ts`) and in `listUnpushedPaymentIds` (`infra/repositories/accounting-feed-syncs.ts`) that selects a `payment_intents` row with `status = 'confirmed'`, `tx_hash IS NOT NULL`, `execution_rail = 'delegation'` and `COALESCE(payment_rail, source) = 'direct'`; the same branch in `LOAD_RECEIPT_UNDERLAG_SOURCE_SQL` so the connector has a document to attach; and one fire-and-forget `feedSettledPaymentBestEffort(user_id, id)` call after `confirmSubmittedIntent` in `routes/payments.ts`. **No migration, and no change to `machine_payment_evidence`'s schema, rail gate or `resource_url` constraint** — widening that table would have turned every direct send into a "machine payment" on 25 readers. **Authority and custody are untouched:** the feed is non-asserting and never moves money; the new call runs after the on-chain settlement is already confirmed and is fire-and-forget, so it cannot block, delay or fail the transfer, and it changes no authorization, budget check or timing. No new spender, no new key custody, no Haven-signed authority, no widening of any delegation. The owner decision (Daniel, 2026-10-29) narrows what is booked: only a payment to an address **outside the user's own Haven footprint** is fed — a transfer to another of the user's smart accounts or to one of their agents' delegate addresses is an internal move and produces no sync row and no document (case-insensitive comparison; an unknown recipient is outside, never silently dropped). The refusals that keep the perimeter hold unchanged: the evidence rail gate and `resource_url NOT NULL` still refuse a direct payment, and the direct branch itself refuses anything not confirmed, not on the delegation rail, or not `direct`. Mutation-proven on real Postgres (`direct-payment-feed.db.test.ts`): an outside direct payment settles → exactly one pushed sync row and one document; firing the hook twice → still one; a payment to the user's own smart account, to their agent's delegate address, and to the own account in lowercase → no sync row and no document; an x402 payment is unchanged (evidence source, resource URL, merchant counterparty); an x402 intent with no evidence row is **not** captured by the direct branch; and a direct payment with no book-time capture is not fed and stays backfillable. Perimeter unchanged.
