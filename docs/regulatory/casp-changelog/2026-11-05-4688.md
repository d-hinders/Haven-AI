- **#4688** — **`haven_settle_mcp_tool` said `settled: true` on the EIP-3009 bridge without a verified merchant settlement. Agent guidance only — no signing, custody or authorization behaviour moved.**
  What changed. On the EIP-3009 bridge (funding leg), the hosted settle tool
  answered `settled: true`, `next_action: none` and "Funding and merchant
  settlement both succeeded." for every non-error merchant 2xx, whatever the
  settlement-evidence report said — `confirmed`, `retryable`, `refused`, or
  nothing at all because the merchant gave no settlement hash or a zero one.
  A merchant that verified the payment but never settled it left the funded
  USDC on the delegate EOA while the agent was told the purchase settled.
  `settled` is now `settlement_evidence_outcome?.outcome === 'confirmed'`, the
  rule the tool's own contract already stated. The unverified arms
  (`retryable` → `SETTLEMENT_PENDING`; `refused` or nothing posted →
  `DELIVERED_UNSETTLED`) carry `settled: false`, `delivered: true`, a
  `SETTLEMENT_UNCONFIRMED` warning, `settlement_tx_hash: null`, the allowance
  block, and no `purchase_summary` (its published `status` is `'settled'`
  only). `haven_complete_mcp_tool` answers the same unverified shape, and its
  confirmed arm now carries `settled: true` (additive). The unverified reason
  sends the agent to `haven_get_payment_status` after the #3888 sweeper's
  window and names `haven_sweep_delegate` only if
  `merchant_settlement_recorded` is still not true then.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected. No signer, signature, delegation, route,
  schema, allowance or on-chain redemption path changes: the funding
  signature, the X-PAYMENT header, the delegate allowance and the merchant's
  single-use authorization are byte-identical. The change narrows what the
  tool CLAIMS — it can no longer report a settlement Haven did not verify —
  and never widens what an agent may sign or move. The sweep it names is the
  existing verify-then-sweep path, gated on the status read, not a new
  authority. The published `AgentPurchaseSummary.status: 'settled'` type is
  not widened; an unverified arm simply omits `purchase_summary`.

  Perimeter unchanged.
