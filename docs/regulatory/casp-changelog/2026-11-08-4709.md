- **#4709** — "Since your last visit": one line under the dashboard heading,
  only when something happened since the previous entry. The marker is three
  new NULLABLE `users` columns (migration 124: `visit_current_at`,
  `visit_previous_at`, `visit_entry_id`), written by ONE atomic
  `UPDATE … RETURNING` under the row lock (`POST /user/visits`), so a phone and
  a laptop agree and two near-simultaneous entries get consecutive windows
  that do not overlap. The figures come from `GET /dashboard/since` over
  `(previous, current]` — the user's confirmed agent payments, grouped by
  agent, on #3803's NET definition with the #3755 netting and the same
  `spend.scope` as the 7-day figures, in the display currency. **The marker is
  never read by a payment path.** It records when a person opened the
  dashboard; it changes no balance, budget, delegation, allowance, signer,
  credential or settlement fact, and it grants no authority. Both routes are
  authenticated and tenant-scoped by the JWT subject; the new statement takes
  the existing `DELEGATION_RAIL_JOIN` and `spendNetFactorSql` fragments rather
  than a fourth copy of either. The sentence is assembled from fixed templates
  in `lib/dashboard-summary.ts` (never model-written), names at most two
  agents, reads a sub-cent total as a count, and never names a raw address.
  Residual, disclosed: a retry that arrives after ANOTHER device opened a new
  entry is treated as a new entry, so the lost window's line is not shown —
  nothing is counted twice. Perimeter unchanged.
