- **#4850** — the demo merchant's receiving address and settlement kind
  become properties of a product instead of one merchant-wide value. A
  refactor with no behaviour change, so that a later product (#4851) can be
  verified and settled differently from the existing seven.

  What changed (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/`):
  - **`products.ts`.** A product may carry `receivingAddress` (absent: the
    merchant's `MERCHANT_ADDRESS`) and `settlement` (absent: `self`, the only
    kind — the merchant verifies and settles with its own gas-funded key).
    Both are read through one resolver each, `productReceivingAddress` and
    `productSettlementKind`. No product sets either field.
  - **`http.ts`, `server.ts`.** The 402 challenge's `payTo`, the address the
    payment is verified against (`verifyAndSettle`, hence the EIP-3009 `to`
    check, the quoted-option match and the ERC-7710 transfer target), the
    in-band refusal's payment address and the invoice's recipient address are
    all resolved from the purchased product.
  - **`invoice.ts`.** The seller block's `crypto_address` is passed in per
    invoice instead of read from `process.env.MERCHANT_ADDRESS` at import. The
    keys Haven's accounting reads (`name`, `org_nr`, `moms_nr`) are unchanged.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - The demo merchant sells only its own demo products; no third-party funds
    are received or moved, before or after. With no product naming its own
    address, every resolved address is `MERCHANT_ADDRESS`, exactly as before.
  - No verification step is removed or loosened: the same comparisons run
    against the same value. A product with its own address is held to that
    address by the same checks. On EIP-3009 that is test-pinned: a payment
    made out to `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` for it is refused. On ERC-7710 it holds by
    threading, not by a test: the option match and the redeem call's transfer
    target read the same per-purchase value `verifyAndSettle` receives.
  - No key, route, scope or settlement path changes; `self` is the only
    settlement kind and the only one implemented.

  Tests: `packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/product-receiving-address.test.ts`.
  Its characterization half was committed before the refactor and snapshots
  `/healthz`, the discovery document, `list_products`, every product's 402 and
  every in-band refusal; the snapshot is unchanged by the refactor. Its second
  half (run with `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` set before the merchant modules load)
  builds a product with a different receiving address and pins that the
  EIP-3009 402, the verification, the settlement call, both invoice paths and
  the in-band refusal use it, and that a payment to `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` for it
  is refused at the option match and at the signed `to` check. Mutation cells,
  each going red in that file: the 402, the `verifyAndSettle` address and the
  `x-receipt-json` invoice each built from the merchant-wide address
  (`http.ts`); the refusal text, the in-band requirements and the handler's
  own invoice each built from it (`server.ts`); the resolver ignoring the
  product's field (`products.ts`); the signed `to` check removed (`x402.ts`);
  and `invoice.ts` reading `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` again ahead of the product's
  address.

  Perimeter unchanged.
