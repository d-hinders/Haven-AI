- **#4851** — the demo merchant gains one product, a joke for 0.01 USDC
  (10,000 atomic), that offers EIP-3009 only and is verified and settled by
  Coinbase's hosted x402 facilitator (CDP) instead of the merchant's own key.
  It is offered only when `CDP_API_KEY_ID` and `CDP_API_KEY_SECRET` are both
  set. The seven existing products, `vpn_legacy` included, are unchanged.

  What changed (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/`):
  - **`src/products.ts`.** A second settlement kind, `cdp`, and the `joke`
    product: receiving address `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` (the #4850 default),
    EIP-3009 only, one-time. `offeredProducts` drops a `cdp` product when CDP
    is not configured.
  - **`src/x402.ts`.** For a `cdp` product the processor first runs the
    merchant's own checks: the extensions echo, the x402 version, the quoted
    option, the resource, the recipient, the amount, the signature and the
    validity window. Only then does it call CDP's `verify` and `settle`.
    - A refusal at verification, which includes CDP's sanctions and risk
      screening, is final. It carries `reason_code: "facilitator_refused"` and
      nothing is delivered.
    - After any settle failure or timeout, the merchant reads USDC's
      `authorizationState` itself and matches no facilitator error string:
      - Unused: a retryable refusal (`"facilitator_retryable"`).
      - Used: USDC's `AuthorizationUsed` event for this payer and nonce,
        searched inside the authorization's own validity window, decides. Found
        and paying this merchant the price: served, with that hash. Found
        paying anything else, or the whole window empty (a cancellation, or the
        nonce spent by another authorization): refused as final. When the
        search fails or the window is too long to search in full: served, with
        the reference marked unavailable.
    - A retry reads the chain before calling CDP, so a header whose earlier
      settle landed is served without a second settlement, even after
      `validBefore`.
  - **`src/cdp.ts`** (new): the CDP client (`createFacilitatorConfig` from
    `@coinbase/x402` inside `HTTPFacilitatorClient` from `@x402/core/server`,
    loaded lazily), a 30 s bound on each facilitator call, the chain reader
    (`authorizationState`, and an `eth_getLogs` search for this payer's
    `AuthorizationUsed` with the authorizer and the lowercased nonce encoded as
    topics, in chunks of at most 500 blocks over the signed window, at most
    about 12 h of it),
    and credential resolution: both values set or CDP is off, and a half-set
    pair warns and never stops the merchant from starting.
  - **`src/http.ts`, `src/server.ts`, `src/index.ts`, `src/invoice.ts`.**
    - The product appears in `list_products`, the discovery document and the
      `buy_joke` tool only when CDP is configured. Without CDP, every one of
      those, and `/healthz`, is byte-identical to before (#4850's snapshot).
    - `/healthz` reports a `cdp` block naming no credential.
    - The own-key readiness gate does not refuse the CDP product, because CDP
      pays its gas.
    - The invoice marks a one-time purchase.
  - **`scripts/start-smoke.mjs`** runs the built service with a throwaway CDP
    pair. It asserts that CDP is reported and that the secret appears in
    neither the startup output nor `/healthz`.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - The demo merchant still sells only its own demo products. The joke's
    payment goes from the buyer straight to `MERCHANT_ADDRESS`, the merchant's
    own wallet, in one EIP-3009 transfer. Haven receives and moves no
    third-party funds, holds no key for the buyer, and is not a party to the
    settlement. CDP submits the transfer the buyer signed and pays its gas.
  - No buyer-side authority changes. The agent signs the same standard
    EIP-3009 authorization it signs for `vpn_legacy`. The budget, recipient
    and expiry are enforced exactly as before, and a recipient-pinned budget
    cannot pay this product, as with any EIP-3009-only offer.
  - No existing check is loosened. The one widening is deliberate and narrow:
    for a `cdp` product an expired authorization is not refused until the chain
    has been read. A used one is served, because the money moved. An unused one
    is refused as expired, as before, and CDP is not called.
  - Where the bridge funded the agent's delegate and CDP then refuses at
    verification, the balance comes back through the agent-signed
    `haven_sweep_delegate`, as for any merchant rejection after funding
    (`MERCHANT_REJECTED_AFTER_FUNDING`). It is not automatic, and this change
    does not alter that.
  - The CDP secret is read from the environment and passed to the Coinbase
    config constructor only. It is not logged, not reported by `/healthz` and
    not in any test (the smoke check asserts it).

  Tests: `src/cdp-settlement.test.ts` runs against a mocked facilitator and
  chain reader and covers the acceptance criteria case by case;
  `src/cdp-chain-reader.test.ts` runs the chain reader on a real viem client
  over a stub JSON-RPC node, so the `eth_getLogs` topics it pins are the ones a
  node would receive. `products.test.ts`'s "only EIP-3009-only product"
  test was rewritten on purpose to "only self-settled EIP-3009-only product".
  Mutation cells, each going red:
  - the resource pre-check removed (the other pre-checks are the own-key
    path's functions, reused unchanged; a test pins each one refusing before
    CDP is called);
  - the chain read before CDP removed;
  - the expired-unused refusal removed;
  - the expiry deferral flipped;
  - the chain read after a settle failure removed;
  - the not-in-window branch skipped;
  - a verification refusal labelled retryable;
  - a thrown `VerifyError` treated as an outage;
  - the readiness exemption removed;
  - the offer filter removed;
  - the `reason_code` dropped;
  - a recovered hash discarded;
  - one credential enough to enable CDP;
  - the merchant check dropped from the event match;
  - and, by hand, the secret printed in the banner (the smoke check reddens).

  Perimeter unchanged.
