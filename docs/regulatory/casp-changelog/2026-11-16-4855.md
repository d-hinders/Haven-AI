- **#4855** — the demo merchant's receipt shows what the buyer declared and the
  VAT treatment that follows from it. Display only: no payment path, key,
  authority or settlement step changes, and the merchant does not compute,
  assert or file tax.

  What changed (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/`):
  - **`tax-declaration.ts` (new).** Reads the `X-Tax-Declaration` header a paid
    request may carry, and accepts it only when the signature verifies and
    recovers to the payment's payer (`authorization.from`), `principalId` names
    that payer on the payment's chain, `principalAttributionHash` recomputes,
    and `validUntil` is in the future. Every failure — a missing header, a
    malformed payload, a bad signature, a foreign signer, a principal scoped to
    another chain, a stale hash, an expired bound — returns `null` and is
    treated as "no declaration". The function never throws. `vatTreatmentFor`
    maps an accepted declaration to `SE_25` (Sweden, or none) or
    `REVERSE_CHARGE` (another EU country).
  - **`http.ts`.** After settlement, the paid request's header is verified
    against `settled.from` and the merchant's chain, and the accepted
    declaration is passed to the invoice. The header is added to the CORS
    allow-list. Nothing on the payment path reads it: the 402 challenge, the
    verification, the settlement call and the payment response are untouched.
  - **`invoice.ts`.** The receipt JSON gains `momsbehandling` and, only when a
    declaration was accepted, `kopare.deklaration` (`land`, `moms_nr`,
    `status`, `giltig_till`). Under `REVERSE_CHARGE` the full price is the net
    amount with VAT rate 0 and a zero VAT amount; under `SE_25` the amounts are
    exactly as before. Both renders name both VAT numbers and the reverse-charge
    rule. The seller keys Haven's accounting reads (`saljare.name`,
    `saljare.org_nr`, `saljare.moms_nr`) are unchanged, and a receipt without a
    declaration keeps its previous shape apart from `momsbehandling`.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - The declaration is buyer-supplied display data. It cannot move funds,
    change the price, alter the signed payment intent, or gate a purchase: a
    refused declaration leaves the purchase and the receipt as if no header had
    been sent. The merchant applies a display rule; it makes no tax
    determination and files nothing.
  - No key, route, scope, settlement path or authority surface changes. The
    verification is a pure read of a header the buyer already signed with the
    key that signed the payment.
  - The merchant remains a demo seller of its own products; no third-party
    funds are received or moved, before or after.

  Tests: `packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/tax-declaration.test.ts` (acceptance
  and each refusal, isolated so only the intended check can refuse it),
  `invoice.test.ts` (both treatments, the unchanged no-declaration receipt, the
  seller keys) and `http.test.ts` (the header over the real HTTP boundary, both
  locales, and every refusal still settling). Mutation cells, each going red:
  the principal check, the attribution recomputation and the expiry check
  removed (`tax-declaration.ts`); the treatment ignored in the amounts
  (`invoice.ts`); the verified declaration not passed to the receipt
  (`http.ts`).

  Perimeter unchanged.
