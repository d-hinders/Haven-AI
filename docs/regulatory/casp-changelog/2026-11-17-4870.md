- **#4870** — Business details gain three optional fields: a receipt email and
  two structured addresses (billing and shipping). Migration 129 adds
  `receipt_email TEXT NULL`, `billing_address JSONB NULL` and
  `shipping_address JSONB NULL` to `owner_company_details`; `GET` and `PUT
  /user/company-details` carry them, the OpenAPI schemas declare them, and the
  Settings → Business form edits them. `DELETE /user/company-details` removes
  them with the row, in the same statement.
  - **No authority, custody or money movement changes.** The fields are
    descriptive data about the owner, stored on a row the owner already owns
    and can already erase. Nothing here constructs, signs, authorizes or
    settles a payment; no spend picker, budget, delegation or allowance reads
    them. The migration adds nullable columns with no default and no backfill,
    so every existing row stays valid and reads back with the three fields
    `NULL`.
  - **The buyer block is deliberately unchanged.** `parties.buyer` on payment
    evidence (`modules/mpp/evidence.ts`) and on the settled-payment receipt
    (`modules/payments/receipt.ts`) keeps exactly the six fields it had
    before this slice: the new columns are NOT added to
    `OWNER_COMPANY_DETAILS_JOIN_COLUMNS`. Both consumers are pinned with an
    exact `toEqual` on the buyer key set, with a stored email and both
    addresses present — a looser assertion would pass on unchanged code and
    guard nothing.
  - **Nothing is sent to any merchant by this slice.** The fields are stored
    for the merchant-facing answer #4871 builds. The receipt email is
    prefilled from the sign-up email in the form only; the sign-up email is
    never stored as a receipt email until the owner saves, and never used as
    a fallback anywhere else (owner decision 2026-11-17, #4868).
  - **Erasure.** The three fields live on the row `DELETE
    /user/company-details` already removes, so there is no second erasure
    path to forget. A merchant receipt that already shows them is the
    merchant's own bookkeeping record and is not reached by that delete —
    recorded in `docs/product/owner-company-details.md` § *Purpose, retention
    and GDPR basis*.
  - **Mutation-tested:** dropping the three columns from
    `OWNER_COMPANY_DETAILS_JOIN_COLUMNS`'s exclusion (i.e. adding them to the
    fragment) turns the two buyer-block tests red; removing the
    "omitted keeps" merge in `writeCompanyDetails` turns the old-request-shape
    route test red (all three fields are wiped); removing the `null` branch
    turns the clear-one-field test red; removing the email or address
    validation turns the corresponding 400 tests red.

  Perimeter unchanged.
