- **#4875** — The accounting entry reads the product lines and VAT figures a
  merchant states on its own receipt (`receipt-statement.ts`, carried as
  `AccountingEntry.receiptStatement`). **Read-only and reporting only: no
  authority, custody, route, address handling or money movement changes.** The
  receipt is the one already stored in `merchant_receipts`; nothing new is
  accepted from anyone. The amount booked is always the amount Haven settled:
  the stated total is only compared with it, in the same currency and at the
  settled atomic amount's full precision, and the entry says `matches`,
  `differs` or `not_comparable`. The read is strict and
  bounded (at most 50 lines, 300 characters per description with no control,
  bidirectional-override or zero-width characters, non-negative decimal
  strings): any malformed part makes the whole receipt "not stated",
  never a partial list, and it never throws. The receipt is relayed by the
  agent and unsigned by the merchant, so nothing treats the figures as
  verified; how a document labels them is #4876's decision, held on an owner
  question. Nothing new reaches a provider or the underlag in this change.
  The demo merchant changes only a comment and a test that pins the receipt
  keys Haven reads (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/invoice.ts`,
  `invoice.test.ts`); its behaviour is unchanged. Perimeter unchanged.
