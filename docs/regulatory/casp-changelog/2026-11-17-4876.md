- **#4876** — The merchant receipt's stated product lines and VAT are printed
  on Haven's accounting document (the underlag) and in the Fortnox `Comments`
  field, and the accounting feed waits for the receipt before its single push
  (owner decisions of 2026-11-17 on #4874). **No authority, custody, route,
  address handling or money movement changes:** the feed reports a payment
  that already settled; the wait changes only WHEN a report is sent, never
  what moves on-chain.
  - **The wait** holds an eip3009 or erc7710 payment with no merchant receipt
    for `MERCHANT_RECEIPT_WAIT_MIN` (2 minutes), before any claim: no sync row
    is written and no retry attempt is spent. A captured receipt releases it,
    and the retry sweep feeds one whose receipt never arrives. A pushed voucher
    is still never edited (#3767 decision 2); the #4824 first-render pin is
    not reopened, and a pin written before this change renders no receipt
    block, so retries keep their bytes.
  - **What is printed** is labelled "On the merchant's receipt, as reported by
    the agent": the receipt is relayed by the agent and unsigned by the
    merchant. Only the merchant's own VAT treatment is shown; Haven's derived
    `vatTreatment` stays off the document and the feed. Every stated figure
    keeps the receipt's currency and is never converted; the booked amount is
    always the amount Haven settled, and a differing receipt total is flagged,
    not booked.
  - **No VAT row, field or account** reaches Fortnox (`ASSERTING_KEYS` guard
    unchanged): the information is text the accountant reads.
  - **Merchant text** is bounded by #4875's parser (50 lines, 300 characters,
    control, bidirectional and zero-width characters refused) and folded to
    ASCII before it is printed or sent.
  Perimeter unchanged.
