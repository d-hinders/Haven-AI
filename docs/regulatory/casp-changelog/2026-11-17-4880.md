- **#4880** — the demo merchant's Stripe record carries the receipt: a
  description, the receipt number, the VAT treatment and amount and, from an
  accepted buyer declaration, the buyer's declared VAT number and country. The
  receipt number of a payment settled on-chain is now derived from its
  transaction hash. The merchant still sells only its own demo product; no
  third-party funds are received or moved, and Haven is not a party to the
  transfer.

  What changed (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/`):
  - **`invoice.ts`.** `receiptNumberForTransaction`: `FAK-` and the
    transaction hash modulo 10^16 as sixteen digits, so a restart and the
    reconciliation script give one payment one number. A payment with no
    transaction hash keeps the clock-seeded counter. `invoice_id` and
    `fakturanummer` keep their keys; only the format of the number changes.
    The VAT split moves into `vatAmountsAtomic`, which the receipt and the
    Stripe record share.
  - **`stripe.ts`.** The PaymentIntent create sends `description` (product
    name and receipt number) and `receipt_number`, `vat_treatment`,
    `vat_amount` (atomic USDC with the unit), `buyer_vat_nr` and
    `buyer_country` (only from an accepted declaration; absent, never empty,
    otherwise). Values are cut to Stripe's 500-character limit. A sandbox probe
    on 2026-11-17 confirmed `description` is accepted on a
    `transaction_verification` PaymentIntent, and that Stripe creates a
    `receipt_url` on the charge.
  - **`http.ts`.** The receipt is built before the recording, which then
    carries it. Both steps before the recording are synchronous; the recording
    stays fire-and-forget.
  - **`stripe-reconcile.ts`.** Reconciliation sends the description, the
    receipt number and `recorded_by=reconciliation`, and no VAT or buyer keys:
    it has no request, so a reverse-charge sale is never recorded as a 25% sale.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - Recording stays downstream of settlement and fire-and-forget: a Stripe
    refusal caused by a new field is an ordinary recording failure — logged,
    counted, and the goods delivered (test).
  - The Stripe key's use is unchanged: create and read PaymentIntents.
  - The data sent to Stripe is the merchant's own receipt data plus, when the
    buyer chose to declare it, the buyer's company VAT number and country —
    what the buyer already sends this merchant for its receipt (#4855). Nothing
    secret and no personal data beyond those declared company details.
  - The receipt number is display and bookkeeping data; no settlement,
    refusal or authority decision reads it. No key, route, scope or settlement
    step changes.

  Tests: `invoice.test.ts` (the number's format and padding, distinct per
  transaction, the 17-digit OCR still Luhn-valid, the same number for two
  payment objects of one transaction, the counter kept without a hash, the
  shared VAT split), `stripe.test.ts` (the form fields sent without a
  declaration, with a German and with a Swedish declaration, from
  reconciliation, Stripe's key and value limits, nothing new without details),
  `stripe-recording.test.ts` (over HTTP: the PaymentIntent's number is the
  receipt's; a second process and the reconciliation script give the same
  number; a refusal of `description` still delivers), and `http.test.ts` /
  `erc7710.test.ts` (three receipt-number comparisons that compared
  `undefined` with `undefined` under the new format now require a number).
  Mutation cells, each going red: the counter for settled payments; no zero
  padding; recording without the receipt (`http.ts`); buyer keys always sent;
  buyer keys dropped; the VAT amount always `SE_25`; reconciliation unmarked;
  reconciliation claiming `SE_25`; no clipping; reconciliation without
  details.

  Perimeter unchanged.
