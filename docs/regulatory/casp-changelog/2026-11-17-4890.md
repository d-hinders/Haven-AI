- **#4890 (first PR)** — the demo merchant's receipt builder and the buyer's
  tax-declaration check move into a new workspace-private package,
  `@haven_ai/merchant-payments` (`packages/merchant-payments/`), the shared
  payment core the paywall proxy (#4893) will also use. Behaviour is
  unchanged: the demo merchant passes its own seller, product line, network
  name, support contact and VAT rule, and its receipts are byte-identical. The
  second #4890 PR moves the Coinbase facilitator client, the pre-verification
  checks and the chain recovery.

  What changed:
  - **`packages/merchant-payments/src/receipt.ts`** (new; moved from
    `packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/invoice.ts`). `buildReceipt`,
    `renderReceiptText`, `receiptNumberForTransaction`, `vatAmountsAtomic`.
    The issue's seven demo couplings are handled: the product line (name,
    description, price, optional billing period), the chain (as its display
    name), the seller (any further fields carried through in order), the
    support contact and the VAT rule are parameters; the fallback numbering
    for a payment with no transaction hash and the cache of built receipts
    stay in the demo merchant, so the core takes the receipt number as a
    parameter. The clock is a parameter too. The core has no
    built-in seller, product table, chain or support contact and reads no
    environment variable.
  - **`packages/merchant-payments/src/tax-declaration.ts`** (moved whole from
    the demo merchant). `vatTreatmentFor` now takes the VAT rule as a
    parameter; one rule exists, `SE_25_WITH_EU_REVERSE_CHARGE`, the Swedish
    rule the demo merchant applied, and an unknown rule throws rather than
    defaulting.
  - **`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/invoice.ts`** keeps the demo's seller,
    `support@haven.xyz`, its VAT rule, the clock-seeded counter for payments
    with no transaction hash and the per-payment cache, and calls the core.
  - Money-path list: `packages/merchant-payments/src/**` added as a runtime
    glob in `.github/money-path-globs.json`, `.github/labeler.yml` and the
    perimeter reference, and to this doc's `covers:`. The moved code was on
    the list through `packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/**`; the #1892
    accident-of-location rule keeps it there.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - The receipt is display and bookkeeping data downstream of settlement. No
    settlement, refusal or authority decision reads it, and the move changes
    no key, route, scope or settlement step.
  - The declaration check is display only, exactly as before (#4855): a
    missing or refused declaration never gates a purchase.
  - No funds are received or moved by the new package. It holds no key and
    makes no network call.

  Tests: `receipt-fixtures.test.ts` (new, written before the move: the demo
  builder regenerates the backend's two receipt fixtures,
  `demo-merchant-se25.json` and `demo-merchant-reverse-charge.json`, byte for
  byte with the clock injected, and pins the Swedish and English receipt text
  of both whole; the pinned text was checked against the pre-move builder at
  `1acf788ff`), `invoice.test.ts`, `stripe*.test.ts`,
  `http.test.ts` and the rest of the demo suite unchanged and green;
  `receipt.test.ts` (new, in the core: a non-demo seller with no bank
  details, extra seller fields kept in order, a line without a period, the
  given network and support contact printed and Haven's never, sub-cent VAT
  figures, the injected clock); `tax-declaration.test.ts` (moved, plus an
  unknown VAT rule refused). Mutation cells, each going red: the seller name
  changed; the due date moved by a day; one double space in the Swedish text
  collapsed (caught only by the text pin).

  Perimeter unchanged.
