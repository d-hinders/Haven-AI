- **#4893 (second PR)**: the paywall proxy's retries and limits, built to
  `docs/architecture/12-merchant-kit.md` §6 and §7. The merchant still receives
  every payment directly at its own `PAY_TO`; Haven is not in the flow and the
  proxy still has no settlement key and submits no transaction.

  What it adds:
  - **Proof-backed retries.** A forward other than the reserving request's
    own first one (the retry of a failed call, a settlement found on the
    chain, a stale in-flight call) needs a `SIGN-IN-WITH-X` proof signed by
    the payment's payer over a challenge the proxy issued. The proxy builds
    the signed message itself and checks the signature recovers to the payer,
    so a stranger who rebuilds a public payment header buys nothing. A proof
    moves no money and approves no payment; it only lets the payer have the
    call it already paid for, within the three-forward cap.
  - **Challenge nonces.** One live nonce per payment, in the proxy's own
    table in `PAYMENTS_DB`, valid 300 s and accepted once. It is retired
    only after an upstream slot is taken, so a proof refused for capacity or
    for its body is not spent.
  - **Limits.** Per client address, unpaid requests and payment headers that
    fail verification are limited (60 a minute, a burst of 10, 10 concurrent
    unpaid). A header that verified, or that matches a known payment, is
    never counted or refused for rate. Two global caps bound the merchant's
    own cost: 16 concurrent verifications and 32 concurrent upstream calls.
    A request with no upstream slot after verification sends no settle, so
    nothing is charged for a call that will not be served. `CLIENT_IP_HEADER`
    names the header a merchant's own front proxy sets to the client address.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected: Haven receives, holds and moves nothing
  here, the proxy holds no key that can sign a transfer, and Haven's own
  buyer-side paths do not change. Money-path list unchanged:
  `packages/paywall-proxy/src/**` stays a control glob.

  Tests: 142 in the proxy suite, against a mocked facilitator, chain reader
  and upstream, with a buyer that signs real EIP-3009 authorizations and real
  sign-in proofs through the reference x402 client (Haven's signer matches it
  byte for byte). 19 mutation cells, each going red.

  Perimeter unchanged.
