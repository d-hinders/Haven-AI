- **#4897 — the sales ingest: a sale record is accepted only against a transfer Haven read from the chain (epic #4885, PR A of two).** The merchant key from #4896 gets its one write, `POST /merchant/sales`. The merchant's server reports one sale record per settled payment (`docs/architecture/12-merchant-kit.md` section 5); Haven reads the transaction itself, through the by-hash read #4903 added, and stores the sale only when it finds a USDC transfer into the key's account. This PR is the ingest half; the owner's reads (totals, breakdowns, the paged list, clearing a record) are the second PR.

  What changed (`packages/backend/src/`):
  - **`routes/merchant-sales.ts` (new), `modules/transactions/merchant-sales.ts` (new).** The route is the auth door (`merchantAuthMiddleware`: the account comes from the key and nowhere else), a per-key rate limit of 120 a minute (`merchantSalesRateLimit`, keyed by `merchantRateLimitKeyFor` on the `sk_merchant_` credential from `Authorization: Bearer` or `X-API-Key`, so neither a junk `Authorization` header nor the choice of header opens a second bucket), and the status mapping. The module is the ingest. The body is the section 5 record with `additionalProperties: false`, through the request-validation plugin, and the module is on the enforced list from its first commit. Answers: **201** a new sale; **200** an identical resend (same transaction and the same `product_id`, `product_name`, `merchant_reference`, `receipt_number` and `vat_treatment`), and nothing is written; **409** `sale_record_conflict` for a different record on a transaction that already has one, the first stands (also answered, with a message saying the record changed while it was written, when a lost insert race finds no winner row); **425** `transaction_not_visible` with `Retry-After: 10` when the receipt is not found or has fewer than 3 confirmations (the retry answer, distinct from every refusal); **422** `no_payment_to_account` when the receipt is confirmed and has no USDC leg into the key's account; **422** `chain_mismatch`; **400** `unsupported_record_version`; **503** `chain_unavailable`.
  - **Amount, payer, token and time come only from the chain read's legs.** The record's `amount` and `payer` are validated as strings and stored nowhere (the table has no column for either), so a record that states a different amount changes nothing. The sale never reads an existing `inbound_transfers` row as its basis: rows written by the old caller-supplied route and the explorer carry `log_index NULL`, so a row's presence is not proof the chain carried the transfer (the #4940 review finding the issue records). A test plants such a row while the chain shows no leg, and gets 422 and no sale.
  - **No oracle.** The chain read is scoped to the key's own account, and nothing else is looked up on the refusal path, so a hash that paid another Haven account and a hash that paid nobody get the same 422 body. A test seeds the other account's transfer and compares the two answers.
  - **`db/migrations/131_merchant_sales.ts` (new).** `merchant_sales`, unique on `(chain_id, LOWER(tx_hash), account_id)`: one record per transaction per account, whatever the number of transfers in it. The chain facts (`amount_raw`, `token_address`, `payer_address`, `several_payers`, `sender_is_own_address`, `block_number`, `block_time`) are copied from the legs at ingest; the record's own fields (`product_id`, `product_name`, `merchant_reference`, `receipt_number`, `vat_treatment`, `record_version`) are stored as stated. `held_by` (`account` or `stripe`, default `account`) and `receiving_address` are added for #4952 and only `'account'` and NULL are written here. It is **not a match kind** and never touches `match_kind`, `balance_consumed` or the receive balance; a test leaves a matched, consumed row beside a sale and asserts the balance and the row unchanged.
  - **Several transfers in one transaction.** The sale attaches to the transaction: its amount is the sum of the legs that do not come from the owner's own addresses, its payer is the single sender of the revenue legs (read over all legs when none counts) and NULL with `several_payers` otherwise, so one own leg beside one customer leg is the customer's sale. A transaction paid only from own addresses is stored with amount 0 and `sender_is_own_address`, and is left out of revenue by the reads in the second PR. Zero-value legs are already dropped by the chain read, so an all-zero transaction has no leg and is never a sale.
  - **`infra/repositories/inbound-transfers.ts` — the own-address set is widened (owner decision 2026-11-16).** `listOwnAccountAddresses` now returns every `smart_accounts.account_address` of the user on any network, every `agents.delegate_address` of the user's agents (revoked included) and every `agent_rekeys.old_delegate_address` of those agents, lower-case, in one query. The index-time flag and the sale share this one set. **It intentionally changes `sender_is_own_address` for newly indexed rows of every account:** a transfer from one of the owner's agent delegates, which previously counted as revenue, is now flagged as a move between the owner's own addresses. Rows already indexed keep what they recorded, so a later account or rotation never rewrites past revenue.
  - **`modules/transactions/chain-read.ts`.** A sibling, `readInboundTransactionByTxHash`, returns whether the receipt exists, its block and the head from the same provider, so "no receipt yet" (retry) is told from "a receipt with no leg to the account" (refusal). `readInboundTransfersByTxHash`, and so `POST /receive/{accountAddress}/ingest`, is unchanged; its existing route suite pins that.
  - **`middleware/rate-limit.ts`, `openapi/spec.ts`, `openapi/route-modules.generated.ts`, `index.ts`.** The rate-limit constant, the operation and its schemas with every status documented, the regenerated route-module map, and the route registration.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - Nothing here holds a key, signs, moves funds or settles for anyone. The route stores a merchant's statement beside a chain read; the merchant key stays receive-only and authenticates this route and no other.
  - The chain is the only source of the sale's amount, payer, token and time. The merchant's word (`product_id`, `product_name`, `merchant_reference`, `receipt_number`, `vat_treatment`) is stored as stated and checked by nothing, which the merchant-kit document says in section 5.
  - A key cannot test whether a hash paid another account, and cannot create, clear or read a sale of another account.
  - The device-login census gains `POST /merchant/sales` on its must-refuse list.

  Mutation-tested (each cell reddens the named test):
  - Making the unique index non-unique (`131_merchant_sales.ts`) -> `131_merchant_sales.test.ts` "holds one record per transaction per account".
  - Answering a different record as the same sale (`isSameRecord`) -> `merchant-sales.db.test.ts` "refuses a different record for the same transaction (409) and the first stands".
  - Removing the version check -> "refuses an unknown version (400)".
  - Counting one confirmation instead of three -> "answers the retry with fewer than 3 confirmations, and accepts at exactly 3".
  - Storing the record's `amount` instead of the chain's -> "stores the chain's amount and payer when the record states different ones" and the multi-leg tests.
  - Answering a different 422 when another account holds the hash -> "answers a hash that paid another Haven account exactly as a hash that paid nobody (no oracle)".
  - Removing the no-leg refusal (so an existing row could stand in) -> "never takes a caller-supplied inbound row as the basis of a sale".
  - Keying the limit on the shared `rateLimitKeyFor`, or dropping the Bearer-first read -> the junk-`Authorization` and both-headers rate-limit tests.
  - Answering a lost insert race as an identical resend -> "two different records: one is stored (201), the other is refused (409)".
  - Reading the payer over all legs, or an own-only transaction over no legs -> the own-leg-plus-customer-leg and own-only tests.
  - Widening the unique key -> "pins the unique index to exactly (chain_id, lower(tx_hash), account_id)".
  - Removing the agent-delegate or the rotation arm of `listOwnAccountAddresses` -> `own-account-addresses.db.test.ts` and "leaves the owner's own addresses out of the amount".

  Perimeter widened, not narrowed. `routes/merchant-sales.ts` and `modules/transactions/merchant-sales.ts` join the CONTROL list in `.github/money-path-globs.json` (and `.github/labeler.yml` and the perimeter doc): no QA scenario posts a sale yet. The repository and the migration are already on the runtime list through `infra/repositories/**` and `db/migrations/**`.

  Verdict: **Perimeter unchanged.** A receive-side bookkeeping route that reads the chain and stores a record; it holds no key, signs nothing, moves no funds and grants no authority.
