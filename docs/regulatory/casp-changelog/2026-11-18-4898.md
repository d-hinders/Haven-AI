- **#4898 — the paywall proxy reports each settled sale to Haven through the shared core reporter (epic #4885).** After a call is settled, `packages/paywall-proxy` sends one sale record to `POST /merchant/sales` (#4897) with the merchant's Sales key, using the record builder and sender written once in `packages/merchant-payments` (#4950). The proxy adds only what is its own: the persistent outbox (`SALES_OUTBOX`, design §12), the store's existing `saleReported` flag, and the re-report command. No second implementation.

  What changed:
  - **`packages/paywall-proxy/src/sales.ts` (new).** `saleRecordFor` builds the §5 record from the consumed-payment store's record — the settlement's transaction hash, the product's `id` and name, the amount, the payer, and the `merchantReference` the store reserved for the payment — plus the receipt number and VAT treatment of the receipt the buyer got, both absent when the deployment issues no receipt. `createSalesReporter` posts it with the core's `reportSale`, fire-and-forget: `report` returns at once and never throws, so the buyer's answer and its timing do not depend on Haven. An accepted record sets `saleReported`; a record the reporter gave up on, or one Haven answered 429 to, goes to the outbox; any other 4xx is a refusal, logged once by the core and never resent.
  - **`packages/paywall-proxy/src/sales-outbox.ts` (new).** A bounded JSON Lines file at `SALES_OUTBOX` (10,000 records), rewritten whole on every change (temp file, then rename), replayed every 60 s. A replay takes a snapshot, attempts each record once, and removes only the snapshot's entries by id, so a sale that settles during a replay survives the merge; a crash mid-replay leaves every entry in the file and the next replay sends it again. One record is replayed at most 5 times, then logged as `sales_outbox_gave_up` and dropped. When the outbox is full the proxy keeps what it has and logs `sales_outbox_full` for each record it cannot add.
  - **`packages/paywall-proxy/src/report-sale.ts` (new), `src/index.ts`.** `node dist/index.js report-sale <tx-hash> [--vat-treatment SE_25|REVERSE_CHARGE] [--new-reference]` sends a past settled call again from the payment the store kept, for a sale whose record was cleared on Haven or never recorded. It opens `PAYMENTS_DB`, so the proxy must be stopped (§6: one process per store), and it exits 0 only when Haven accepted the record.
  - **`src/config.ts`, `src/main.ts`, `src/handler.ts`, `src/sqlite-store.ts`.** `HAVEN_MERCHANT_KEY` is the switch: unset, nothing is reported, no outbox is opened, and the proxy starts exactly as without it. Set, reporting is on and `HAVEN_SALES_URL` (Haven's origin) and `SALES_OUTBOX` are required — a key that is not a Sales key, a missing or unusable origin, or no outbox refuses startup and names the variable. The key joins `SECRET_VARIABLES`, so the log redactor, the console redactor and the response redactor all cover it. The report is hooked at the two places a payment becomes settled (the holder's own settlement, and a settlement resolved from the chain), before the forward. `findByTxHash` reads the payment the re-report command sends. `/healthz` reports the outbox depth when reporting is on.
  - **`README.md`, `.env.example`.** The sales reporter is no longer "not in this release": the settings, the resend limit (three inline resends, then at most 5 outbox replays), the outbox bound, the re-report command and what to monitor are documented for the merchant.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - **No new authority.** The Sales key is receive-only (#4896): it authenticates the sales routes and nothing else, cannot move funds, sign, or change signers, budgets or agents. The proxy holds it only to report, passes it to the core reporter and nowhere else, and treats it as a secret everywhere it could surface.
  - **The report is a claim, not a control.** Haven stores a sale only against a USDC transfer it reads from the chain into the key's own account (#4897), taking the amount, payer, token and time from the chain and never from the record. A wrong or malicious record cannot create revenue, move a balance or reach another account.
  - **Haven receives no one else's funds, and is not in the payment path.** The proxy still settles buyer to merchant directly through Coinbase's facilitator; the report is a POST after settlement. Reporting never delays or fails the buyer's call: the buyer's answer is the same, and as fast, whether Haven accepts the record, refuses it, is slow, or is unreachable.
  - **Opt-in and additive.** With no merchant key, every surface is as before: nothing is sent, no outbox file is opened, `/healthz` has no `sales` block, and the startup line says `sales_reporting: "off (no HAVEN_MERCHANT_KEY)"`. The payment path, the refusals and the receipt are unchanged.
  - **The outbox holds no secret and no buyer declaration.** It is a bounded local file of sale records (transaction hash, product, amount, payer address, merchant reference, receipt number and VAT treatment), replayed with the key the reporter holds in memory.

  Mutation-tested (each cell reddens the named test):
  - Awaiting the report before answering the buyer -> `sales-proxy.test.ts` "gives the buyer the same answer, as fast, whether Haven accepts, refuses, is slow or is unreachable".
  - Not reporting a settled payment -> "reports one sale record for a settled call, with the settlement hash, the product and the reserved reference".
  - Treating a 429 as a refusal instead of queueing it -> `sales.test.ts` "queues a record the reporter gave up on, and a 429, but not a refusal".
  - Retrying a 422 from the outbox -> `sales-outbox.test.ts` "drops a refusal and never resends it".
  - Dropping the outbox's attempt limit -> "keeps a record Haven asks to be retried, and drops it after the attempt limit".
  - Losing a record added while a replay is running -> "keeps a record added while a replay is running".
  - Leaving the merchant key out of the proxy's secret list -> `sales-proxy.test.ts` "never puts the merchant key in a log line or a response".
  - Omitting the receipt number and VAT treatment -> "carries the receipt number and VAT treatment of the receipt the buyer got, and leaves both out without a receipt".
  - Sending a fresh reference on a re-report by default -> "reports a past settled call again on request, with the reference the store reserved or a new one".

  Verdict: **Perimeter unchanged.** A receive-only report of the merchant's own settled sales; it adds no key with spend authority, signs nothing new, and Haven still receives payment only for the merchant's own products.
