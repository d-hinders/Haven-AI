- **#4950 — the demo merchant receives into a Haven Sales account and reports its sales through the shared core reporter (epic #4885).** Haven's own demo merchant gains one opt-in product paid into a Haven Sales account, and reports each settled sale of it to `POST /merchant/sales` (#4897) with the account's receive-only Sales key (#4896). The record builder and sender are written once, in the shared core, for the demo merchant now and the paywall proxy later (#4898).

  What changed:
  - **`packages/merchant-payments/src/sale-report.ts` (new).** `buildSaleRecord` makes the `docs/architecture/12-merchant-kit.md` section 5 record; `reportSale` posts it with `Authorization: Bearer <Sales key>`, resends a 5xx, a timeout or network failure, and the route's one retry answer (425 `transaction_not_visible`) after 1 s, 4 s and 16 s (each raised to `Retry-After`, capped at 60 s), and logs any other 4xx once as a refusal and never resends it. It never throws, and no outcome or log line it produces carries the key or a response body's text.
  - **`packages/merchant-payments/src/merchant-reference.ts` (new).** `newMerchantReference` (`sale_<ULID>`) moved from `packages/paywall-proxy/src/handler.ts`, which now imports it. The settle-once coordinator uses it in place of `randomUUID()`, and `SettledPayment` gains `merchantReference`, so every report of one payment carries the reference reserved for it.
  - **`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/sales.ts` (new), `products.ts`, `http.ts`, `server.ts`, `index.ts`, `x402.ts`.** A new product, `field_notes` (0.0025 USDC, one-time, its own category and `buy_field_notes` tool), offered only when `MERCHANT_SALES_ADDRESS`, `MERCHANT_SALES_KEY` and `MERCHANT_HAVEN_BASE_URL` are all set and usable. Its `payTo` is the Sales account, resolved through `productReceivingAddress`; every other product's receiving address, id, price and settlement is unchanged (the #4850 characterization snapshot is unchanged). The report is sent after the receipt is built, only for this product, only for a payment `settled_onchain`, once per transaction, and without the buyer waiting on it.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - **No new authority.** The Sales key is receive-only (#4896): it authenticates the sales routes and nothing else, cannot move funds, sign, or change signers, budgets or agents. The demo merchant holds it only to report, and passes it to the core reporter and nowhere else.
  - **The report is a claim, not a control.** Haven stores a sale only against a USDC transfer it reads from the chain into the key's own account (#4897), taking amount, payer, token and time from the chain, never from the record. A wrong or malicious report cannot create revenue, move a balance or reach another account.
  - **Haven receives no one else's funds.** The new product is Haven's own demo product: it pays Haven's own demo Sales account directly, by the buyer's EIP-3009 authorization or ERC-7710 redemption, with the merchant's existing self-settlement. Nothing is pooled, held or forwarded, and the buyer's answer does not depend on Haven.
  - **Opt-in and additive.** Unset, the merchant is byte-identical to before: the product is on no surface and `/healthz` has no `sales` block. No catalog row, migration or QA scenario changes.

  Mutation-tested (each cell reddens the named test):
  - Dropping the per-transaction dedupe in `createSalesReporting` -> `sales.test.ts` "reports a settled sale once, with the record built from the payment, the product and the receipt" (the replay sends a second report).
  - Reporting a payment that is not `settled_onchain` -> "reports only an on-chain settlement of the Sales product, once per transaction".
  - Treating 425 as a refusal in `reportSale` -> `sale-report.test.ts` "resends after \"not visible yet\", waiting at least the answer's Retry-After".
  - Retrying every 4xx -> "logs a 409 refusal once and never resends it" (and its sibling cells).
  - Awaiting the report in `http.ts` -> "gives the same answer, status and timing whether Haven accepts, refuses, hangs or is unreachable".
  - Generating a fresh reference per serve instead of reserving it once -> `settle-once.test.ts` "#4950: the settled payment carries the reference the store reserved".

  Verdict: **Perimeter unchanged.** A receive-only report of Haven's own demo sales; it adds no key with spend authority, signs nothing new, and Haven still receives payment only for its own products.
