- **#4952 — the Stripe deposit-address registry and Stripe-held sales (epic #4886; dev and Stripe sandbox only).** A Sales account's owner registers the address a payment processor receives on their behalf, and the sales ingest accepts a sale paid there. A proof of address ownership is required before any production use. Haven holds no Stripe key and creates no Stripe records.

  What changed (`packages/backend/src/`):
  - **`routes/merchant-deposit-addresses.ts` (new), `modules/transactions/merchant-deposit-addresses.ts` (new), `infra/repositories/merchant-deposit-addresses.ts` (new).** `GET /merchant-deposit-addresses` (the account's registrations, active and retired), `POST /merchant-deposit-addresses` (register) and `POST /merchant-deposit-addresses/:id/retire`. Owner-session routes (dashboard JWT) enforced by the request-validation plugin from their first commit; the Sales key is refused at the door (401) because a key route would let a leaked key attach any address's payments to the account. Register and retire carry `moneyPathRateLimit` and are on the device-login must-refuse list. With merchant services off the list is empty and the writes answer 409 `MERCHANT_ACCOUNT_REQUIRED`, as the sales reads do.
  - **What a registration refuses.** `INVALID_DEPOSIT_ADDRESS` (400), `DEPOSIT_ADDRESS_IS_OWN` (409: the owner's own smart accounts on any network and every current and past agent delegate, `listOwnAccountAddresses`), `DEPOSIT_ADDRESS_ALREADY_REGISTERED` (409: an active registration on any account on the chain). The chain is the Sales account's own; the request names none. Every registration in every answer carries `verified: false` and the note "Registered by you, not verified by Haven."
  - **`db/migrations/134_merchant_deposit_addresses.ts` (new).** The registry table. A partial unique index `(chain_id, address) WHERE retired_at IS NULL` holds one active registration per address and chain; a retired address can be registered again. Rows are never deleted: retiring sets `retired_at`. A plain, transactional migration on a new, empty table.
  - **`modules/transactions/merchant-sales.ts`, `modules/transactions/chain-read.ts`, `infra/repositories/merchant-sales.ts`.** The sale record is unchanged (no field, no version). When the transaction pays the key's account nothing, the by-hash read (`readInboundTransactionByTxHash`, which takes an optional list of further recipients and returns their legs apart; its `legs` and every existing caller's answers are unchanged) also returns the legs to the account's registered addresses. A leg counts only when its address is registered to the key's account and was active at the leg's block time. The sale is stored with `held_by = 'stripe'` and `receiving_address` from the registry, never from the record, and **no `inbound_transfers` row is written**, so nothing changes in any Haven balance. A transaction that pays the account is an account sale, counting only the legs into the account. The own-sender fold is decided at ingest with the same own-address set. Legs to another account's address, an unregistered one, or one outside its window all get the same 422 `no_payment_to_account` as a hash that paid nobody (the registry read is scoped to the key's own account).
  - **`infra/repositories/merchant-sales-reads.ts`, `modules/transactions/merchant-sales-reads.ts`.** Stripe-held sales are their own arm of the per-transaction relation. The summary's tiles and breakdowns count only account-held payments (unchanged for an account with none) and gain a separate `received_in_stripe` figure (a count and an amount, own moves left out). The list carries them with `held_by: 'stripe'` and `receiving_address`; the detail read returns their chain facts from the sale row, as no transfers are indexed for them. Clearing one works as before.
  - **`openapi/spec.ts`, `openapi/route-modules.generated.ts`, `index.ts`, `modules/transactions/index.ts`.** The three operations and their schemas, the regenerated map and API types, the registration and the enforced-validation entry.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - Nothing here holds a key, signs, moves funds or settles for anyone. The registry stores the owner's own statement about an address; the ingest records a merchant's statement beside a chain read.
  - A Stripe-held sale is in no Haven balance and in no revenue figure of the Sales account; it is shown apart and marked.
  - **Accepted residuals (demo only; a proof of ownership closes all three before production):** an owner can register an address that is not theirs, producing false sales on their own page with no money moving; someone can register another merchant's address first; the "already registered" refusal tells one owner that some other account holds the address.
