- **#4953 — the demo merchant reports its Stripe-settled joke sales to Haven (epic #4886; dev and Stripe sandbox only).** The demo merchant's joke, the one product Stripe records, is also reported to `POST /merchant/sales` through the shared reporter from #4950, so Haven can show the sale as "Received in Stripe" against the deposit address registered under #4952. A proof of address ownership is required before any production use.

  What changed (`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/`):
  - **`src/sales.ts`, `src/http.ts`, `src/index.ts`.** After the Stripe record is made, a joke payment that the recorder decided to send (`settled_onchain`, paid to the current deposit address) is reported with its transaction hash, once per transaction, without the buyer waiting. A payment the recorder skips (`paid_other_address`, Stripe off) or that is not `settled_onchain` reports nothing. The record is #4950's, unchanged.
  - **Settings.** `MERCHANT_SALES_KEY` and `MERCHANT_HAVEN_BASE_URL` turn reporting on; `MERCHANT_SALES_ADDRESS` now concerns only Field Notes. Its checks are unchanged. With any of the joke's three (Stripe, key, base URL) unset, the joke behaves as before. The startup line names which products report.
  - **Docs.** The README and the secret-rotation steps say to register the deposit address in Haven before a demo and to re-register and retire it on rotation.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - **No new authority, no custody.** The Sales key is the receive-only key from #4896; the merchant holds it only to report. Haven holds no Stripe key and creates no Stripe record. Nothing here signs, moves funds or settles for anyone.
  - **The report is a claim, not a control.** Haven stores the sale only against a chain read of a transfer to an address registered by the Sales account's owner (#4952), takes the figures from the chain, and counts it in no Haven balance.
  - **Additive and demo-only.** Without the settings every surface is as before.
