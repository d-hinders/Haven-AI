- **#4963 — the Sales reads carry today's USDC rate (epic #4885, backend half).** The Sales page (#4900) showed USDC to an owner whose display currency is SEK or EUR. The three owner reads now carry a display rate so the page converts on the client. Read-only; no frontend in this half.

  What changed (`packages/backend/src/`):
  - **`infra/fiat-values.ts`.** `loadTokenRates` moves here from the private body of `routes/dashboard.ts`, beside `FiatValues`, which is the same shape as the dashboard's `TokenRates` (one type, no copy). Behaviour is unchanged: a failed read nulls the whole triple, a zero quote nulls only that currency. `routes/dashboard.ts` imports it; the dashboard answers are byte-identical.
  - **`modules/transactions/merchant-sales-reads.ts`, `routes/merchant-sales-reads.ts`.** `GET /merchant-sales/summary` (top level), `GET /merchant-sales/payments` (top level of the page) and `GET /merchant-sales/payments/:txHash` (the detail's own branch, not on each row) carry `usdc_rates: { usd, eur, sek } | null`. The symbol is the chain registry's settlement token for the account's chain (`settlementTokenForChain`), not a literal. The price read runs in parallel with the database reads. The two Sales-off empty answers carry `null` and read no price; the detail stays a 404 with Sales off.
  - **`openapi/spec.ts`, `packages/core/src/api-types.ts`.** The shared `MerchantSalesUsdcRates` schema, required on the three answers; types regenerated.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - The field is a read-only display rate at today's price (the same 60 s cached CoinGecko read the dashboard's spot rates use). It is not the rate at the time of sale and books nothing; every amount on the wire stays atomic USDC from the chain.
  - A failed price read costs the page its conversion, never a figure, and nothing gates on the rate. No key, no signing, no funds, no authority, no write.
  - No query, scope or auth changed: the reads are still the caller's merchant account only, behind the same owner-session door.

  Mutation-tested (each cell reddens the named tests, in `merchant-sales-reads.db.test.ts`; `dashboard.test.ts` and the dashboard characterization suite pass with the move):
  - The summary answering `usdc_rates: null` always -> "carries the USDC rate on the summary, the page and the detail" and "nulls only the currency with no usable quote".
  - A hard-coded wrong symbol in place of the registry lookup -> "carries the USDC rate ... read for the chain's settlement token".
  - The Sales-off payments page dropping `usdc_rates: null` -> "reads as the empty state, and the clear answers 409" and "does not read a price for the Sales-off answers".
  - `loadTokenRates` letting a thrown read escape -> "answers null for the rate, and still every figure, when the price read fails".
  - A zero quote kept as a rate (`> 0` loosened to `>= 0`) -> "nulls only the currency with no usable quote".

  Perimeter unchanged. Both Sales read files are already on the CONTROL list in `.github/money-path-globs.json`; `routes/dashboard.ts` is touched by the move only.

  Verdict: **Perimeter unchanged.** One read-only display-rate field on three owner reads; no key, no signing, no funds, no authority.
