- **#4872 (part 1 of 2) — checkout details on the paid x402 request, on the SDK paths (epic #4868).** When a merchant's payment challenge asks for checkout details under `extensions["haven-checkout-details"]`, the SDK answers on the paid request with the fields asked for, or refuses before the payment is authorised when a required one cannot be shared. The design is `docs/architecture/13-checkout-details.md`. This part covers the SDK's `fetch`, `payX402Quote` and `resumeX402Payment` and the local MCP tools that call them; the hosted MCP paths follow in part 2.

  What changed (`packages/sdk/src/`):
  - **`checkout-details.ts` (new, pure).** It reads the merchant's request, and a missing, malformed or unknown-version entry is no request. It builds the answer from the fields asked for, in the merchant's order, within a 1,536-byte budget. It estimates the paid request's header block against the 16 KB ceiling. And it holds the one attach site, `withCheckoutDetailsHeader`.
  - **`client-checkout-details.ts` (new, wire).** It reads the agent's own saved details (`GET /agents/:id/checkout-details`, #4871) only when something was asked. Every failure maps to a named state: sharing off, the deployment off, nothing saved, a 404 from a backend without the read, and any other failure as unreadable. It turns the decision into the header, or into `CheckoutDetailsUnavailableError`.
  - **`client.ts`.** `fetch` and `payX402Quote` decide before `authorizeX402`. A refusal there means no `/x402` intent is created and no funds move. `resumeX402Payment` decides before re-signing; the payment is already funded, so a refusal there means the paid request is not sent and the payment can be resumed again later.
  - **`merchant-completion.ts`.** `retryRequest` takes the decided answer and attaches it after any captured-request snapshot, so it never lands in a resume state, a Haven-API body or the payment payload. It re-checks the actual header block, and drops the answer rather than send a block that would answer 431.
  - **`types.ts`.** The failure code `CHECKOUT_DETAILS_UNAVAILABLE` and `CheckoutDetailsUnavailableError`: status 409, deterministic. The body carries the missing field names, the reason and `stop_and_tell_user`, never a value.
  - **`tools.ts`.** The adapter's `authorize_x402_payment` and `resume_x402_payment` rebuild the challenge without `extensions`, so they neither answer nor refuse. Their descriptions say so.

  Also changed: `packages/mcp/src/tools.ts`, where the local tools answer the refusal as a typed result (code, `missing_fields`, `reason`, `stop_and_tell_user`, and why no tool follows). Their three descriptions, and the regenerated local contract golden, say so.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - **No custody.** The answer is unsigned data the owner saved and turned on for this agent. It moves no money, authorises nothing, and is not built by any signing surface.
  - **No change to who can pay.** It changes no amount, no recipient and no signing rule. The only new behaviour on the money path is a refusal BEFORE authorisation, which can only stop a payment, never start or widen one.
  - **Seller only.** The answer goes to the merchant alone: never to Haven, never into the echoed `extensions` that reach the facilitator, never into a resume state. The values are never logged; the one log line names fields, the size, the outcome and the host.
  - **Unchanged without a request.** A challenge that asks for nothing, or asks in a shape Haven cannot read, gets exactly today's paid request, and no read of the details is made.

  Tests:
  - **`checkout-details.test.ts`** covers:
    - reading the request, including every malformed shape;
    - only the fields asked for;
    - non-ASCII values;
    - optional versus required;
    - each refusal reason;
    - the largest answer the shape allows (1,426 bytes) fitting the budget;
    - over-budget omission and refusal;
    - the header block;
    - the attach site.
  - **`x402-checkout-details-flow.test.ts`** goes through the real client on all three paths:
    - **Characterization:** no request means no read and today's header names.
    - **Answers:** the email-only answer, and an optional field off or a failed read proceeding.
    - **Unreadable requests** (version 2) proceed.
    - **Refusals:** a refusal per reason on `fetch` and `payX402Quote`, with no `/x402` call. The funded resume refusal does not re-sign or deliver.
    - **No values leak:** a census that no value reaches Haven, the payment header's echo, the refusal or the log.
    - **Unsupported paths:** the two adapter tools pay as today.
  - **`packages/mcp/src/checkout-details-refusal.test.ts`** covers the typed local answer on the three tools.

  **Proved red** with `origin/dev`'s `client.ts` and `merchant-completion.ts` swapped in: 16 of the 28 flow tests fail. The 12 that pass there are the characterization and the "proceeds" cases, whose behaviour is today's.

  **Mutation-tested:** 15 mutations, each run against the suites, and each reddens a named test:
  - an unknown field read;
  - the version not checked;
  - required treated as optional;
  - unasked fields sent;
  - no budget check;
  - a 404 not told apart from other failures;
  - "nothing saved" read as "sharing off";
  - the details read when nothing was asked;
  - the refusal not thrown;
  - the refusal moved after `authorizeX402`;
  - the header not attached;
  - the encoded value logged;
  - the answer merged into the echoed `extensions`;
  - the local typed mapping dropped;
  - the resume refusal moved after the re-sign.

  **Perimeter unchanged.** The SDK and MCP sources are already in `casp-risk-guardrails.md`'s scope, and `merchant-completion.ts` is on the money-path control list.

  **Verdict: Perimeter unchanged.** One unsigned header to the seller, and a refusal before authorisation; no key, no signing, no funds, no authority.
