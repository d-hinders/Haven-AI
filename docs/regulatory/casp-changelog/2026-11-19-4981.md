- **#4981 — Stripe-held sales count in the Sales revenue figures, split by where received (epic #4885).** After the demo the owner decided that a sale is a sale wherever the money landed. `GET /merchant-sales/summary` now counts a sale received at a registered Stripe deposit address in `revenue_raw`, `sales`, `by_product`, `by_payer` and `by_day`, where #4952 kept it apart in `received_in_stripe`. Read-only, backend and frontend, in one pull request.

  What changed (`packages/frontend/src/`, display only): the Sales page shows four tiles, the Revenue tile's foot and the payments list's summary line split the total as "in Haven · in Stripe", the By day chart draws "In Haven" and "In Stripe", the panel says a Stripe-held sale counts in revenue but not in a balance, and Analytics' Revenue tiles follow the same definition. No wire, auth or write change on the client.

  What changed (`packages/backend/src/`):
  - **`infra/repositories/merchant-sales-reads.ts`.** The summary's revenue figures read the filtered relation `pay_f` (both arms) instead of its account-held part, which is removed as unused. `without_product` and `own_moves` read the same rows as before: a Stripe-held row is always a sale and never an own move. New `revenue_in_account_raw` and `by_day[].in_stripe_raw` carry the split under the active filters.
  - **`modules/transactions/merchant-sales-reads.ts`.** `average_sale_raw` is now `revenue_raw` over `sales + without_product.count`, rounded down, and 0 with none. `received_in_stripe` is kept, now a part of `revenue_raw`.
  - **`openapi/spec.ts`, `packages/core/src/api-types.ts`.** The two additive fields, and the descriptions of the summary, `average_sale_raw`, `received_in_stripe` and the list's `held_by`; types regenerated. No endpoint, parameter or error changed.
  - **Not changed.** The payments list, the facets, the filters, ingest and every balance read. A several-payer Stripe sale stores no individual payers and is `unattributed`. No migration.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - It is reporting only. The change decides which stored rows a revenue figure adds up; no row is written, no balance is read or changed, and no funds move.
  - Haven still holds no Stripe money and no Stripe credential. A Stripe-held sale remains in no Haven balance, no Accounts, Receive or Transactions read, and the row stays marked "Received in Stripe".
  - No key, no signing, no authority, no auth or scope change: the reads are still the caller's merchant account only, behind the same owner-session door.

  Mutation-tested (each cell reddens the named tests in `merchant-sales-reads.db.test.ts` and `merchant-deposit-addresses.db.test.ts`; the two files were run together and each cell was reverted after):

  - `by_payer` reading the account-held rows only -> 4 failed.
  - `by_day` reading the account-held rows only -> 3 failed.
  - `by_product` reading the account-held rows only -> 4 failed.
  - `average_sale_raw` back to the sales' amount over `sales` -> 5 failed.
  - `by_day[].in_stripe_raw` always 0 -> 3 failed.
  - `revenue_in_account_raw` counting every row -> 5 failed.
  - `unattributed` reading the account-held rows only -> 2 failed.
  - The own-only Stripe exclusion (`NOT ms.sender_is_own_address`) removed -> 2 failed.

  Perimeter unchanged. Both Sales read files are already on the CONTROL list in `.github/money-path-globs.json`.

  Verdict: **Perimeter unchanged.** Read-only reporting; one meaning change and two additive fields on an owner read; no key, no signing, no funds, no authority.
