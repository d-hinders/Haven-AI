- **#4987**: the demo merchant serves a product list at `/.well-known/x402`,
  the path a Haven Sales account's Products tab reads, so the
  merchant-services demo can show what the demo merchant sells. The merchant
  still sells only its own demo products, and no third-party funds are received
  or moved.

  What changed:
  - **`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/kit-discovery.ts`** (new) builds a reduced
    version 1 merchant-kit discovery document: `version`, `network`, `asset`,
    `pay_to` (the merchant's Sales account, `MERCHANT_SALES_ADDRESS`), and the
    products that report a sale to Haven (Field Notes, and the joke when Stripe
    recording is on) with their names, descriptions and atomic prices.
  - **`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/http.ts`** answers `GET` and `HEAD` on
    `/.well-known/x402` with that document when the Sales address is set, and
    with the existing 404 otherwise.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - The route is a read of public data. It settles nothing, refuses nothing,
    and changes no price, receiving address, payment requirement or tool. The
    402 challenges, the settlement paths and the sale reports are unchanged.
  - Nothing pays from the document. The SDK's merchant discovery reads only
    `/.well-known/haven-demo-merchant` and `/`
    (`MERCHANT_DISCOVERY_PATHS` in `packages/sdk/src/merchant-discovery.ts`),
    and the document carries no `mcp_url`. Haven's catalogue reader
    (`modules/catalog/merchant-products.ts`) uses it only to name products,
    after comparing `pay_to` and `network` with the Sales account's own; it
    never uses `pay_to` as a payment destination.
  - The joke is listed under the Sales account's `pay_to` although its 402
    names the Stripe deposit address. That address is what a buyer pays, and
    it is unchanged; the document's `pay_to` is a label check only.
  - The demo merchant's own discovery document at `/` and
    `/.well-known/haven-demo-merchant` is unchanged; a test checks it carries
    no kit field.

  Tests:
  - `kit-discovery.test.ts`: the document's shape and products with Stripe
    recording on and off and without CDP, a 404 without sales reporting and
    without a Sales address, `HEAD`, `POST` not answered, and the demo
    merchant's own document unchanged. It also pins the backend fixture
    `catalog/__tests__/fixtures/demo-merchant-x402.json` byte for byte.
  - `catalog/__tests__/demo-merchant-x402.test.ts` (backend): Haven's
    `parseDiscoveryDocument` reads that fixture as a version 1 document with
    every product understood.

  Perimeter unchanged.
