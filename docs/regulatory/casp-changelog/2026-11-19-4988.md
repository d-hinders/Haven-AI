- **#4988**: the merchant kit (`packages/paywall-proxy`) checks a request body
  against the product's `input_schema` before it answers with a challenge and
  again before any facilitator call. A body the schema refuses is answered
  400 `invalid_request_body`, paid or not, and nothing is verified, settled or
  forwarded. Before this, a valid payment on a body the product could not
  serve was settled for any product without a `model` or an output cap.

  What changed:
  - **`src/input-schema.ts`** (new) compiles a product's schema (JSON Schema
    draft 2020-12, Ajv, already a dependency of the backend and the hosted MCP
    server at the same `^8.18.0` range) and reports failures as `{ path, rule }`
    with no value from the body, at most 10 entries of at most 200 characters.
    A `$ref` outside the schema is refused rather than fetched.
  - **`src/products.ts`** compiles every `input_schema` at load. An
    uncompilable schema, a remote `$ref`, `input_schema` on a `GET` or `DELETE`
    route, and an `input_example` that fails its schema each fail startup,
    naming the product.
  - **`src/handler.ts`** (`checkBody`) runs the schema after the product is
    picked by `model` and before the output-token cap is set, and refuses a
    non-JSON Content-Type for a product with a schema. A known payment
    re-presented with a failing body keeps its existing answer,
    `bad_request_after_settlement`, which now carries `errors`.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - The change only adds refusals before settlement. It moves no funds,
    changes no price, receiving address, payment requirement, signing step or
    key, and gives the kit no new authority. Haven's own payment paths and the
    shared `packages/merchant-payments` code are untouched.
  - A product without `input_schema` behaves exactly as before; the existing
    proxy tests pass unchanged.
  - The no-oracle rules hold: the refusal is decided from the body and the
    public products file alone, so its content says nothing about any payment,
    nonce or payer. It joins the existing body checks in their existing place
    (a paid request's key is still looked up first, and a known key is still
    answered from its record only when the header's digest matches), so the
    known-key answers keep their codes and statuses.

  Tests and mutation cells:
  - `input-schema.test.ts`: the validator's pointers, rules, bounds and
    no-echo property; remote and internal `$ref`; dialect and unknown-keyword
    refusals; Content-Type; each load-time refusal; the Opper demo file's
    schemas against real chat-completions bodies.
  - `proxy.test.ts`, `input_schema (#4988)`: an empty body, broken JSON, a
    `text/plain` body, a missing required field and a value over a `maximum`
    are each refused unpaid and with a payment header, with no facilitator
    call and no store record; a valid body is quoted, settled and forwarded as
    sent; a product without a schema is unchanged; on a capped product a
    schema failure comes before `cap_not_settable`, an over-cap `max_tokens` is
    still lowered and `messages: []` is the schema's call; a retried known
    payment with a failing body is not forwarded and its record is unchanged.
  - 14 prove-red cells, each caught at `bf4be3127`, among them the validation
    removed, the refusal moved after the facilitator's verify and settle, the
    no-schema branch validating anyway, and the refusal echoing a body value.

  Perimeter unchanged.
