- **#4989**: the merchant kit's answers now tell an agent what to do next. An
  unknown path (including `GET /`) carries `discovery_url`, the absolute URL of
  the discovery document; a path sold with another method answers 405
  `method_not_allowed` with an `Allow` header instead of 404; and the refusals
  that used to say only "no" name the field and the next step. Nothing changes
  what is paid, settled or delivered.

  What changed:
  - **`packages/paywall-proxy/src/handler.ts`**: `GET /` and every
    `unknown_route` answer carry `discovery_url` (`<origin>/.well-known/x402`,
    the origin from `PUBLIC_BASE_URL` or the request's `Host`). A path that is
    sold with another method answers 405 with `Allow` and `expected`; `OPTIONS`
    answers 204 with the same `Allow` and no body; neither makes a facilitator
    call. A missing `model` is `model_required` and an unsold one is
    `model_not_sold`, each with `field: "/model"` and `expected` listing the
    models sold on the route. A capped product whose body has no `messages`
    field is `messages_required` with `field: "/messages"`; a body that has one
    the cap cannot be set on keeps `cap_not_settable`. The unpaid 402 body
    carries `error: "payment_required"` and `message` like every other 402.
    `localCheckError` reads the code a `PaymentError` carries instead of
    matching its message text.
  - **`packages/merchant-payments/src/payment-checks.ts`**: `PaymentError`
    gains optional `code`, `field`, `expected` and `next`; every message stays
    byte-identical. `selectQuotedPaymentOption` names the differing field
    (`accepted.amount`, `accepted.payTo`, …) only when exactly one quoted
    option shares the payment's method, scheme and network — with several rails
    and none matching, naming a field would be a guess. `parseExactEvmPayload`
    refuses a payload that is not an object with a `PaymentError` naming
    `payload`, instead of reading `.authorization` on `undefined` and being
    answered with the generic "could not be read" sentence.
  - **`docs/architecture/12-merchant-kit.md`** and
    **`packages/paywall-proxy/README.md`**: the refusal tables list every code,
    status and field, and section 8 states the shape of a refusal and the
    precedence with `input_schema`.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - The change is wording and shape only. It moves no funds, changes no price,
    receiving address, payment requirement, signing step or key, and gives the
    kit no new authority. Settlement logic is untouched: the shared
    `PaymentError` messages are byte-identical, so the demo merchant's answers
    do not change and its tests pass unmodified.
  - The `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` header is still encoded from the challenge object,
    whose `error` stays the sentence "Payment required"; only the body of the
    unpaid 402 changed, and the client check recorded on the issue found
    nothing in `packages/sdk`, `packages/mcp-server`, `packages/backend` or
    `packages/signer` that matches on that sentence, `unknown_route` or
    `payment_mismatch`.
  - The no-oracle rules hold: every new field carries only what the discovery
    document or the challenge already publishes (the models sold on a route,
    the quoted option's values, the methods a path is sold with). The answers
    for known payments keep their codes and statuses, and a known-key
    `payment_mismatch` carries no `field`, as before.

  Tests and mutation cells:
  - `packages/merchant-payments/src/payment-checks.test.ts` (new): the field is
    named on a single-option challenge for method, scheme, network, amount,
    timeout, `payTo` and `asset`; no field when several options exist and none
    shares the payment's method, scheme and network, or when two do; the shared
    message is pinned byte-identical; `parseExactEvmPayload` names `payload`,
    `payload.authorization` and `payload.signature`.
  - `packages/paywall-proxy/src/proxy.test.ts`, `#4989`: `GET /` and an unknown
    path carry `discovery_url` with and without `BASE_PATH`; `GET`, `HEAD` and
    `OPTIONS` on a sold `POST` route answer 405/405/204 with `Allow: POST` and
    no facilitator call; `model_required` and `model_not_sold` list exactly the
    models sold; `messages_required` names `/messages` while a `messages` field
    the cap cannot use keeps `cap_not_settable`; a payment that differs on
    method, network, amount, `payTo`, `asset` or timeout names that field; a
    known-key `payment_mismatch` carries no field; a header without the payload
    object names `payload`; the unpaid 402 body carries `payment_required` and
    the `PAYMENT-REQUIRED` header re-encodes byte-identically; a schema product
    answers `invalid_request_body` for a missing `messages` while a schema-less
    one answers `messages_required`, and a missing `model` reaches
    `model_required` whatever the schema says; `refusalCodeFor` reads the code,
    not the message text.

  Perimeter unchanged.
