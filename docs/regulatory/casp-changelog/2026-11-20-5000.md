- **#5000 — a sale reads as a sale outside the Sales page (epic #4885).** The
  Transactions list, the account page, the dashboard card and the transaction
  detail panel called a sale "Deposit". The transaction reads now mark each
  inbound row into the Sales account that the Sales page counts as a sale, and
  the screens title it "Sale · <product>" or "Sale". Display only.

  What changed (`packages/backend/src/`):
  - **`infra/repositories/merchant-sales-reads.ts`.** The Sales relation's `$6`
    parameter is a list of transaction hashes instead of one hash; `findPayment`
    passes a list of one, and the four callers that pass `NULL` are unchanged.
    One new read, `listFeedSaleMarkers`, selects from the same `pay` relation,
    restricted to `held_by = 'account'` and `kind IN ('sale', 'no_product')`.
    The rule for what is a sale is therefore the Sales page's own, including its
    recorded `sender_is_own_address` flag; nothing re-derives it.
  - **`modules/transactions/sale-marker.ts` (new).** Marks a feed row only when
    it is inbound into the Sales account, on its chain, in the chain's USDC, not
    a folded own-account move, and not from another of the user's own accounts
    by the live lookup (#4099). Only while merchant services are on
    (`resolveSalesScope`). The product label is the Sales page's
    (`labelProducts`). A failed read leaves the rows unmarked and is logged.
  - **`routes/transactions.ts`, `routes/dashboard.ts`.** The marker is attached
    after the fold and before the filter (`foldAndMarkSales`), on the list, the
    export and the dashboard feed. The list sends it as `sale` without the
    product id. The CSV export's columns and cells do not change.
  - **`modules/transactions/activity.ts`, `search.ts`.** Sales group under their
    own `sale` key per day; a group names a product only when every member has
    the same recorded product id. Search matches the product label.
  - **`openapi/spec.ts`, `@haven_ai/core` `fallback-names.ts`.** The optional
    `sale` field on the row and the activity group; "Sale" and the "Sale · "
    prefix join Haven's own fallback words, so a receipt seller name equal to
    them is refused (`RECEIPT_MERCHANT_NAMES_SQL`), as "Deposit" already is.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected:
  - Nothing here holds a key, signs, moves funds, settles or authorises. The
    new read only reads `inbound_transfers` and `merchant_sales`, scoped by
    `user_id` and `account_id`, and never reads `match_kind`,
    `balance_consumed` or the receive balance.
  - A Stripe-held sale is never a Transactions row and never lends a feed row
    its record, even when it shares a transaction hash with one: the read
    selects the `account` arm only.
  - Own money is never shown as a sale: the recorded own flag excludes the
    owner's accounts and agents, and a live own-account sender or a folded move
    drops the marker even where the recorded flag disagrees.

  Mutation-tested (`prove-red`, each cell reddens the named test in
  `routes/__tests__/transactions-sale-marker.db.test.ts`):
  - The feed read without its `held_by = 'account'` restriction -> "a Stripe-held sale sharing a hash with a feed row never lends that row its record".
  - Own moves returned as sales -> "`own_move`: a payment from the owner's own agent (recorded own) is not a sale".
  - The USDC token check dropped -> "another token in the same transaction is never marked".
  - The live own-account sender check removed -> "own money stays own: a transfer from another own account is never a sale, folded or not, even if the recorded flag says otherwise".
  - The merchant setting ignored -> "with merchant services off (the account keeps is_merchant_account), no row is marked".
  - Every non-own inbound row keyed `deposit` again -> the demo's two-group test.
  - A group named by its first member's product whatever the others are -> "two sales of different products on one day are one group with a null product name".
  - The product label dropped from search -> "search matches what the row shows".
  Each cell was run with `scripts/ci/prove-red.mjs mutate` at `f2848d742`; three further cells pin the frontend title, the panel's kind line and the Sales link.

  Verdict: **Perimeter unchanged.** A display marker over the existing receive
  index and sale records; no key, no signing, no funds, no authority.
