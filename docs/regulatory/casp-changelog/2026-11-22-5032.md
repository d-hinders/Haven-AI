- **#5032**: a sale report still being resent when the paywall proxy stopped was
  lost — the buyer was served and the payment was `delivered`, but the merchant's
  Sales record was never written.

  What was wrong:
  - A record lived only in the reporter's in-memory `inFlight` map until delivery
    finished; the outbox was written only after the core reporter gave up.
  - `close()` stopped the outbox timer and closed the store without waiting for
    the reporter, and SIGTERM calls `close()` then exits. The window is not small:
    the reporter resends after 1, 4 and 16 s, each wait raised to the answer's
    `Retry-After` (capped at 60 s), and Haven answers 425
    `transaction_not_visible` until the transaction has three confirmations. The
    README's deploy procedure stops the old process before starting the new one.

  What changed:
  - **Queue-first.** `sales-outbox.ts` gains `queue(key, record)` — the record is
    written to `SALES_OUTBOX` *before* the first attempt — and `remove(key,
    reason)`, which deletes it by key when Haven accepts it or refuses it for
    good (a 4xx other than 429 and 425). `release(key, outcome)` marks a record
    whose first delivery round ended without an answer as waiting for a replay.
  - A record in its first delivery round is *held* from replay in memory only, so
    the reporter and the 60 s replay task never send the same record at once; a
    restart holds nothing, which is what makes the record replayable.
  - `sales.ts` queues before `reportSale`, removes on accept or permanent
    refusal, and releases on a 5xx/timeout/425/429. `stop()` makes a late answer
    write nothing: the record is already durable, and the next proxy replays it.
  - `main.ts`'s `close()` stops the reporter before the outbox and the store.
  - `/healthz` `outbox_depth` and the `sales_outbox_queued` log count only
    records past their first delivery round, so the documented meaning ("a
    growing outbox means Haven is not receiving the sales") still holds; the
    README and `docs/architecture/12-merchant-kit.md` §5 and §12 say so.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected: this changes *when* a record is written
  to a local file, not what is sent, by whom, or with what authority. The Sales
  key is still receive-only (#4896) and still reaches only the core reporter; the
  record is still a claim Haven checks against the chain (#4897), so a wrong or
  malicious record cannot create revenue, move a balance or reach another
  account. No key, signer, budget, allowance, settlement decision or payment path
  moves, and the buyer's answer still never waits on Haven. The outbox holds no
  secret and no buyer tax declaration.

  Mutation-tested (each cell reddens the named test):
  - Queueing after the first attempt instead of before -> `sales-proxy.test.ts`
    "delivers a sale whose report is between resends when the proxy stops, from
    the outbox of the next proxy".
  - Not removing an accepted record by key -> "removes an accepted record from
    the outbox, and a restarted proxy does not send it again".
  - Not removing a permanent refusal -> "removes a record Haven refuses for good,
    and a restarted proxy does not send it again".
  - Counting a first-round record in `outbox_depth` -> "counts only records past
    their first delivery round in /healthz outbox_depth".
  - Replaying a held record -> `sales-outbox.test.ts` "writes the record before
    the first attempt, and holds it from a replay until the first round ends".
  - Writing to the store or the outbox after `stop()` -> `sales.test.ts` "stops
    writing to the store and the outbox once the proxy is closing".

  Perimeter unchanged.
