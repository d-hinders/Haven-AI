- **#5033**: the merchant kit's receipt now states the amount that settled,
  and the kit's tests now see what the kit asks the facilitator to verify and
  settle.

  What was wrong:
  - `buildReceiptForPayment` priced the receipt line from the products file's
    current price (`packages/paywall-proxy/src/receipt.ts`). After a price
    change and a restart, a buyer's proof retry for an earlier settled call
    got a receipt, line price and VAT, for the new price, while
    PAYMENT-RESPONSE stated what settled. Haven's accounting already booked
    the settled amount and flagged the receipt; the merchant's own receipt
    was still wrong.
  - `FakeFacilitator.verify()` and `settle()` took no arguments, so no test
    could see the requirements the kit passed. Settling with
    `payTo: 0x…dEaD, amount: '1'` left all 297 kit tests green (the
    2026-11-21 quality scan's block 1 probe).

  What changed:
  - `buildReceiptForPayment` takes a required `settledAmountAtomic`, the
    consumed-payment record's `amount` (fixed at reservation from the price
    then). Every caller passes it: `receiptFor` in the handler (the proof
    retry and the chain-found path) and the `report-sale` rebuild. The type
    makes a caller pass an amount; that it is the settled one is pinned by the
    tests below for the handler's paths.
  - The fake records each call's `payload` and `requirements`, and the
    settlement test asserts the settled requirements' scheme, network,
    `payTo`, amount and asset against the configuration and the product, and
    that the settled payload is the buyer's own authorization.

  Why the CASP perimeter is unaffected: nothing here signs, holds a key,
  moves funds or changes what is settled. The settle call is unchanged; only
  the receipt document's price and the tests change.

  Pinned by tests: `receipt.test.ts` (a settled amount different from the
  products file's price), `receipt-proxy.test.ts` (price raised and the proxy
  restarted between the settlement and the proof retry), `proxy.test.ts`
  (the settled requirements).

  Mutation-tested: with the receipt priced from `params.product.price` again,
  both receipt tests fail; with the settle call given
  `payTo: 0x…dEaD, amount: '1'`, the settlement test fails.

  Not covered by a test: the `report-sale` rebuild's receipt price is not
  used (the sale record carries only the receipt's number and VAT treatment),
  so no test can see it, and passing the products file's price there would
  still typecheck. The chain-found path has no test of its own; it builds the
  receipt through the same `receiptFor` as the proof retry, which
  `receipt-proxy.test.ts` pins.
