- **#5091**: the demo merchant's sale reporter and its `/.well-known/x402`
  product list now decide "this product's sales appear on the Sales account"
  through one shared function instead of two copies. No behaviour changes: the
  same products report and the same products are listed. The merchant still
  sells only its own demo products, and no third-party funds are received or
  moved.

  What changed:
  - **`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/products.ts`** adds `salesAccountRoute`:
    a product's sales appear on the Sales account when its receiving address
    (read through `productReceivingAddress`, as the 402 quotes it) is the
    Sales account, or when it is the Stripe-recorded joke and Stripe recording
    is on.
  - **`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/sales.ts`** reports through that
    function; `SalesConfig` carries `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` for the read. The
    Stripe recorder's per-payment decision still gates the joke.
  - **`packages/demo-merchant-mcp/src/kit-discovery.ts`** and **`http.ts`**
    list through that function.

  Why the perimeter is unaffected:
  - No receiving address, price, payment requirement, settlement path or tool
    changes. `productReceivingAddress` is unchanged and is only read.
  - On today's catalogue the rule gives the same answer as before for every
    product and configuration: only Field Notes names the Sales account, and
    `resolveSalesConfig` still refuses a Sales address equal to
    `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` or a Stripe deposit address.
  - The discovery document stays a label check for Haven's catalogue reader;
    nothing pays from it.

  Tests:
  - `kit-discovery.test.ts`: the committed backend fixture still matches byte
    for byte with Stripe recording on; for the default catalogue and for a
    catalogue paid wholly into the Sales account, with Stripe on and off, the
    document lists exactly the products the reporter reports; a whole
    catalogue paid into the Sales account is listed in full; a product that
    pays `MERCHANT_ADDRESS` is neither listed nor reported.

  Perimeter unchanged.
