## If you cannot open a browser

Nothing here needs you to. Steps 1-3 — account, funding, budget — are links: hand your user the full `<host>/…` URL and ask them to say when it is done. Step 5 is a link only when your run reported one in `approval.url` — otherwise it is the tab they already have open. Then poll `haven_get_agent` until it reads `ready`. Do not route around the sign-in wall — it makes the account theirs, not yours.
