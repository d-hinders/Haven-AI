Discover and inspect your agent budgets on-chain, and get everything you need to revoke them — no Haven account, no Haven server.

DelegationManager:
Check or revoke a specific delegation

Paste a delegation object Haven gave you (or one found above). The page computes its delegation hash and reads its status from the DelegationManager. Revoking it is an operation your ACCOUNT signs (a UserOp calling disableDelegation) — the docs show the exact steps, or, with a wallet signer on the account, your account's own signers can always rotate keys / move funds as the universal backstop.

A delegation that was issued but never used is an off-chain object no one can discover until first use — that is by design. Your revocation guarantee does not depend on finding it: you can revoke any delegation you can reconstruct (paste it above), and, with a wallet signer on the account, your account's own signers can always rotate keys or move funds. The step-by-step manual path (Etherscan + the contract ABI) is in Haven's public docs and works without this page.

If your account's only signer is a passkey: a passkey is bound to the website it was created on — Haven's passkeys belong to Haven's site (WebAuthn), so a browser offers them only there. You can sign a revocation with a passkey only through Haven. To keep a signer that works without Haven, add a wallet to your account (Security & details on your account page); a wallet can sign the revocation from anywhere.
